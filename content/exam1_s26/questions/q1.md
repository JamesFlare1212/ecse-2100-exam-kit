## Question

### English

You are given a spool of coaxial cable of length $L=100\mathrm{m}$ and are tasked with determining its properties through measurements. A diagram of your measurement setup is shown below. Assume the coaxial cable can be treated as a low-loss transmission line.

### 中文

给你一卷长度为 $L=100\mathrm{m}$ 的同轴电缆，要求通过测量确定其性质。测量装置如下图所示。假设可将同轴电缆视为低损耗传输线。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem works backward from measurements to the propagation, matching and loss parameters of a transmission line.

#### 中文

本题是从实验读数反推传输线参数：传播、匹配和衰减需要依次联系起来。

### Givens

#### English

The cable is $100\,\mathrm m$ long with measured delay $392\,\mathrm{ns}$. The circuit has $R_g=25\,\Omega$ and $R_L=50\,\Omega$, and the input-voltage magnitude is independent of frequency. Later parts supply frequency and endpoint amplitudes.

#### 中文

电缆长 $100\,\mathrm m$，测得延迟 $392\,\mathrm{ns}$；图中 $R_g=25\,\Omega$、$R_L=50\,\Omega$，输入电压幅值不随频率变化。后续给出频率及输入、输出幅值。

### Goal

#### English

Find velocity factor, $Z_0$, wavelength and distributed inductance and capacitance; then infer $\alpha$ and resistance per unit length from attenuation.

#### 中文

求速度因子、$Z_0$、波长、单位长度电感与电容，再由幅值衰减求 $\alpha$ 和单位长度电阻。

### Approach

#### English

Find speed from length and delay, then interpret the frequency-independent input measurement to identify matching. Handle loss afterward, keeping voltage-amplitude attenuation distinct from power attenuation.

#### 中文

先由长度和延迟确定波速，再利用频率无关的输入测量判断匹配。最后处理损耗，注意电压幅值的衰减指数与功率的衰减指数不同。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

You measure the time delay of your cable to be $T_d=392\mathrm{ns}$. What is the velocity factor of the coaxial cable?

#### 中文

测得电缆的时间延迟为 $T_d=392\mathrm{ns}$。这根同轴电缆的速度因子是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Use Lecture 04’s [transit-time relation](formula:bounce) and the crib sheet’s [velocity-factor definition](formula:velocity):

#### 中文

使用 Lecture 04 的[传播时间关系](formula:bounce)和 crib sheet 的[速度因子定义](formula:velocity)：

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac L{T_d},\qquad \mathrm{vf}=\frac u{u_c}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $L=100\,\mathrm m$ and $T_d=392\,\mathrm{ns}$, find the velocity factor. Substitute the distance and delay:

#### 中文

已知 $L=100\,\mathrm m$、$T_d=392\,\mathrm{ns}$，求速度因子。先把距离与时延代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle L=100\,\mathrm m,\qquad T_d=392\,\mathrm{ns}=392\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]&\displaystyle u=\frac{100}{392\times10^{-9}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Dividing by the vacuum speed $3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ gives

#### 中文

再除以真空光速 $3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$，得

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{vf}=0.85034\approx0.85
$$

### Answer

$$
\mathrm{vf}=0.85034\approx0.85
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

You measure the amplitude of the voltage at the input to the coaxial cable $V_{in}$ to be $1\mathrm{V}$ for any frequency you set for $V_g$. What is the characteristic impedance $Z_0$ of the cable?

#### 中文

不论将 $V_g$ 设为何种频率，测得同轴电缆输入电压 $V_{in}$ 的幅值均为 $1\mathrm{V}$。这根电缆的特性阻抗 $Z_0$ 是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [reflection](formula:reflection) and [input-impedance formula](formula:zin)s from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[反射系数](formula:reflection)与[输入阻抗公式](formula:zin)：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0},\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta L)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta L)}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given a frequency-independent $|V_{\mathrm{in}}|=1\,\mathrm V$ and $R_L=50\,\Omega$, find $Z_0$. Substitute the input impedance into the source divider and apply the matched-load inference for the stated low-loss model:

#### 中文

已知扫频时 $|V_{\mathrm{in}}|=1\,\mathrm V$ 不变、$R_L=50\,\Omega$，求 $Z_0$。把输入阻抗代入源端分压，按题设低损耗模型采用匹配推断：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V_{\mathrm{in}}=V_g\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in}}}{R_g+Z_{\mathrm{in}}},\qquad \beta=\frac{2\pi f}{u}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_L\approx0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_L-Z_0\approx0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

From $Z_L-Z_0\approx0$, this gives

#### 中文

由 $Z_L-Z_0\approx0$，得

#### Equation

$$
Z_0\approx50\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0\approx50\,\Omega
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the wavelength $\lambda$ of the voltage wave on the coaxial cable if the frequency of the generator is $f=100\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

#### 中文

若发生器的频率为 $f=100\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$，计算同轴电缆上电压波的波长 $\lambda$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [wavelength formula](formula:wave) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[波长公式](formula:wave)：

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac uf
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}$, find $\lambda$. Substitute the unrounded speed from (a):

#### 中文

已知 $f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}$，求 $\lambda$。把 (a) 未舍入的波速代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle u=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{100\times10^6\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=2.5510\,\mathrm m\approx2.55\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
\lambda=2.5510\,\mathrm m\approx2.55\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the per-unit-inductance $l$ and per-unit-capacitance $c$ of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

计算同轴电缆的单位长度电感 $l$ 和单位长度电容 $c$。

### Step 1

#### English

In the low-loss approximation, use the [characteristic-impedance](formula:impedance) and [speed formulas](formula:velocity) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

在低损耗近似下，使用 crib sheet 的[特性阻抗](formula:impedance)和[波速公式](formula:velocity)：

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc},\qquad u=\frac1{\sqrt{lc}}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Using $u$ and $Z_0$ from (a) and (b), find $l$ and $c$. Combine the two relations:

#### 中文

已知 (a)、(b) 得到 $u$ 和 $Z_0$，求 $l$、$c$。联立两式：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{Z_0}{u}&=\sqrt{l/c}\sqrt{lc}=l,\\Z_0u&=\frac{\sqrt{l/c}}{\sqrt{lc}}=\frac1c\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $u=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$:

#### 中文

把 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$u=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}$ 代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle l=\frac{Z_0}{u}=\frac{50}{2.5510204\times10^8}=1.9600\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle c=\frac1{Z_0u}=\frac1{50(2.5510204\times10^8)}=7.8400\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Converting units gives

#### 中文

换算单位，得

#### Equation

$$
l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH/m},\qquad c=78.40\,\mathrm{pF/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH/m},\qquad c=78.40\,\mathrm{pF/m}
$$

### Source correction

#### English

Using the unrounded $392\,\mathrm{ns}$ gives $l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$. The official $196.1\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$ uses $\mathrm{vf}$ rounded to 0.85.

#### 中文

使用未舍入的 $392\,\mathrm{ns}$ 得 $l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$；官方先取 $\mathrm{vf}=0.85$ 得 $196.1\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$，属于舍入差异。

## Part e

### Question

#### English

If you measure the amplitude of the voltage at the load to be $V(L=100\mathrm{m})=0.904\mathrm{V}$ and the amplitude of the voltage at the input to be $V(L=0\mathrm{m})=1\mathrm{V}$, determine the attenuation coefficient $\alpha$.

#### 中文

若测得负载电压幅值为 $V(L=100\mathrm{m})=0.904\mathrm{V}$，输入电压幅值为 $V(L=0\mathrm{m})=1\mathrm{V}$，求衰减系数 $\alpha$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the amplitude envelope of the [lossy wave](formula:lossy-wave) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet [有损行波式](formula:lossy-wave)的幅度包络：

#### Equation

$$
|V(L)|=|V(0)|e^{-\alpha L}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $|V(0)|=1\,\mathrm V$, $|V(L)|=0.904\,\mathrm V$ and $L=100\,\mathrm m$, find $\alpha$. Substitute and take natural logarithms:

#### 中文

已知 $|V(0)|=1\,\mathrm V$、$|V(L)|=0.904\,\mathrm V$、$L=100\,\mathrm m$，求 $\alpha$。代入后取自然对数：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}0.904&=e^{-\alpha(100\,\mathrm m)},\\\ln(0.904)&=-\alpha(100\,\mathrm m),\\\alpha&=-\frac{\ln(0.904)}{100\,\mathrm m}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=1.00926\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
\alpha=1.00926\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}
$$

## Part f

### Question

#### English

Calculate the per-unit-length resistance $r$ of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

计算同轴电缆的单位长度电阻 $r$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [low-loss attenuation formula](formula:attenuation) from the crib sheet, neglecting shunt leakage:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[低损耗衰减式](formula:attenuation)，忽略并联漏电导：

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=\frac r{2Z_0}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Using $Z_0$ and $\alpha$ from (b) and (e), find the resistance per unit length $r$. Rearrange and substitute:

#### 中文

已知 (b)、(e) 的 $Z_0$ 和 $\alpha$，求单位长度电阻 $r$。解出 $r$ 并代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle r=2Z_0\alpha\\[4pt]&\displaystyle r=2(50\,\Omega)(1.0092592\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
r=0.10093\,\Omega/\mathrm m\approx0.10\,\Omega/\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
r=0.10093\,\Omega/\mathrm m\approx0.10\,\Omega/\mathrm m
$$
