## Question

### English

You are given a spool of coaxial cable of length $L=100\mathrm{m}$ and are tasked with determining its properties through measurements. A diagram of your measurement setup is shown below. Assume the coaxial cable can be treated as a low-loss transmission line.

### 中文

给你一卷长度为 $L=100\mathrm{m}$ 的同轴电缆，要求通过测量确定其性质。测量装置如下图所示。假设可将同轴电缆视为低损耗传输线。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem works backward from measurements to the propagation, matching and loss parameters of a transmission line.

#### 中文

本题是从实验读数反推传输线参数：传播、匹配和衰减需要依次联系起来。

### Givens

#### English

The cable is $100\,\mathrm m$ long with measured delay $392\,\mathrm{ns}$. The circuit has $R_g=25\,\Omega$ and $R_L=50\,\Omega$, and the input-voltage magnitude is independent of frequency. Later parts supply frequency and endpoint amplitudes.

#### 中文

电缆长 $100\,\mathrm m$，测得延迟 $392\,\mathrm{ns}$；图中 $R_g=25\,\Omega$、$R_L=50\,\Omega$，输入电压幅值不随频率变化。后续给出频率及输入、输出幅值。

### Goal

#### English

Find velocity factor, $Z_0$, wavelength and distributed inductance and capacitance; then infer $\alpha$ and resistance per unit length from attenuation.

#### 中文

求速度因子、$Z_0$、波长、单位长度电感与电容，再由幅值衰减求 $\alpha$ 和单位长度电阻。

### Approach

#### English

Find speed from length and delay, then interpret the frequency-independent input measurement to identify matching. Handle loss afterward, keeping voltage-amplitude attenuation distinct from power attenuation.

#### 中文

先由长度和延迟确定波速，再利用频率无关的输入测量判断匹配。最后处理损耗，注意电压幅值的衰减指数与功率的衰减指数不同。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

You measure the time delay of your cable to be $T_d=392\mathrm{ns}$. What is the velocity factor of the coaxial cable?

#### 中文

测得电缆的时间延迟为 $T_d=392\mathrm{ns}$。这根同轴电缆的速度因子是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the distance, one-way time and speed relation, then the velocity-factor definition.

#### 中文

先写传播距离、单程时间与波速的关系，再用速度因子的定义。

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac L{T_d},\qquad \mathrm{vf}=\frac u{u_c}
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)
- [velocity](formula:velocity)

### Step 2

#### English

Express length and measured delay in meters and seconds.

#### 中文

将长度和测得时延统一为米与秒。

#### Equation

$$
L=100\,\mathrm m,\qquad T_d=392\,\mathrm{ns}=392\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm s
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute distance divided by time to compute the propagation speed first.

#### 中文

代入距离除以时间，先求实际传播速度。

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac{100}{392\times10^{-9}}\,\mathrm{m/s}=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Divide by vacuum light speed to obtain a dimensionless factor.

#### 中文

再除以真空光速，速度因子没有单位。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{vf}=\frac{2.5510204\times10^8}{3.00\times10^8}=0.85034\approx0.85
$$

### Answer

$$
\mathrm{vf}=0.85034\approx0.85
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

You measure the amplitude of the voltage at the input to the coaxial cable $V_{in}$ to be $1\mathrm{V}$ for any frequency you set for $V_g$. What is the characteristic impedance $Z_0$ of the cable?

#### 中文

不论将 $V_g$ 设为何种频率，测得同轴电缆输入电压 $V_{in}$ 的幅值均为 $1\mathrm{V}$。这根电缆的特性阻抗 $Z_0$ 是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the reflection and input-impedance equations to identify frequency-dependent standing-wave behavior.

#### 中文

先写反射系数与输入阻抗原式，识别随频率变化的驻波来源。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0},\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta L)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta L)}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)
- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Sweeping frequency changes $\beta L$. With load mismatch, input impedance and hence the source divider generally vary with frequency.

#### 中文

扫频改变 $\beta L$；若有明显负载失配，输入阻抗通常随频率改变，源端分压也会改变。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\mathrm{in}}=V_g\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in}}}{R_g+Z_{\mathrm{in}}},\qquad \beta=\frac{2\pi f}{u}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

For the stated fixed-source, low-loss model, follow the official inference that constant input amplitude indicates $\Gamma_L\approx0$.

#### 中文

按题设固定源和低损耗近似，输入幅度不随扫频变化，采用官方的匹配推断 $\Gamma_L\approx0$。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L\approx0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_L-Z_0\approx0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The $50\,\Omega$ load therefore implies approximately the same characteristic impedance.

#### 中文

负载是 $50\,\Omega$，所以推断出的特性阻抗也是约 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0\approx50\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0\approx50\,\Omega
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the wavelength $\lambda$ of the voltage wave on the coaxial cable if the frequency of the generator is $f=100\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

#### 中文

若发生器的频率为 $f=100\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$，计算同轴电缆上电压波的波长 $\lambda$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib wavelength–speed relation.

#### 中文

先写公式表的波长与波速关系。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac uf
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 2

#### English

Use the unrounded speed measured in a) and convert frequency to $\mathrm{Hz}$.

#### 中文

使用 a) 从时延测出的未舍入波速，并把频率换成 $\mathrm{Hz}$。

#### Equation

$$
u=2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s},\qquad f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the values and cancel the time units.

#### 中文

代入数值，$\mathrm s^{-1}$ 与 $\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}$ 中的时间单位约去。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac{2.5510204\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{100\times10^6\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Retain at least the two significant digits requested.

#### 中文

按题目要求保留至少两位有效数字。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=2.5510\,\mathrm m\approx2.55\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
\lambda=2.5510\,\mathrm m\approx2.55\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the per-unit-inductance $l$ and per-unit-capacitance $c$ of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

计算同轴电缆的单位长度电感 $l$ 和单位长度电容 $c$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with both crib equations, using the lossless relations for $Z_{0}$ and speed in the low-loss approximation.

#### 中文

先写需要联立的两条原表公式；低损耗近似下使用无损线的 $Z_{0}$ 与波速关系。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc},\qquad u=\frac1{\sqrt{lc}}
$$

#### References

- [impedance](formula:impedance)
- [velocity](formula:velocity)

### Step 2

#### English

First form $Z_0/u$. Division by the reciprocal becomes multiplication. Combine the two positive square roots, cancel capacitance and obtain $l^2$. Since inductance is positive, its positive square root is $l$.

#### 中文

先求 $Z_0/u$。分母的倒数变成乘法；两个正平方根相乘可合到同一个根号内，约去电容后得到 $l^2$。电感为正，所以正平方根为 $l$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{Z_0}{u}&=\frac{\sqrt{l/c}}{1/\sqrt{lc}}\\&=\sqrt{l/c}\sqrt{lc}\\&=\sqrt{(l/c)(lc)}=\sqrt{l^2}=l\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Next form $Z_0u$. Combine the square-root ratio, cancel inductance and take the positive square root of $1/c^2$. Finally invert to isolate $c$.

#### 中文

再求 $Z_0u$。根号相除后先约去电感，再对 $1/c^2$ 开正平方根。最后取倒数，把 $c$ 单独留在左侧。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_0u&=\frac{\sqrt{l/c}}{\sqrt{lc}}\\&=\sqrt{\frac{l/c}{lc}}=\sqrt{\frac1{c^2}}=\frac1c\\c&=\frac1{Z_0u}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute to obtain inductance per unit length, retaining $\mathrm{H}/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

先代入求每单位长度电感，保留 $\mathrm{H}/\mathrm{m}$ 单位。

#### Equation

$$
l=\frac{Z_0}{u}=\frac{50}{2.5510204\times10^8}=1.9600\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Substitute separately for capacitance, retaining $Z_{0}$ in the denominator.

#### 中文

再独立代入求电容，不要把分母中的 $Z_{0}$ 漏掉。

#### Equation

$$
c=\frac1{Z_0u}=\frac1{50(2.5510204\times10^8)}=7.8400\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Convert $\mathrm H$ and $\mathrm F$ to $\mathrm{nH}$ and $\mathrm{pF}$. The small difference from the official inductance comes from its intermediate rounding.

#### 中文

把 $\mathrm H$、$\mathrm F$ 分别换为 $\mathrm{nH}$、$\mathrm{pF}$；此处沿用未舍入时延，原解的微小差别来自中间舍入。

#### Equation

$$
l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH/m},\qquad c=78.40\,\mathrm{pF/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH/m},\qquad c=78.40\,\mathrm{pF/m}
$$

### Source correction

#### English

Using the unrounded $392\,\mathrm{ns}$ gives $l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$. The official $196.1\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$ uses $\mathrm{vf}$ rounded to 0.85.

#### 中文

使用未舍入的 $392\,\mathrm{ns}$ 得 $l=196.0\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$；官方先取 $\mathrm{vf}=0.85$ 得 $196.1\,\mathrm{nH}/\mathrm{m}$，属于舍入差异。

## Part e

### Question

#### English

If you measure the amplitude of the voltage at the load to be $V(L=100\mathrm{m})=0.904\mathrm{V}$ and the amplitude of the voltage at the input to be $V(L=0\mathrm{m})=1\mathrm{V}$, determine the attenuation coefficient $\alpha$.

#### 中文

若测得负载电压幅值为 $V(L=100\mathrm{m})=0.904\mathrm{V}$，输入电压幅值为 $V(L=0\mathrm{m})=1\mathrm{V}$，求衰减系数 $\alpha$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the lossy-wave envelope; with a matched load, the amplitude ratio gives one-way exponential attenuation.

#### 中文

先写有损行波包络。负载匹配时幅度之比直接给出单程指数衰减。

#### Equation

$$
|V(L)|=|V(0)|e^{-\alpha L}
$$

#### References

- [lossy-wave](formula:lossy-wave)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute the measured amplitudes and length, forming a dimensionless ratio first.

#### 中文

把两个测得幅度及线长代入，先形成无量纲比值。

#### Equation

$$
\frac{0.904\,\mathrm V}{1.000\,\mathrm V}=e^{-\alpha(100\,\mathrm m)}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Take natural logarithms of both sides. The natural logarithm and exponential are inverse functions, so the entire exponent remains, including its minus sign and line length.

#### 中文

对等式两边取自然对数。因为自然对数与指数函数互为反函数，右边会留下完整指数，包含负号和线长。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\ln(0.904)&=\ln\!\left(e^{-\alpha(100\,\mathrm m)}\right)\\\ln(e^x)&=x\\\ln(0.904)&=-\alpha(100\,\mathrm m)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Divide by the negative length coefficient to obtain a positive attenuation constant.

#### 中文

再除以负的长度系数，得到正衰减常数。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=-\frac{\ln(0.904)}{100\,\mathrm m}=\frac{0.1009259186}{100\,\mathrm m}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

This amplitude attenuation is in $\mathrm{Np}/\mathrm{m}$, rather than a power coefficient or $\mathrm{dB}/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

幅度的指数衰减用 $\mathrm{Np}/\mathrm{m}$ 表示，不是功率衰减或 $\mathrm{dB}/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=1.00926\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
\alpha=1.00926\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}
$$

## Part f

### Question

#### English

Calculate the per-unit-length resistance $r$ of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

计算同轴电缆的单位长度电阻 $r$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib low-loss attenuation equation, neglecting shunt conductance $g$ in this model.

#### 中文

先写原表低损耗衰减公式。按该题模型忽略并联漏电导 $g$。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=\frac r{2Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [attenuation](formula:attenuation)

### Step 2

#### English

Multiply both sides by $2Z_{0}$ to isolate series resistance per length.

#### 中文

两边乘 $2Z_{0}$，先解出未知串联电阻密度。

#### Equation

$$
r=2Z_0\alpha
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Insert the unrounded attenuation and the $50\,\Omega$ characteristic impedance from b).

#### 中文

代入未舍入的衰减系数和 b) 的 $50\,\Omega$ 特性阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
r=2(50\,\Omega)(1.0092592\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}})
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Simplify and check the units are $\Omega/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

化简并检查单位是 $\Omega/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
r=0.10093\,\Omega/\mathrm m\approx0.10\,\Omega/\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
r=0.10093\,\Omega/\mathrm m\approx0.10\,\Omega/\mathrm m
$$
