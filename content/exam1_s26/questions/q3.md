## Question

### English

For all circuits in this problem, the frequency of the generator voltage is $f=200\mathrm{MHz}=200\times10^6\mathrm{Hz}$ and the velocity factor of all coaxial cables is $0.65$. In each part, provide a numerical answer with units.

### 中文

本题所有电路中，发生器电压频率均为 $f=200\mathrm{MHz}=200\times10^6\mathrm{Hz}$，所有同轴电缆的速度因子均为 $0.65$。每问都应给出带单位的数值答案。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

These circuits test impedance transformation, matching and the filtering action of an open-circuit stub.

#### 中文

本题通过不同电路考查传输线的阻抗变换、匹配和开路支节的滤波作用。

### Givens

#### English

All circuits operate at $200\,\mathrm{MHz}$ with velocity factor $0.65$. Part a offers $25\,\Omega$, $50\,\Omega$ and $75\,\Omega$ cables; each diagram supplies its connections and terminations.

#### 中文

所有电路均有 $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$、速度因子 $0.65$；第 a) 问可选 $25\,\Omega$、$50\,\Omega$、$75\,\Omega$ 电缆，各电路的连接与终端见原图。

### Goal

#### English

Select cable type and physical length for matching, calculate the specified input impedance, and choose a stub length that produces total reflection.

#### 中文

选出匹配所需的电缆类型和实际长度，计算指定输入阻抗，并确定使主线发生全反射的支节长度。

### Approach

#### English

Relate electrical length to physical length, then recognize half-wave and quarter-wave properties. For a parallel stub, use admittance and identify whether the junction effectively sees an open or a short.

#### 中文

先把电长度与米数联系起来，再识别半波、四分之一波等特殊长度。遇到并联支节时改用导纳，先判断接点实际呈现开路还是短路。

## Part a(i)

### Question

#### English

For part a) only: you have access to spools of coaxial cable with characteristic impedances:
• Cable type #1: $Z_{01}=25\Omega$
• Cable type #2: $Z_{02}=50\Omega$
• Cable type #3: $Z_{03}=75\Omega$
In each of the circuits below, specify the type of cable (which characteristic impedance $Z_0$) and length $L$ that match the generator impedance to the load impedance.

Circuit #1

#### 中文

仅对第 a) 问：可以使用下列特性阻抗的同轴电缆卷：
• 电缆类型 #1：$Z_{01}=25\Omega$
• 电缆类型 #2：$Z_{02}=50\Omega$
• 电缆类型 #3：$Z_{03}=75\Omega$
对下列每个电路，指定能将发生器阻抗匹配到负载阻抗的电缆类型（即特性阻抗 $Z_0$）和长度 $L$。

电路 #1

### Step 1

#### English

Use the half-wave impedance repetition from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的半波阻抗重复性质：

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L
$$

#### References

- [half](formula:half)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $R_g=R_L=100\,\Omega$, velocity factor $0.65$ and $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$, find the cable type and length. Evaluate the wavelength and choose a half-wave line:

#### 中文

已知 $R_g=R_L=100\,\Omega$、速度因子 $0.65$、$f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$，求电缆类型和长度。把速度与频率代入波长式，再取半波长：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=100\,\Omega=R_g,\qquad Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{\mathrm{vf}\,u_c}{f}=\frac{0.65(3\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})}{200\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}}=0.975\,\mathrm m\\[4pt]&\displaystyle L=\frac{0.975\,\mathrm m}{2}=0.4875\,\mathrm m\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [velocity](formula:velocity)

### Step 3

#### English

All three cable types repeat the $100\,\Omega$ load, giving

#### 中文

三种电缆均重复 $100\,\Omega$ 负载，故得

#### Equation

$$
Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.4875\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.4875\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Determine $Z_{in}$ for the circuit below.

#### 中文

求下列电路的 $Z_{in}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the capacitor impedance and the quarter-wave transformation from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用电容阻抗关系和 crib sheet 的四分之一波长变换式：

#### Equation

$$
Z_C=\frac1{j\omega C},\qquad Z_L=R+Z_C,\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$, $C=1\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $R=Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$. Evaluate the series load and multiply by the denominator’s conjugate:

#### 中文

已知 $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$、$C=1\,\mu\mathrm F$、$R=Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$。先代入串联负载，再乘分母共轭：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \omega=2\pi(200\times10^6)\,\mathrm{rad/s},\qquad C=10^{-6}\,\mathrm F\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_C=-\frac j{2\pi(200\times10^6)(10^{-6})}=-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega,\qquad Z_L=50-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in}}&=\frac{2500}{50-jX_C}\\&=\frac{2500(50+jX_C)}{50^2+X_C^2},\quad X_C=7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}\approx(50+j7.95775\times10^{-4})\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}\approx(50+j7.95775\times10^{-4})\,\Omega
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

In the circuit below, what length of open-circuited cable $\ell$ would lead to full reflection of the voltage wave at $z=A$?

#### 中文

下图电路中，开路电缆长度 $\ell$ 为多少时，会使电压波在 $z=A$ 处全反射？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the open-line input impedance from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的开路线输入阻抗：

#### Equation

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell)
$$

#### References

- [open](formula:open)

### Step 2

#### English

An open stub is in parallel with a finite load. Find its shortest length for total reflection at A. Set $Z_s=0$ and substitute into the parallel impedance and reflection coefficient:

#### 中文

已知开路支节与有限负载并联，求 A 点全反射时的最短支节长度。令 $Z_s=0$，代入并联阻抗与反射系数：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \beta\ell=\frac\pi2,\qquad\ell=\frac{\pi/2}{2\pi/\lambda}=\frac\lambda4\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_A=\frac{Z_sZ_L}{Z_s+Z_L}=\frac{0\cdot Z_L}{0+Z_L}=0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_A=\frac{Z_A-Z_0}{Z_A+Z_0}=\frac{-Z_0}{Z_0}=-1\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 3

#### English

Substituting $\lambda=0.975\,\mathrm m$ gives

#### 中文

把 $\lambda=0.975\,\mathrm m$ 代入，得

#### Equation

$$
\ell=\frac\lambda4=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad|\Gamma_A|=1
$$

### Answer

$$
\ell=\frac\lambda4=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad|\Gamma_A|=1
$$
