## Question

### English

For all circuits in this problem, the frequency of the generator voltage is $f=200\mathrm{MHz}=200\times10^6\mathrm{Hz}$ and the velocity factor of all coaxial cables is $0.65$. In each part, provide a numerical answer with units.

### 中文

本题所有电路中，发生器电压频率均为 $f=200\mathrm{MHz}=200\times10^6\mathrm{Hz}$，所有同轴电缆的速度因子均为 $0.65$。每问都应给出带单位的数值答案。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

These circuits test impedance transformation, matching and the filtering action of an open-circuit stub.

#### 中文

本题通过不同电路考查传输线的阻抗变换、匹配和开路支节的滤波作用。

### Givens

#### English

All circuits operate at $200\,\mathrm{MHz}$ with velocity factor $0.65$. Part a offers $25\,\Omega$, $50\,\Omega$ and $75\,\Omega$ cables; each diagram supplies its connections and terminations.

#### 中文

所有电路均有 $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$、速度因子 $0.65$；第 a) 问可选 $25\,\Omega$、$50\,\Omega$、$75\,\Omega$ 电缆，各电路的连接与终端见原图。

### Goal

#### English

Select cable type and physical length for matching, calculate the specified input impedance, and choose a stub length that produces total reflection.

#### 中文

选出匹配所需的电缆类型和实际长度，计算指定输入阻抗，并确定使主线发生全反射的支节长度。

### Approach

#### English

Relate electrical length to physical length, then recognize half-wave and quarter-wave properties. For a parallel stub, use admittance and identify whether the junction effectively sees an open or a short.

#### 中文

先把电长度与米数联系起来，再识别半波、四分之一波等特殊长度。遇到并联支节时改用导纳，先判断接点实际呈现开路还是短路。

## Part a(i)

### Question

#### English

For part a) only: you have access to spools of coaxial cable with characteristic impedances:
• Cable type #1: $Z_{01}=25\Omega$
• Cable type #2: $Z_{02}=50\Omega$
• Cable type #3: $Z_{03}=75\Omega$
In each of the circuits below, specify the type of cable (which characteristic impedance $Z_0$) and length $L$ that match the generator impedance to the load impedance.

Circuit #1

#### 中文

仅对第 a) 问：可以使用下列特性阻抗的同轴电缆卷：
• 电缆类型 #1：$Z_{01}=25\Omega$
• 电缆类型 #2：$Z_{02}=50\Omega$
• 电缆类型 #3：$Z_{03}=75\Omega$
对下列每个电路，指定能将发生器阻抗匹配到负载阻抗的电缆类型（即特性阻抗 $Z_0$）和长度 $L$。

电路 #1

### Step 1

#### English

Both terminations are $100\,\Omega$, so start with the crib half-wave impedance-repeat property.

#### 中文

此问两端都是 $100\,\Omega$，优先使用原表的半波阻抗重复性质。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L
$$

#### References

- [half](formula:half)

### Step 2

#### English

A half-wave line of 25, 50 or $75\,\Omega$ repeats the $100\,\Omega$ load as a $100\,\Omega$ input, matching the source.

#### 中文

无论选 25、50 还是 $75\,\Omega$ 电缆，半波线都将 $100\,\Omega$ 负载重复为 $100\,\Omega$ 输入，等于源电阻。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=100\,\Omega=R_g,\qquad Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega
$$

### Step 3

#### English

First calculate the cable wavelength from velocity factor and frequency.

#### 中文

先由速度因子和频率计算电缆内波长。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac{\mathrm{vf}\,u_c}{f}=\frac{0.65(3\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})}{200\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}}=0.975\,\mathrm m
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [velocity](formula:velocity)

### Step 4

#### English

One positive half-wavelength length gives the required match.

#### 中文

取一个半波的正长度即可完成所需匹配。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{0.975\,\mathrm m}{2}=0.4875\,\mathrm m
$$

### Step 5

#### English

All three available cables therefore work with the same length.

#### 中文

因此三种给定电缆都可使用，长度相同。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.4875\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.4875\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part a(ii)

### Question

#### English

Circuit #2

#### 中文

电路 #2

### Step 1

#### English

Use a quarter-wave transformer between unequal real resistances. Begin with impedance inversion and set the input equal to source resistance.

#### 中文

不同的纯电阻之间使用四分之一波长变换。先写原表阻抗反演式并设输入等于源电阻。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{R_L}=R_g
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Solve for the cable characteristic impedance and substitute $50\,\Omega$ and $12.5\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

先解出待选电缆的特性阻抗，代入 $50\,\Omega$ 与 $12.5\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{R_gR_L}=\sqrt{(50\,\Omega)(12.5\,\Omega)}=\sqrt{625}\,\Omega=25\,\Omega
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Among the available cables, $25\,\Omega$ is type #1. Next calculate wavelength using the common velocity factor.

#### 中文

三种给定电缆中，$25\,\Omega$ 对应类型 #1。再由统一的速度因子求波长。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac{0.65(3\times10^8)}{200\times10^6}=0.975\,\mathrm m
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 4

#### English

Choose a quarter wavelength and verify the transformed resistance is $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

取四分之一波长，并核对反演后确实为 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{0.975}{4}=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad \frac{25^2}{12.5}=50\,\Omega
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The required cable and length are:

#### 中文

所需类型与长度如下。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=25\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.24375\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0=25\,\Omega,\qquad L=0.24375\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Determine $Z_{in}$ for the circuit below.

#### 中文

求下列电路的 $Z_{in}$。

### Step 1

#### English

First combine the series resistor and capacitor as the load, then use the quarter-wave property.

#### 中文

先把串联电阻与电容合并为负载阻抗，再用四分之一波长性质变换。

#### Equation

$$
Z_C=\frac1{j\omega C},\qquad Z_L=R+Z_C,\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Calculate angular frequency and express $1\,\mu\mathrm F$ as $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{F}$.

#### 中文

由频率求角频率，并把 $1\,\mu\mathrm F$ 写为 $10^{-6}\,\mathrm{F}$。

#### Equation

$$
\omega=2\pi(200\times10^6)\,\mathrm{rad/s},\qquad C=10^{-6}\,\mathrm F
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

Use $1/j=-j$ for the capacitor impedance and add the $50\,\Omega$ resistor.

#### 中文

利用 $1/j=-j$ 求电容阻抗，并与 $50\,\Omega$ 相加。

#### Equation

$$
Z_C=-\frac j{2\pi(200\times10^6)(10^{-6})}=-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega,\qquad Z_L=50-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute into the crib inversion and multiply by the denominator conjugate to show the imaginary-sign reversal.

#### 中文

代入原表反演式，再乘分母共轭数，显示虚部符号为何反转。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in}}&=\frac{2500}{50-jX_C}\\&=\frac{2500(50+jX_C)}{50^2+X_C^2},\quad X_C=7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Since $X_c^{2}$ is negligible relative to $50^{2}$, the real part is approximately $50\,\Omega$ with a small positive reactance.

#### 中文

$X_c^{2}$ 远小于 $50^{2}$，因此实部约为 $50\,\Omega$，虚部为小的正电抗。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}\approx(50+j7.95775\times10^{-4})\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}\approx(50+j7.95775\times10^{-4})\,\Omega
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

In the circuit below, what length of open-circuited cable $\ell$ would lead to full reflection of the voltage wave at $z=A$?

#### 中文

下图电路中，开路电缆长度 $\ell$ 为多少时，会使电压波在 $z=A$ 处全反射？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib open-stub impedance. To obtain total reflection at a shunt node with a resistive load contribution, make the stub input a short.

#### 中文

先写原表的开路支节输入阻抗。要使有电阻负载的并联节点全反射，可把支节输入变成短路。

#### Equation

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell)
$$

#### References

- [open](formula:open)

### Step 2

#### English

Set the stub input to zero, requiring $\cot(\beta\ell)=0$; choose the shortest positive electrical length $\pi/2$.

#### 中文

令支节输入阻抗为零，即 $\cot(\beta\ell)=0$，选择最短正电长度 $\pi/2$。

#### Equation

$$
\beta\ell=\frac\pi2,\qquad\ell=\frac{\pi/2}{2\pi/\lambda}=\frac\lambda4
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

A shorted shunt branch makes the total impedance at $A$ zero for any finite parallel load.

#### 中文

短路支节并联任何有限负载都会把节点 $\mathrm{A}$ 的总阻抗压为零。

#### Equation

$$
Z_A=\frac{Z_sZ_L}{Z_s+Z_L}=\frac{0\cdot Z_L}{0+Z_L}=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute the node impedance into the reflection equation; voltage reverses sign and the magnitude is one.

#### 中文

将节点阻抗代入反射系数原式；电压反相，模为 1。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_A=\frac{Z_A-Z_0}{Z_A+Z_0}=\frac{-Z_0}{Z_0}=-1
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 5

#### English

The common velocity factor and frequency give $\lambda=0.975\,\mathrm{m}$; take one quarter of it.

#### 中文

由共同速度因子和频率求得 $\lambda=0.975\,\mathrm{m}$，再取四分之一波长。

#### Equation

$$
\ell=\frac\lambda4=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad|\Gamma_A|=1
$$

### Answer

$$
\ell=\frac\lambda4=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad|\Gamma_A|=1
$$
