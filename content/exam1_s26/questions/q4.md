## Question

### English

Use the included Smith chart to match the feedline’s characteristic impedance $Z_0$ to the load impedance $Z_L$ via the single-stub matching method. As shown below, the matching circuit consists of a through-line of length $d$, which is connected to the load and an open-circuited stub of length $\ell$, connected in parallel with the through-line. $Z_0=50\Omega$ for all transmission lines in this problem.

### 中文

使用所附 Smith 图，通过单支节匹配法将馈线的特性阻抗 $Z_0$ 与负载阻抗 $Z_L$ 匹配。如下图所示，匹配电路包含一段连接负载、长度为 $d$ 的直通线，以及一段与直通线并联、长度为 $\ell$ 的开路支节。本题所有传输线均有 $Z_0=50\Omega$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem constructs a single-stub match on the Smith chart, using admittance for the parallel connection.

#### 中文

本题考查用 Smith 图完成单支节匹配，重点是并联时应使用导纳。

### Givens

#### English

The load is $Z_L=(25+j90)\,\Omega$, all line sections have $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, and the matching stub is open-circuited.

#### 中文

负载为 $Z_L=(25+j90)\,\Omega$，所有线段均有 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$，匹配支节开路。

### Goal

#### English

Find normalized impedance and admittance, then the through-line distance and open-stub length, showing the Smith-chart readings.

#### 中文

求归一化阻抗、导纳，以及直通线距离和开路支节长度，并标出 Smith 图上的读数过程。

### Approach

#### English

Normalize, reflect through the chart center to convert impedance to admittance, then rotate toward the generator to match conductance. The stub cancels susceptance; keep track of both branches and the direction of the wavelength scale.

#### 中文

归一化后先作阻抗到导纳的中心对称转换，再向发生器方向旋转到电导匹配处。此时支节只需补偿电纳，注意两条可行分支与读数方向。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the normalized load impedance $z_L$ and plot it on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

计算归一化负载阻抗 $z_L$，并在 Smith 图上标出。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the definition of normalized impedance:

#### 中文

使用归一化阻抗的定义：

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_0}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_L=25+j90\,\Omega$ and $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find $z_L$. Substitute and locate the point using the crib sheet’s [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection):

#### 中文

已知 $Z_L=25+j90\,\Omega$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求 $z_L$。代入并用 crib sheet 的[反射系数](formula:reflection)定位：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
z_L&=\frac{(25+j90)\,\Omega}{50\,\Omega}=0.5+j1.8,\\[4pt]
\Gamma_z&=\frac{z_L-1}{z_L+1}=\frac{-0.5+j1.8}{1.5+j1.8}\approx0.453552+j0.655738
\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

Mark the intersection of the $r=0.5$ circle and $x=+1.8$ arc:

#### 中文

在 $r=0.5$ 圆与 $x=+1.8$ 弧交点标出，得

#### Equation

$$
z_L=0.5+j1.8
$$

### Answer

$$
z_L=0.5+j1.8
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Determine and plot the location of the normalized load admittance $y_L$ on the Smith chart, then read off its numerical value.

#### 中文

确定并在 Smith 图上标出归一化负载导纳 $y_L$ 的位置，然后读出其数值。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the normalized admittance–impedance relation:

#### 中文

使用归一化导纳与阻抗的关系：

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac{Y_L}{Y_0}=\frac{1/Z_L}{1/Z_0}=\frac{Z_0}{Z_L}=\frac1{z_L}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Given $z_L=0.5+j1.8$, find $y_L$. Take the reciprocal; rotate the [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection)’s coordinate on the same Smith grid by $180^\circ$:

#### 中文

已知 $z_L=0.5+j1.8$，求 $y_L$。代入倒数式；将[反射系数](formula:reflection)坐标在同一 Smith 网格上旋转 $180^\circ$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle y_L=\frac1{0.5+j1.8}=\frac{0.5-j1.8}{0.5^2+1.8^2}=\frac{50-j180}{349}\approx0.143266-j0.515759\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_y=\frac{y_L-1}{y_L+1}=\frac{1-z_L}{1+z_L}=-\Gamma_z\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
y_L=0.143266-j0.515759
$$

### Answer

$$
y_L=0.143266-j0.515759
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Determine the length of the through-line $d$ that yields an input admittance whose real part is matched to the characteristic admittance $Y_0=1/Z_0$ (i.e. $y_d=1+jA$). Show all work for how you determined this length on the Smith chart. You may leave your answer in terms of wavelengths.

#### 中文

确定直通线的长度 $d$，使其输入导纳的实部与特性导纳 $Y_0=1/Z_0$ 匹配（即 $y_d=1+jA$）。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一长度的全部过程。答案可以用波长表示。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s [input-impedance formula](formula:zin) with $t=\tan(\beta d)$ and normalize:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[输入阻抗式](formula:zin)，令 $t=\tan(\beta d)$ 并归一化：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in},d}&=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta d)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta d)}\\z_d&=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in},d}}{Z_0}=\frac{z_L+jt}{1+jz_Lt}\\t&=\tan(\beta d),\qquad\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $y_L=(50-j180)/349$, find the distances where $\Re(y_d)=1$. Take the reciprocal impedance:

#### 中文

已知 $y_L=(50-j180)/349$，求 $\Re(y_d)=1$ 的距离。取阻抗的倒数：

#### Equation

$$
y_d=\frac1{z_d}=\frac{y_L+jt}{1+jy_Lt},\qquad y_L=g+jb
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $g+jb$, multiply by the denominator’s conjugate and set the real part to 1:

#### 中文

把 $g+jb$ 代入，乘分母共轭并令实部为 1：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_d&=\frac{[g+j(b+t)][(1-bt)-jgt]}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2},\\\Re(y_d)&=\frac{g(1+t^2)}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}=1,\\0&=(g^2+b^2-g)t^2-2bt+(1-g)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute $g=50/349$ and $b=-180/349$, solve both roots and choose $0\le d<\lambda/2$:

#### 中文

把 $g=50/349$、$b=-180/349$ 代入，解出两根并取 $0\le d<\lambda/2$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}50t^2+360t+299&=0,\\t_{1,2}&=\frac{-360\mp\sqrt{360^2-4(50)(299)}}{100}=\frac{-360\mp\sqrt{69800}}{100},\\t_1&\approx-6.241969,\qquad t_2\approx-0.958031,\\\frac{d_1}{\lambda}&=\frac{\arctan t_1+\pi}{2\pi}\approx0.275283,\\\frac{d_2}{\lambda}&=\frac{\arctan t_2+\pi}{2\pi}\approx0.378411\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Substitute both roots into $y_d$ to obtain the intersections of the constant-magnitude circle and $g=1$ circle:

#### 中文

把两根代回 $y_d$，得到等模圆与 $g=1$ 圆的两交点：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,1}&\approx\frac{0.143266-j6.757728}{-2.219354-j0.894265}\approx1+j2.641969,\\y_{d,2}&\approx\frac{0.143266-j1.473790}{0.505887-j0.137254}\approx\frac{0.274760-j0.725907}{0.274760}\approx1-j2.641969\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Choosing the shorter distance gives

#### 中文

选较短距离，得

#### Equation

$$
d=0.275283\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j2.641969
$$

### Answer

$$
d=0.275283\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j2.641969
$$

### Source correction

#### English

Check: the official chart gives $d\approx0.271\lambda$, $\ell\approx0.305\lambda$, $b\approx2.82$ and an alternative $d\approx0.318\lambda$. Direct substitution of $z_L=0.5+j1.8$ gives the values here; the other exact branch is $d=0.378411\lambda$, $\ell=0.192411\lambda$. Both are independently checked with the input-admittance equation.

#### 中文

核对：官方图给出 $d\approx0.271\lambda$、$\ell\approx0.305\lambda$、$b\approx2.82$，另一分支写 $d\approx0.318\lambda$。直接代入给定 $z_L=0.5+j1.8$ 后，精确结果为本页数值；另一分支 $d=0.378411\lambda$、$\ell=0.192411\lambda$。已用输入导纳方程独立校验。

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Determine the length of the open-circuited stub $\ell$ that completes the impedance match. Show all work for how you determined this length on the Smith chart. You may leave your answer in terms of wavelengths.

#### 中文

确定完成阻抗匹配所需的开路支节长度 $\ell$。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一长度的全部过程。答案可以用波长表示。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [open-stub impedance](formula:open) from the crib sheet, take its reciprocal and normalize:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[开路支节阻抗](formula:open)，取倒数并归一化：

#### Equation

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell),\qquad y_s=\frac{1/Z_s}{1/Z_0}=j\tan(\beta\ell)
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given the selected branch $y_d=1+j2.641969$, find $y_s$ and $\ell$. Substitute into the shunt matching condition:

#### 中文

已知选中分支 $y_d=1+j2.641969$，求 $y_s$、$\ell$。把 $y_d$ 代入并联匹配条件：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle y_{\mathrm{in}}=y_d+y_s=1,\qquad y_s=1-(1+jB)=-jB\\[4pt]&\displaystyle y_s=-j2.641969,\qquad \tan(2\pi\ell/\lambda)=-2.641969\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the required susceptance into the tangent relation and take the shortest nonnegative length. The other main-line branch uses the corresponding positive-susceptance stub:

#### 中文

把所需电纳代入正切式，取最短非负长度；另一主线分支使用对应的正电纳支节：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \beta\ell=\pi-\arctan(2.641969),\qquad \frac{\ell}{\lambda}=\frac{\pi-\arctan(2.641969)}{2\pi}=0.307589\\[4pt]&\displaystyle d_2=0.378411\lambda,\quad y_{s,2}=+j2.641969,\quad \ell_2=0.192411\lambda\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\ell=0.307589\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j2.641969
$$

### Answer

$$
\ell=0.307589\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j2.641969
$$
