## Question

### English

Use the included Smith chart to match the feedline’s characteristic impedance $Z_0$ to the load impedance $Z_L$ via the single-stub matching method. As shown below, the matching circuit consists of a through-line of length $d$, which is connected to the load and an open-circuited stub of length $\ell$, connected in parallel with the through-line. $Z_0=50\Omega$ for all transmission lines in this problem.

### 中文

使用所附 Smith 图，通过单支节匹配法将馈线的特性阻抗 $Z_0$ 与负载阻抗 $Z_L$ 匹配。如下图所示，匹配电路包含一段连接负载、长度为 $d$ 的直通线，以及一段与直通线并联、长度为 $\ell$ 的开路支节。本题所有传输线均有 $Z_0=50\Omega$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem constructs a single-stub match on the Smith chart, using admittance for the parallel connection.

#### 中文

本题考查用 Smith 图完成单支节匹配，重点是并联时应使用导纳。

### Givens

#### English

The load is $Z_L=(25+j90)\,\Omega$, all line sections have $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, and the matching stub is open-circuited.

#### 中文

负载为 $Z_L=(25+j90)\,\Omega$，所有线段均有 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$，匹配支节开路。

### Goal

#### English

Find normalized impedance and admittance, then the through-line distance and open-stub length, showing the Smith-chart readings.

#### 中文

求归一化阻抗、导纳，以及直通线距离和开路支节长度，并标出 Smith 图上的读数过程。

### Approach

#### English

Normalize, reflect through the chart center to convert impedance to admittance, then rotate toward the generator to match conductance. The stub cancels susceptance; keep track of both branches and the direction of the wavelength scale.

#### 中文

归一化后先作阻抗到导纳的中心对称转换，再向发生器方向旋转到电导匹配处。此时支节只需补偿电纳，注意两条可行分支与读数方向。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the normalized load impedance $z_L$ and plot it on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

计算归一化负载阻抗 $z_L$，并在 Smith 图上标出。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with normalization. The load impedance $z$ is dimensionless and referenced to the feedline $Z_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写归一化定义。这里 $z$ 为无量纲负载阻抗，参考阻抗取该题馈线的 $Z_{0}$。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Divide the real and imaginary parts by $Z_{0}$ separately, preserving the positive reactance.

#### 中文

把复负载的实部和虚部分别除以 $Z_{0}$，保留电抗的正号。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{(25+j90)\,\Omega}{50\,\Omega}=0.5+j1.8
$$

### Step 3

#### English

On the impedance chart intersect the $r=\operatorname{Re}(z_L)$ circle with the positive-$x$ reactance arc; verify the plotted coordinate with the reflection coefficient.

#### 中文

在 Smith 阻抗图中找 $r=\operatorname{Re}(z_L)$ 圆与 $x=\operatorname{Im}(z_L)>0$ 弧的交点；也可用反射系数验证坐标。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_z=\frac{z_L-1}{z_L+1}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 4

#### English

Multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's complex conjugate. Keep $j^2=-1$ explicit when expanding, so the real-part sign stays correct.

#### 中文

先把分子和分母同乘分母的共轭数。逐项相乘时保留 $j^2=-1$，否则实部的符号容易算错。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Gamma_z&=\frac{(-0.5+j1.8)(1.5-j1.8)}{(1.5+j1.8)(1.5-j1.8)}\\(-0.5+j1.8)(1.5-j1.8)&=-0.75+j0.9+j2.7-j^2(3.24)\\&=2.49+j3.60\\(1.5+j1.8)(1.5-j1.8)&=1.5^2+1.8^2=5.49\\\Gamma_z&=\frac{2.49}{5.49}+j\frac{3.60}{5.49}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Substitute the normalized complex impedance to obtain its $\Gamma$-plane coordinate, in the upper half-plane.

#### 中文

将已归一化的复数代入，得到该点的 $\Gamma$ 平面坐标；它位于上半平面。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_z=\frac{-0.5+j1.8}{1.5+j1.8}\approx0.453552+j0.655738
$$

### Answer

$$
z_L=0.5+j1.8
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Determine and plot the location of the normalized load admittance $y_L$ on the Smith chart, then read off its numerical value.

#### 中文

确定并在 Smith 图上标出归一化负载导纳 $y_L$ 的位置，然后读出其数值。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with normalized admittance and substitute $Y=1/Z$ to show why it equals $1/z_L$.

#### 中文

先写归一化导纳定义，再代入 $Y=1/Z$，说明为什么结果是 $1/z_L$。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac{Y_L}{Y_0}=\frac{1/Z_L}{1/Z_0}=\frac{Z_0}{Z_L}=\frac1{z_L}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

The conjugate of $u+jv$ is $u-jv$. Their cross terms cancel, and the final term $-j^2v^2$ becomes positive $v^2$.

#### 中文

复数 $u+jv$ 的共轭数是 $u-jv$。两者相乘时，两个交叉项一正一负而相消，最后的 $-j^2v^2$ 变成正的 $v^2$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(u+jv)(u-jv)&=u^2-juv+juv-j^2v^2\\&=u^2+v^2\\\frac1{u+jv}&=\frac{u-jv}{u^2+v^2}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Take the complex reciprocal by multiplying by the conjugate; the denominator becomes the sum of squared components.

#### 中文

复数取倒数要乘共轭数；分母变成实部平方加虚部平方。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac1{0.5+j1.8}=\frac{0.5-j1.8}{0.5^2+1.8^2}=\frac{50-j180}{349}\approx0.143266-j0.515759
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Reading admittance on the same impedance grid requires reversing the displayed point through the center, a $180^\circ$ rotation; the actual load reflection does not change sign.

#### 中文

在同一阻抗网格上读取导纳时，表示坐标应关于图心反转，即旋转 $180^\circ$。这不是让实际负载的反射改变符号。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_y=\frac{y_L-1}{y_L+1}=\frac{1-z_L}{1+z_L}=-\Gamma_z
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 5

#### English

Move to the diametrically opposite point on the same magnitude circle and read conductance and susceptance; the algebraic reciprocal verifies the chart.

#### 中文

因此沿同一个等模圆转到对径点，再读出电导与电纳；代数倒数与图上结果一致。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac1{0.5+j1.8}=\frac{0.5-j1.8}{0.5^2+1.8^2}=\frac{50-j180}{349}\approx0.143266-j0.515759
$$

### Answer

$$
y_L=0.143266-j0.515759
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Determine the length of the through-line $d$ that yields an input admittance whose real part is matched to the characteristic admittance $Y_0=1/Z_0$ (i.e. $y_d=1+jA$). Show all work for how you determined this length on the Smith chart. You may leave your answer in terms of wavelengths.

#### 中文

确定直通线的长度 $d$，使其输入导纳的实部与特性导纳 $Y_0=1/Z_0$ 匹配（即 $y_d=1+jA$）。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一长度的全部过程。答案可以用波长表示。

### Step 1

#### English

Start from the crib's lossless input impedance, then divide by the reference impedance. The line and load use the same reference impedance.

#### 中文

先写公式表中的无损线输入阻抗，再除以参考阻抗得到归一化输入阻抗。主线和负载使用同一个参考阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in},d}&=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta d)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta d)}\\z_d&=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in},d}}{Z_0}=\frac{z_L+jt}{1+jz_Lt}\\t&=\tan(\beta d),\qquad\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Admittance is the reciprocal of impedance. Divide numerator and denominator by $z_L$ to express the result using the previously calculated load admittance $y_L=1/z_L$.

#### 中文

导纳是阻抗的倒数。分子、分母同时除以 $z_L$，就能用已经求出的负载导纳 $y_L=1/z_L$ 表示。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_d&=\frac1{z_d}=\frac{1+jz_Lt}{z_L+jt}\\&=\frac{1/z_L+jt}{1+jt/z_L}\\&=\frac{y_L+jt}{1+jy_Lt}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Write load admittance as $g+jb$ and express the denominator with real part $1-bt$ and imaginary part $gt$.

#### 中文

把负载导纳分成 $g+jb$，将分母写成实部 $1-bt$ 与虚部 $gt$。

#### Equation

$$
g=\frac{50}{349},\quad b=-\frac{180}{349},\quad |y_L|^2=\frac{100}{349}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute $y_L=g+jb$ into numerator and denominator. Since $j\cdot j=-1$, the denominator has real part $1-bt$ and imaginary part $gt$.

#### 中文

把 $y_L=g+jb$ 代入分子和分母。分母里的 $j\cdot j=-1$，因此实部是 $1-bt$，虚部是 $gt$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_L+jt&=g+j(b+t)\\1+jy_Lt&=1+j(g+jb)t\\&=(1-bt)+jgt\\y_d&=\frac{g+j(b+t)}{(1-bt)+jgt}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's conjugate. The denominator becomes real, allowing the real and imaginary parts to be read separately.

#### 中文

分子和分母同乘分母的共轭数。这样分母变成实数，才可以分别读取实部和虚部。

#### Equation

$$
y_d=\frac{[g+j(b+t)][(1-bt)-jgt]}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Expand the numerator into real and imaginary terms. The real-part terms $-gbt$ and $gbt$ cancel, leaving $g(1+t^2)$.

#### 中文

展开分子的实部和虚部。实部中的两项 $-gbt$ 与 $gbt$ 恰好相消，留下 $g(1+t^2)$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}N_{\mathrm{real}}&=g(1-bt)+(b+t)gt\\&=g-gbt+gbt+gt^2\\&=g(1+t^2)\\N_{\mathrm{imag}}&=(b+t)(1-bt)-g^2t\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Multiply by the denominator conjugate and take the real part. Matching conductance to $Y_{0}$ requires normalized real part one.

#### 中文

乘分母共轭数，单独取实部。匹配实部要求实际输入电导等于 $Y_{0}$，即归一化实部等于 1。

#### Equation

$$
\Re(y_d)=\frac{g(1+t^2)}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}=1
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Expand the denominator and multiply the matching condition by it. Move $g+gt^2$ to the right and group by powers of $t$.

#### 中文

先展开分母，再将匹配条件两边同乘分母。最后把左边的 $g+gt^2$ 移到右边，并按 $t$ 的幂次整理。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2&=1-2bt+(b^2+g^2)t^2\\g+gt^2&=1-2bt+(b^2+g^2)t^2\\0&=(g^2+b^2-g)t^2-2bt+(1-g)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Substitute this problem's conductance and susceptance term by term. All three coefficients share denominator $349$; multiplying both sides by $349$ gives integer coefficients.

#### 中文

逐项代入本题的电导和电纳。三个系数有共同分母 $349$，两边乘 $349$ 后得到整数系数方程。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}g^2+b^2-g&=\frac{100}{349}-\frac{50}{349}=\frac{50}{349}\\-2b&=\frac{360}{349},\qquad1-g=\frac{299}{349}\\\frac{50}{349}t^2+\frac{360}{349}t+\frac{299}{349}&=0\\50t^2+360t+299&=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 10

#### English

This quadratic is not easily factored, so use the quadratic formula. Compute the discriminant, then retain both signs in the numerator. Negative roots are valid because they correspond to positive-distance tangent branches.

#### 中文

这次二次式不易因式分解，使用求根公式。先算判别式，再代入分子中的正负两个分支；负的根不能直接丢弃，因为它们对应正的传播距离分支。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Delta&=360^2-4(50)(299)\\&=129600-59800=69800\\t&=\frac{-360\pm\sqrt{69800}}{2(50)}\\t_1&=\frac{-360-\sqrt{69800}}{100}\\t_2&=\frac{-360+\sqrt{69800}}{100}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 11

#### English

List both $t$ values, then choose branches satisfying $0\le d<\lambda/2$ using $t=\tan(2\pi d/\lambda)$.

#### 中文

列出两个 $t$ 值，再由 $t=\tan(2\pi d/\lambda)$ 选 $0\le d<\lambda/2$ 的分支。

#### Equation

$$
t_1=-6.241969,\quad t_2=-0.958031
$$

### Step 12

#### English

Both tangent values are negative, so both principal arctangents are negative. For nonnegative distances, add the tangent period $\pi$ to each angle before dividing by $2\pi$, rather than arbitrarily changing the sign of the final distance.

#### 中文

两个正切值都为负，所以反正切给出的主值也都为负。传播距离要求非负，因此各加一个周期 $\pi$，再除以 $2\pi$，而不是给最终距离随意加负号。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}2\pi d_1/\lambda&=\arctan(-6.241969)+\pi\\&\approx-1.411940+\pi\\d_1/\lambda&\approx0.275283\\2\pi d_2/\lambda&=\arctan(-0.958031)+\pi\\&\approx-0.763967+\pi\\d_2/\lambda&\approx0.378411\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 13

#### English

Insert the first root into the full complex fraction. Evaluate numerator and denominator before multiplying by the denominator conjugate. These decimals illustrate the calculation; verify the result using unrounded values.

#### 中文

把第一根代回完整的复数分式。先计算分子和分母，再乘分母共轭数；以下小数只用于展示运算，最终结果使用未舍入值核对。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,1}&\approx\frac{0.143266-j6.757728}{-2.219354-j0.894265}\\&\approx\frac{(0.143266-j6.757728)(-2.219354+j0.894265)}{(-2.219354)^2+0.894265^2}\\&\approx\frac{5.725240+j15.125907}{5.725240}\\&\approx1+j2.641969\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 14

#### English

Substitute the second root independently rather than changing the first answer's sign without calculation. Complex division gives another intersection with real part $1$ and opposite imaginary part.

#### 中文

第二根也必须代回原式，不能只把第一组答案的符号凭空改变。同样做复数除法后，得到实部为 $1$、虚部相反的另一交点。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,2}&\approx\frac{0.143266-j1.473790}{0.505887-j0.137254}\\&\approx\frac{(0.143266-j1.473790)(0.505887+j0.137254)}{0.505887^2+0.137254^2}\\&\approx\frac{0.274760-j0.725907}{0.274760}\\&\approx1-j2.641969\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 15

#### English

Choose the first nonnegative distance as the primary solution; the other branch is also valid.

#### 中文

选第一组非负距离作为本页主答案；另一分支仍是有效匹配方案。

#### Equation

$$
d=0.275283\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j2.641969
$$

### Answer

$$
d=0.275283\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j2.641969
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Determine the length of the open-circuited stub $\ell$ that completes the impedance match. Show all work for how you determined this length on the Smith chart. You may leave your answer in terms of wavelengths.

#### 中文

确定完成阻抗匹配所需的开路支节长度 $\ell$。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一长度的全部过程。答案可以用波长表示。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib open-line impedance, then invert and normalize with $Y_{0}=1/Z_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写原表开路传输线阻抗，再取倒数并用 $Y_{0}=1/Z_{0}$ 归一化。

#### Equation

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell),\qquad y_s=\frac{1/Z_s}{1/Z_0}=j\tan(\beta\ell)
$$

#### References

- [open](formula:open)

### Step 2

#### English

When taking the open-stub impedance reciprocal, cancel the reference impedance first. Then use $1/(-j)=j$ and $1/\cot\theta=\tan\theta$ to obtain the admittance formula step by step.

#### 中文

把开路支节阻抗取倒数时，先约去参考阻抗。再利用 $1/(-j)=j$ 和 $1/\cot\theta=\tan\theta$，逐步得到所用的导纳式。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_s&=\frac{Z_0}{-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell)}\\&=\frac1{-j}\frac1{\cot(\beta\ell)}\\\frac1{-j}&=\frac j{(-j)j}=j\\y_s&=j\tan(\beta\ell)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Parallel admittances add and the target total is one. If the main line is $1+jB$, the stub must supply $-jB$.

#### 中文

支节并联时导纳相加，目标是总归一化导纳为 1。若主线为 $1+jB$，支节必须为 $-jB$。

#### Equation

$$
y_{\mathrm{in}}=y_d+y_s=1,\qquad y_s=1-(1+jB)=-jB
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Insert the susceptance of the selected branch from c) and write the tangent equation.

#### 中文

代入 c) 选中分支的电纳，列出需要满足的正切方程。

#### Equation

$$
y_s=-j2.641969,\qquad \tan(2\pi\ell/\lambda)=-2.641969
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Choose the smallest nonnegative open-stub length; a negative tangent requires a second-quadrant electrical length.

#### 中文

选最小非负的开路支节长度；正切为负时使用第二象限的电长度。

#### Equation

$$
\beta\ell=\pi-\arctan(2.641969),\qquad \frac{\ell}{\lambda}=\frac{\pi-\arctan(2.641969)}{2\pi}=0.307589
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Check exact susceptance cancellation. Choosing the other main-line intersection also requires its corresponding stub length.

#### 中文

核对虚部恰好抵消；若改用另一主线交点，必须同时改用配套的支节长度。

#### Equation

$$
d_2=0.378411\lambda,\quad y_{s,2}=+j2.641969,\quad \ell_2=0.192411\lambda
$$

### Step 7

#### English

The open-stub parameters for the selected branch are:

#### 中文

本页所选分支的开路支节参数如下。

#### Equation

$$
\ell=0.307589\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j2.641969
$$

### Answer

$$
\ell=0.307589\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j2.641969
$$
