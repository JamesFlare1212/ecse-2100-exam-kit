## Question

### English

A transmission line of characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$ and time delay $T_d=50\mathrm{ps}$ is connected between a generator circuit with voltage $V_g=10\mathrm{V}$ and generator impedance $R_g=30\Omega$, and a load impedance $Z_L=150\Omega$, as shown below. The switch closes at time $t=0$ and provides a DC voltage to the transmission line.

### 中文

一根特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$、时间延迟为 $T_d=50\mathrm{ps}$ 的传输线，连接在电压为 $V_g=10\mathrm{V}$、源阻抗为 $R_g=30\Omega$ 的发生器电路与负载阻抗 $Z_L=150\Omega$ 之间，如下图所示。开关在 $t=0$ 闭合，向传输线施加直流电压。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem distinguishes the transient reflections of a sustained DC step from the eventual steady state.

#### 中文

本题考查持续直流阶跃在失配传输线上的反弹过程，以及暂态与稳态的区别。

### Givens

#### English

The sustained source has $V_g=10\,\mathrm V$, $R_g=30\,\Omega$, $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $R_L=150\,\Omega$, with one-way delay $T_d=50\,\mathrm{ps}$.

#### 中文

$V_g=10\,\mathrm V$、$R_g=30\,\Omega$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=150\,\Omega$，单程延迟 $T_d=50\,\mathrm{ps}$；开关闭合后电源持续接通。

### Goal

#### English

Find both reflection coefficients and the first traveling waves, plot the specified load-voltage history, and find the steady-state voltage.

#### 中文

求两端反射系数和前几次行波，画出指定时间内的负载电压，再求稳态电压。

### Approach

#### English

Start with the launched wave and reflect it at each arrival time. Load voltage combines incident and reflected waves; the long-time limit should agree with the DC equivalent circuit.

#### 中文

先求初始发射波，再按到达端点的时刻逐次反射。负载电压是入射波与反射波的和；长时间结果还应与直流等效电路相符。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the following quantities:
• The reflection coefficient at the load $\Gamma_L$
• The reflection coefficient at the generator $\Gamma_g$

#### 中文

计算下列量：
• 负载端反射系数 $\Gamma_L$
• 发生器端反射系数 $\Gamma_g$

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib endpoint voltage reflection coefficient, referencing both ends to the same line $Z_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写原表的端点电压反射系数；负载端和源端都以同一个线路 $Z_{0}$ 为参考。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{Z-Z_0}{Z+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

At the load insert $150\,\Omega$ and $50\,\Omega$, evaluating the difference and sum separately.

#### 中文

负载端代入 $150\,\Omega$ 和 $50\,\Omega$，先算差再算和。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{150-50}{150+50}=\frac{100}{200}=+0.50
$$

### Step 3

#### English

A wave returning to the source sees its $30\,\Omega$ resistance, giving a negative coefficient.

#### 中文

源端返回波看到 $30\,\Omega$ 源电阻，因此对应系数为负。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_g=\frac{30-50}{30+50}=\frac{-20}{80}=-0.25
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Both coefficients are dimensionless voltage ratios; the negative sign indicates voltage inversion at the source.

#### 中文

两系数均为电压比，无量纲；负号表示源端反射电压反相。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=0.50,\qquad\Gamma_g=-0.25
$$

### Answer

$$
\Gamma_L=0.50,\qquad\Gamma_g=-0.25
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Calculate numerical values for $V_1^+$, $V_1^-$, $V_2^+$ and $V_2^-$ in the bounce diagram below.

#### 中文

计算下方反弹图中 $V_1^+$、$V_1^-$、$V_2^+$ 和 $V_2^-$ 的数值。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the launch divider and reflection recurrence, then evaluate in propagation order.

#### 中文

先写初始分压和反射递推式，再按传播顺序逐个计算。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0},\qquad V_n^-=\Gamma_LV_n^+,\qquad V_{n+1}^+=\Gamma_gV_n^-
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)
- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

At $t=0$ substitute $10\,\mathrm{V}$, $30\,\Omega$ and $50\,\Omega$ to obtain the first forward step.

#### 中文

$t=0$ 时把 $10\,\mathrm{V}$、$30\,\Omega$、$50\,\Omega$ 代入，得到第一正向阶跃。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=(10\,\mathrm V)\frac{50}{30+50}=10\frac58=6.25\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 3

#### English

At $50\,\mathrm{ps}$ the wave reaches the load; multiply by $+0.5$ for the first backward wave.

#### 中文

$50\,\mathrm{ps}$ 时到达负载，第一反向波乘负载反射系数 $+0.5$。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^-=\Gamma_LV_1^+=0.5(6.25)=3.125\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 4

#### English

At $100\,\mathrm{ps}$ the return reaches the source; multiply by $-0.25$ for the second forward step.

#### 中文

$100\,\mathrm{ps}$ 时反向波到源，再乘 $-0.25$，得到第二正向阶跃。

#### Equation

$$
V_2^+=\Gamma_gV_1^-=-0.25(3.125)=-0.78125\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 5

#### English

At $150\,\mathrm{ps}$ the second forward step reaches the load; multiply by $+0.5$, retaining its negative sign.

#### 中文

$150\,\mathrm{ps}$ 时第二正向阶跃到负载，再乘 $+0.5$；两次反射的负号不能丢失。

#### Equation

$$
V_2^-=\Gamma_LV_2^+=0.5(-0.78125)=-0.390625\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Label these four traveling-wave amplitudes on their four bounce trajectories.

#### 中文

把四个行波幅度标在反弹图对应的四条轨迹上。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^+=6.25\,\mathrm V,\quad V_1^-=3.125\,\mathrm V\\V_2^+=-0.78125\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=-0.390625\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^+=6.25\,\mathrm V,\quad V_1^-=3.125\,\mathrm V\\V_2^+=-0.78125\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=-0.390625\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Sketch a plot of the voltage at the load $V(z=L)$ vs. time for $0\le t\le150\mathrm{ps}$. Label voltage amplitudes and critical times, such as when the voltage wave undergoes relevant reflections.

#### 中文

画出 $0\le t\le150\mathrm{ps}$ 内负载电压 $V(z=L)$ 随时间变化的曲线。标注电压幅值和关键时刻，例如电压波发生相关反射的时刻。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with each load voltage increment: add the arriving step and its immediate reflected step.

#### 中文

先写每次到达负载所增加的电压：入射阶跃与即时反射阶跃相加。

#### Equation

$$
\Delta V_{L,n}=V_n^++V_n^-=(1+\Gamma_L)V_n^+
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)
- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

The first transit takes $50\,\mathrm{ps}$, so the load voltage remains unchanged before that arrival.

#### 中文

第一行波传播时间为 $50\,\mathrm{ps}$，未到达前，负载处仍没有电压变化。

#### Equation

$$
V_L(t)=0\quad(0\le t<50\,\mathrm{ps})
$$

### Step 3

#### English

At $50\,\mathrm{ps}$ substitute $V_{1}^{+}$ and $\Gamma_L$ to obtain the first plateau.

#### 中文

$50\,\mathrm{ps}$ 到达时代入 $V_{1}^{+}$ 与 $\Gamma_L$，求第一次阶跃后的平台值。

#### Equation

$$
V_L(50^+\,\mathrm{ps})=0+(1+0.5)(6.25)=9.375\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The $100\,\mathrm{ps}$ reflection occurs at the source and needs another $50\,\mathrm{ps}$ to affect the load, so the intermediate plateau stays unchanged.

#### 中文

$100\,\mathrm{ps}$ 的反射发生在源端；还需再传播 $50\,\mathrm{ps}$ 才影响负载，所以中间平台保持不变。

#### Equation

$$
V_L(t)=9.375\,\mathrm V\quad(50\le t<150\,\mathrm{ps})
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The second arrival at $150\,\mathrm{ps}$ gives a negative increment. With the source still on, add it to the existing plateau.

#### 中文

$150\,\mathrm{ps}$ 第二次入射带来负的增量。源持续接通，所以新平台要在旧平台上累加。

#### Equation

$$
\Delta V_{L,2}=(1+0.5)(-0.78125)=-1.171875\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Add the increment to $9.375\,\mathrm{V}$ and mark right-continuous ideal-step values.

#### 中文

将增量加到 $9.375\,\mathrm{V}$，按右连续理想阶跃标记端点。

#### Equation

$$
V_L(150^+\,\mathrm{ps})=9.375-1.171875=8.203125\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_L(t)=\begin{cases}0&0\le t<50\,\mathrm{ps}\\9.375\,\mathrm V&50\le t<150\,\mathrm{ps}\\8.203125\,\mathrm V&t=150^+\,\mathrm{ps}\end{cases}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the voltage on the transmission line after it has reached steady state $V_{ss}$.

#### 中文

计算传输线达到稳态后的电压 $V_{ss}$。

### Step 1

#### English

At final sustained-DC steady state the lossless line is an ideal connection. Begin with the series-loop voltage law.

#### 中文

持续 DC 的最终稳态中，无损传输线等效为理想连接；用串联回路的电压定律开始推导。

#### Equation

$$
V_g=I_\infty R_g+I_\infty R_L=I_\infty(R_g+R_L)
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Solve for final current, then multiply by the load resistance.

#### 中文

先求最终电流，再乘负载电阻。

#### Equation

$$
I_\infty=\frac{V_g}{R_g+R_L},\qquad V_\infty=I_\infty R_L=V_g\frac{R_L}{R_g+R_L}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $10\,\mathrm{V}$, $30\,\Omega$ and $150\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

代入 $10\,\mathrm{V}$、$30\,\Omega$ 和 $150\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
V_\infty=(10\,\mathrm V)\frac{150}{30+150}=\frac{1500}{180}\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 4

#### English

For the bounce-series check, start with a finite geometric sum. Multiply by the common ratio and subtract so the interior terms cancel. Take the infinite limit only when the ratio magnitude is less than $1$.

#### 中文

若用反弹序列求和，先看有限项的等比级数。将级数乘以公比后相减，中间项逐一抵消；只有在公比的模小于 $1$ 时才能再取无限项极限。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}S_N&=1+q+q^2+\cdots+q^N\\qS_N&=q+q^2+\cdots+q^{N+1}\\(1-q)S_N&=1-q^{N+1}\\S_N&=\frac{1-q^{N+1}}{1-q}\\|q|<1&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\lim_{N\to\infty}S_N=\frac1{1-q}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Alternatively sum the bounce increments: the first is $9.375\,\mathrm{V}$ and each round trip multiplies it by $\Gamma_g\Gamma_L=-1/8$.

#### 中文

也可对反弹图求和：第一负载增量为 $9.375\,\mathrm{V}$，每次往返乘 $\Gamma_g\Gamma_L=-1/8$。

#### Equation

$$
V_\infty=9.375\sum_{n=0}^\infty(-1/8)^n=\frac{9.375}{1-(-1/8)}
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 6

#### English

The geometric sum agrees with the DC circuit divider.

#### 中文

级数求和与 DC 电路分压完全一致。

#### Equation

$$
V_\infty=\frac{25}{3}\,\mathrm V\approx8.333\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_\infty=\frac{25}{3}\,\mathrm V\approx8.333\,\mathrm V
$$
