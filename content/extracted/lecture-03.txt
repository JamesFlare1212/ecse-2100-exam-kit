Generalized Impedance (Wave Impedance)




• We see that going backward a quarter wavelength from an open circuit load
  produces the same voltage pattern you would see if there were a short circuit
  load located just to the right of that point.
•  In other words, as you move backward from the load, it begins to “look like” a
   different load.
• The expression for Γ(z), the phase-shifted reflection coefficient, takes into
  account the load as well as the distance from it.
•  If we travel all the way back to the beginning of the transmission line, we can
   figure out what load the source circuit sees when we connect a t-line to it.
  This is called the input impedance of the transmission line.
