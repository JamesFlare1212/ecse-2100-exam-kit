Magnetostatics

• What determines a material’s permeability or whether or not it is
  magnetic?
     •  Atoms are tiny magnets pointed in random directions due to the current and
      spin of the orbiting electrons
     •  In some materials, this intrinsic magnetism is stronger than in others, leading
       to higher 𝜇.
     •  The higher the μ, the more an externally-applied H-field leads to a stronger
       B-field due to alignment of the atoms and the more magnetic the material is

Ampere’s Law
•  In differential form, Ampere’s law states:
           ∇× 𝐻= Ԧ𝐽

        “Current densities generate a circulating magnetic field according

•  Previously, we said the following:

                         “The circulation of a vector field in practice is often
                       due to a distributionof curl - that is, the vector field
                              acts less like it has one whirlpool and more like it
                        has a distribution of infinitesimally-small whirlpools.”

                        How does this relate to Ampere’s law?
