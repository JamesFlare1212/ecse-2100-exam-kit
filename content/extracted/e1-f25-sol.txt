e)  [1 pt] Even though the transmission line you chose in part (d) delivers maximum power to the load in
    steady state, reflections will still occur on the transmission line at the load. How can there be
   maximum power transfer, but also reflections on the transmission line?

    In steady state, the total voltage and current waves on the transmission line result from the sum of the
    incident and reflected waves, which interfere with each other to create an impedance that varies along
    the transmission line. By choosing the transmission line length appropriately, the input impedance 𝑍𝑖𝑛
      can be made to be 50 Ω, enabling the source to deliver maximum power to the load, even though
                                        reflections still occur at the load.
