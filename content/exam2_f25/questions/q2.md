## Question

### English

In the system below, the space between two conducting plates located at $x=0$ and $x=L$ is filled with two different dielectrics:
• Region 1 occupies the space $\frac{H}{2}<z\le H$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_1$.
• Region 2 occupies the space $0\le z<H/2$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_2$.

The plate at $x=0$ is held at a constant potential $V_0$, while the plate at $x=L$ is held at a constant potential $V_1$. The plates have height $H$ in the $z$-dimension and depth $W$ in the $y$-direction.
The electric field in region 1 is given by $\vec E_1(x)=\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat x$.

### 中文

下方系统中，位于 $x=0$ 和 $x=L$ 的两块导体板之间填充了两种不同的电介质：
• 区域 1 占据 $\frac{H}{2}<z\le H$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_1$。
• 区域 2 占据 $0\le z<H/2$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_2$。

位于 $x=0$ 的板保持恒定电势 $V_0$，位于 $x=L$ 的板保持恒定电势 $V_1$。板在 $z$ 方向的高度为 $H$，在 $y$ 方向的深度为 $W$。
区域 1 的电场给定为 $\vec E_1(x)=\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat x$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem combines dielectric boundary conditions with field-energy capacitance and a parallel-capacitor check.

#### 中文

本题考查介质边界条件和电场能量法，并用电路中的并联电容作核对。

### Givens

#### English

The plates are separated by $L$ at potentials $V_0$ and $V_1$. Two dielectrics of permittivities $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ each occupy half the height and share the plate voltage.

#### 中文

两极板间距为 $L$，电势分别为 $V_0$、$V_1$；两种介质沿高度各占一半，介电常数为 $\varepsilon_1$、$\varepsilon_2$，共同承受两板间电压。

### Goal

#### English

Find the field and displacement in the second dielectric, total stored energy and capacitance, then compare with the parallel-capacitor rule.

#### 中文

求第二种介质中的电场与电位移、总储能和电容，再判断是否符合并联电容规则。

### Approach

#### English

Identify the field direction relative to the dielectric interface and use the tangential boundary condition. Integrate energy over each dielectric and add the results, checking the field's sign against the plate potentials.

#### 中文

先看清电场相对介质界面的方向，选择切向边界条件。分别对两块介质积分能量后相加；电场方向的符号还要与极板电势核对。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field, $\vec E_2(x)$, and displacement field, $\vec D_2(x)$, in region 2. Ignore fringing effects.

#### 中文

求区域 2 内的电场 $\vec E_2(x)$ 和电位移场 $\vec D_2(x)$。忽略边缘效应。

### Step 1

#### English

Write tangential-field continuity and the constitutive relation. The dielectric interface normal is $z$, so the $x$-directed field is tangential.

#### 中文

先写切向电场连续条件及本构关系。介质分界面法向为 $z$，题中的电场沿 $x$，属于切向分量。

#### Equation

$$
E_{1t}=E_{2t},\qquad\vec{D}_2=\epsilon_2\vec{E}_2
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

Find the potential from the stated plate potentials: without fringing it varies linearly with $x$.

#### 中文

用给定的两块等势板求电位函数：无边缘效应时电位随 $x$ 线性变化。

#### Equation

$$
V(x)=V_0+\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}x,\quad V(0)=V_0,\quad V(L)=V_1
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Differentiate the linear potential term by term. The plate potentials $V_0$, $V_1$ and separation $L$ are constants; only the path coordinate $x$ varies. The constant term has derivative zero and $x$ has derivative $1$.

#### 中文

对线性电势逐项求导。板电位 $V_0$、$V_1$ 和间距 $L$ 都是常数，只有路径坐标 $x$ 是变量，因此常数项的导数为零，而 $x$ 对自身的导数为 $1$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{dV}{dx}&=\frac d{dx}\left[V_0+\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}x\right]\\&=0+\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}(1)\\&=\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Differentiate using $\vec E=-\nabla V$. The printed positive sign conflicts with the plate potentials; use the direction consistent with those potentials.

#### 中文

按 $\vec E=-\nabla V$ 求导。题面与官方答案给出的正号和板电位矛盾，下面使用与板电位一致的方向。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_1=-\frac{dV}{dx}\hat{x}=-\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat{x}
$$

#### References

- [gradient](formula:gradient)

### Step 5

#### English

Tangential continuity gives $\vec E_2=\vec E_1$. Substitute this into $\vec D_2=\epsilon_2\vec E_2$; both normal components are zero.

#### 中文

切向连续给出 $\vec E_2=\vec E_1$；再将它代入 $\vec D_2=\epsilon_2\vec E_2$，法向分量均为零。

#### Equation

$$
E_{2x}=E_{1x}=\frac{V_0-V_1}{L},\qquad D_{2x}=\epsilon_2\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Assemble the requested vectors. Following the printed field instead reverses both $\vec E$ and $\vec D$; the later energy is unchanged.

#### 中文

组合为所求两个矢量。若沿用试卷印出的场方向，$\vec E$ 和 $\vec D$ 的符号一起反转，后面的能量不变。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_2=\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x},\quad\vec{D}_2=\epsilon_2\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_2=\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x},\quad\vec{D}_2=\epsilon_2\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x}
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The prompt and official solution print $+(V_1-V_0)/L$, inconsistent with the stated potentials and $\vec E=-\nabla V$. This version uses the consistent minus sign. Taking the printed field instead also reverses $\vec D$; energy and capacitance remain unchanged.

#### 中文

原题与官方解把 $\vec E$ 写成 $+(V_1-V_0)/L$，与所给边界电位及 $\vec E=-\nabla V$ 不一致。这里采用物理一致的负号；若按题面给定场作答，$\vec D$ 同时反号，后续能量和电容不受影响。

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Using your results from part a), find the total electric energy stored in the region between the conducting plates.

#### 中文

利用第 a) 问的结果，求导体板之间区域储存的总电能。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the field-energy volume integral; integrate each dielectric separately and then add.

#### 中文

先写电场能量的体积分原式；两种介质须分别积分后相加。

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac12\iiint_{\mathcal V}\epsilon|\vec{E}|^2\,d\mathcal V=W_{e1}+W_{e2}
$$

#### References

- [energy](formula:energy)

### Step 2

#### English

The field magnitudes agree in both regions. Squaring removes dependence on field direction or the printed sign.

#### 中文

两区域的场强大小相同，平方后电场方向或题面符号差异不会影响能量。

#### Equation

$$
|\vec{E}_1|^2=|\vec{E}_2|^2=\frac{(V_1-V_0)^2}{L^2}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

For region 1, integrate $x$ from 0 to $L$, $y$ from 0 to $W$ and $z$ from $H/2$ to $H$.

#### 中文

区域 1 的三个积分范围分别为 $x$ 从 0 到 $L$、$y$ 从 0 到 $W$、$z$ 从 $H/2$ 到 $H$。

#### Equation

$$
W_{e1}=\frac12\int_{H/2}^H\!\int_0^W\!\int_0^L\epsilon_1\frac{(V_1-V_0)^2}{L^2}\,dx\,dy\,dz
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The region-1 integrand is constant. Integrate over $x$ and then $y$, showing the antiderivative and endpoint substitution each time.

#### 中文

区域 1 的被积函数是常数。先对 $x$ 积分，再对 $y$ 积分，每次都写出原函数和端点。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^L1\,dx&=[x]_0^L=L-0=L\\\int_0^W L\,dy&=L[y]_0^W=L(W-0)=LW\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Evaluate the final $z$ integral in region 1. Its lower limit is $H/2$, so the height is $H-H/2$. Then restore the energy-density coefficient.

#### 中文

最后算区域 1 的 $z$ 积分。它的下限是 $H/2$，所以高度为 $H-H/2$。再把能量密度系数乘回来。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_{H/2}^H LW\,dz&=LW[z]_{H/2}^H\\&=LW(H-H/2)=\frac{LWH}{2}\\W_{e1}&=\frac{\epsilon_1(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}\frac{LWH}{2}\\&=\frac{\epsilon_1HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Set up the full triple integral for region 2 as well. The field magnitude is the same, but its permittivity is $\epsilon_2$ and the height interval is $0$ to $H/2$.

#### 中文

区域 2 也先完整列出三重积分。电场大小相同，但介电常数改为 $\epsilon_2$，厚度区间改为 $0$ 到 $H/2$。

#### Equation

$$
W_{e2}=\frac{\epsilon_2(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}\int_0^{H/2}\!\int_0^W\!\int_0^L1\,dx\,dy\,dz
$$

### Step 7

#### English

The two inner integrals in region 2 again give $LW$. For the last integral, explicitly subtract the lower limit from the upper limit to obtain the other half of the volume.

#### 中文

区域 2 的内两层积分仍给出 $LW$。对最后一层写出上限减下限，得到另一半体积。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^L1\,dx&=[x]_0^L=L\\\int_0^W L\,dy&=L[y]_0^W=LW\\\int_0^{H/2}LW\,dz&=LW[z]_0^{H/2}\\&=LW(H/2-0)=\frac{LWH}{2}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Restore the region-2 coefficient. One factor of $2$ comes from the energy formula and the other from the half-height; one numerator factor of $L$ cancels against $L^2$.

#### 中文

乘回区域 2 的系数。分母的 $2$ 来自能量公式，另一个 $2$ 来自半高度；分子的一个 $L$ 与分母的 $L^2$ 约去。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}W_{e2}&=\frac{\epsilon_2(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}\frac{LWH}{2}\\&=\frac{\epsilon_2HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Factor the common terms and add both contributions.

#### 中文

提取两项的公因子并相加。

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

### Answer

$$
W_e=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the capacitance of the system using the total stored electric energy method.

#### 中文

使用总储存电能法求该系统的电容。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib capacitor-energy relation, with voltage equal to the plate potential difference.

#### 中文

原表给出电容器储能与电压的关系，这里的电压是两板电势差。

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac12C(\Delta V)^2,\qquad\Delta V=V_1-V_0
$$

#### References

- [energy](formula:energy)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute the full energy found above rather than jumping to a parallel-plate capacitance formula.

#### 中文

将上问求出的总能量完整代入，而不是直接使用平行板电容结论。

#### Equation

$$
\frac12C(V_1-V_0)^2=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Multiply by two and cancel the squared potential difference. The zero-voltage case follows from the same linear capacitance relation.

#### 中文

两边乘 2 并约去电势差的平方；零电压的情形由同一线性电容关系延续得到。

#### Equation

$$
C=\frac{2}{(V_1-V_0)^2}\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Simplify the coefficient and check that the dimension is farads.

#### 中文

化简系数，并检查量纲为 $\mathrm{F}$。

#### Equation

$$
C=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{2L}
$$

### Answer

$$
C=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{2L}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Bonus: Does your result in part c) agree with how parallel capacitances combine according to circuit theory? Explain.

#### 中文

附加题：第 c) 问的结果是否符合电路理论中并联电容的组合方式？解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the definition of capacitance. The two regions share the same pair of equipotential plates, so they have the same voltage.

#### 中文

从电容定义出发；并联等效的条件是两个区域连接同一对等势板。

#### Equation

$$
C_i=\frac{Q_i}{\Delta V},\qquad \Delta V_1=\Delta V_2=\Delta V
$$

#### References

- [capacitance](formula:capacitance)

### Step 2

#### English

Each region occupies half the height, so each area is $HW/2$ and both field-direction gaps are $L$.

#### 中文

两区域各占 $H/2$ 的高度，面积均为 $HW/2$；场方向上的板间距都是 $L$。

#### Equation

$$
A_1=A_2=\frac{HW}{2},\qquad C_1=\frac{\epsilon_1HW}{2L},\quad C_2=\frac{\epsilon_2HW}{2L}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Add the two charge contributions on a plate and substitute $Q_{i}=C_{i}\Delta V$.

#### 中文

同一板上的两部分电荷相加，把 $Q_{i}=C_{i}\Delta V$ 逐项代入。

#### Equation

$$
Q=Q_1+Q_2=C_1\Delta V+C_2\Delta V=(C_1+C_2)\Delta V
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Divide by the common voltage to recover the same parallel result as the energy method.

#### 中文

除以共同电压，即得到与能量法相同的并联结果。

#### Equation

$$
C=C_1+C_2
$$

### Answer

$$
C=C_1+C_2
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Yes: the regions are in parallel. The bonus is excluded from the exam’s stated 42 points.

#### 中文

是，两区域等效并联。Bonus 不计入试卷标称 42 分。
