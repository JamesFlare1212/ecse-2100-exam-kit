## Question

### English

In the system below, the space between two conducting plates located at $x=0$ and $x=L$ is filled with two different dielectrics:
• Region 1 occupies the space $\frac{H}{2}<z\le H$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_1$.
• Region 2 occupies the space $0\le z<H/2$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_2$.

The plate at $x=0$ is held at a constant potential $V_0$, while the plate at $x=L$ is held at a constant potential $V_1$. The plates have height $H$ in the $z$-dimension and depth $W$ in the $y$-direction.
The electric field in region 1 is given by $\vec E_1(x)=\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat x$.

### 中文

下方系统中，位于 $x=0$ 和 $x=L$ 的两块导体板之间填充了两种不同的电介质：
• 区域 1 占据 $\frac{H}{2}<z\le H$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_1$。
• 区域 2 占据 $0\le z<H/2$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_2$。

位于 $x=0$ 的板保持恒定电势 $V_0$，位于 $x=L$ 的板保持恒定电势 $V_1$。板在 $z$ 方向的高度为 $H$，在 $y$ 方向的深度为 $W$。
区域 1 的电场给定为 $\vec E_1(x)=\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat x$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem combines dielectric boundary conditions with field-energy capacitance and a parallel-capacitor check.

#### 中文

本题考查介质边界条件和电场能量法，并用电路中的并联电容作核对。

### Givens

#### English

The plates are separated by $L$ at potentials $V_0$ and $V_1$. Two dielectrics of permittivities $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$ each occupy half the height and share the plate voltage.

#### 中文

两极板间距为 $L$，电势分别为 $V_0$、$V_1$；两种介质沿高度各占一半，介电常数为 $\varepsilon_1$、$\varepsilon_2$，共同承受两板间电压。

### Goal

#### English

Find the field and displacement in the second dielectric, total stored energy and capacitance, then compare with the parallel-capacitor rule.

#### 中文

求第二种介质中的电场与电位移、总储能和电容，再判断是否符合并联电容规则。

### Approach

#### English

Identify the field direction relative to the dielectric interface and use the tangential boundary condition. Integrate energy over each dielectric and add the results, checking the field's sign against the plate potentials.

#### 中文

先看清电场相对介质界面的方向，选择切向边界条件。分别对两块介质积分能量后相加；电场方向的符号还要与极板电势核对。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field, $\vec E_2(x)$, and displacement field, $\vec D_2(x)$, in region 2. Ignore fringing effects.

#### 中文

求区域 2 内的电场 $\vec E_2(x)$ 和电位移场 $\vec D_2(x)$。忽略边缘效应。

### Step 1

#### English

Use [tangential-field continuity](formula:boundary), the [constitutive relation](formula:constitutive) and the [potential gradient](formula:gradient) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[切向场连续](formula:boundary)、[本构关系](formula:constitutive)和[电势梯度](formula:gradient)：

#### Equation

$$
E_{1t}=E_{2t},\qquad\vec D_2=\epsilon_2\vec E_2,\qquad\vec E=-\nabla V
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $V(0)=V_0$ and $V(L)=V_1$, find $\vec E_2$ and $\vec D_2$. Form the linear potential and [differentiate](formula:gradient) using the plate potentials; the printed field-sign conflict is retained in the source note.

#### 中文

已知 $V(0)=V_0$、$V(L)=V_1$，求 $\vec E_2$、$\vec D_2$。先按板电位代入线性电势并用[电势梯度](formula:gradient)求导；题面场方向的符号矛盾见核对说明。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V(x)=V_0+\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}x,\quad V(0)=V_0,\quad V(L)=V_1\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \vec{E}_1=-\frac{dV}{dx}\hat{x}=-\frac{V_1-V_0}{L}\hat{x}\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

The field is tangential to the dielectric interface along $x$. Substitute $E_{2x}=E_{1x}$ into the constitutive relation to obtain

#### 中文

因电场沿介质界面的切向 $x$ 方向，把 $E_{2x}=E_{1x}$ 代入本构关系，得

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_2=\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x},\quad\vec{D}_2=\epsilon_2\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_2=\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x},\quad\vec{D}_2=\epsilon_2\frac{V_0-V_1}{L}\hat{x}
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The prompt and official solution print $+(V_1-V_0)/L$, inconsistent with the stated potentials and $\vec E=-\nabla V$. This version uses the consistent minus sign. Taking the printed field instead also reverses $\vec D$; energy and capacitance remain unchanged.

#### 中文

原题与官方解把 $\vec E$ 写成 $+(V_1-V_0)/L$，与所给边界电位及 $\vec E=-\nabla V$ 不一致。这里采用物理一致的负号；若按题面给定场作答，$\vec D$ 同时反号，后续能量和电容不受影响。

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Using your results from part a), find the total electric energy stored in the region between the conducting plates.

#### 中文

利用第 a) 问的结果，求导体板之间区域储存的总电能。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [electric-field energy integral](formula:energy) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电场储能积分](formula:energy)：

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac12\iiint_{\mathcal V}\epsilon|\vec{E}|^2\,d\mathcal V=W_{e1}+W_{e2}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Using the field from (a), with each dielectric occupying half the height, find the total energy. First substitute the squared field:

#### 中文

已知 (a) 的场、两介质各占一半高度，求总储能。先把场强平方代入：

#### Equation

$$
|\vec{E}_1|^2=|\vec{E}_2|^2=\frac{(V_1-V_0)^2}{L^2}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute region 1’s $x,y,z$ bounds into the triple integral:

#### 中文

把区域 1 的 $x,y,z$ 范围代入三重积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}W_{e1}&=\frac12\int_{H/2}^H\!\int_0^W\!\int_0^L\epsilon_1\frac{(V_1-V_0)^2}{L^2}\,dx\,dy\,dz\\&=\frac{\epsilon_1(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}[x]_0^L[y]_0^W[z]_{H/2}^H\\&=\frac{\epsilon_1HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute region 2’s permittivity and height bounds:

#### 中文

把区域 2 的介电常数和高度范围代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}W_{e2}&=\frac{\epsilon_2(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}\int_0^{H/2}\!\int_0^W\!\int_0^L1\,dx\,dy\,dz\\&=\frac{\epsilon_2(V_1-V_0)^2}{2L^2}[x]_0^L[y]_0^W[z]_0^{H/2}\\&=\frac{\epsilon_2HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Adding the two regions gives

#### 中文

把两个区域相加，得

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

### Answer

$$
W_e=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the capacitance of the system using the total stored electric energy method.

#### 中文

使用总储存电能法求该系统的电容。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [capacitor-energy formula](formula:energy) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电容储能公式](formula:energy)：

#### Equation

$$
W_e=\frac12C(\Delta V)^2,\qquad\Delta V=V_1-V_0
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Using the total energy from (b), find $C$. Substitute and cancel the common squared potential difference:

#### 中文

已知 (b) 的总储能，求 $C$。把储能代入，并约去共同的电势差平方：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \frac12C(V_1-V_0)^2=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\\[4pt]&\displaystyle C=\frac{2}{(V_1-V_0)^2}\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{4L}(V_1-V_0)^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
C=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{2L}
$$

### Answer

$$
C=\frac{(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)HW}{2L}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Bonus: Does your result in part c) agree with how parallel capacitances combine according to circuit theory? Explain.

#### 中文

附加题：第 c) 问的结果是否符合电路理论中并联电容的组合方式？解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [capacitance definition](formula:capacitance) from the crib sheet; both regions share the same equipotential plates:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电容定义](formula:capacitance)；两个区域连接同一对等势板：

#### Equation

$$
C_i=\frac{Q_i}{\Delta V},\qquad \Delta V_1=\Delta V_2=\Delta V
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given area $HW/2$ and spacing $L$ for each region, check the parallel-capacitance relation. Substitute both charges into the total charge:

#### 中文

已知两区域面积均为 $HW/2$、间距为 $L$，求并联关系是否成立。把两部分电荷代入总电荷：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle A_1=A_2=\frac{HW}{2},\qquad C_1=\frac{\epsilon_1HW}{2L},\quad C_2=\frac{\epsilon_2HW}{2L}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Q=Q_1+Q_2=C_1\Delta V+C_2\Delta V=(C_1+C_2)\Delta V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
C=C_1+C_2
$$

### Answer

$$
C=C_1+C_2
$$

### Answer text

Yes: the regions are in parallel. The bonus is excluded from the exam’s stated 42 points.

#### 中文

是，两区域等效并联。Bonus 不计入试卷标称 42 分。
