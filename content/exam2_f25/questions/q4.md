## Question

### English

Two conducting spheres of radius $a$ are located on the $x$-axis: One sphere is located at $x=-d$ and has total surface charge $Q$, while the other sphere is located $x=d$ and has total surface charge $-Q$.
A diagram of this system is shown below.

### 中文

两个半径为 $a$ 的导体球位于 $x$ 轴上：一个位于 $x=-d$，总表面电荷为 $Q$；另一个位于 $x=d$，总表面电荷为 $-Q$。
下图给出了这一系统的示意图。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem combines vector field superposition, electric force and path independence of electrostatic potential.

#### 中文

本题考查电场的矢量叠加、受力以及静电势差的路径无关性。

### Givens

#### English

Two conducting spheres of radius $a$ are centered at $x=\pm d$ with charges $+Q$ and $-Q$. The observation point is $P=(0,0,z)$; the potential-difference question runs from the origin to $P$.

#### 中文

两个半径为 $a$ 的导体球位于 $x=\pm d$，分别带 $+Q$、$-Q$；观察点为 $P=(0,0,z)$，另有关于原点至 $P$ 的电势差问题。

### Goal

#### English

Start with the exterior field of one sphere, find the combined field and force on a test charge, and set up the potential integral with an explanation of path independence.

#### 中文

从单球外部电场出发求两球的合场、试探电荷受力，并列出电势差积分，说明积分路径是否影响结果。

### Approach

#### English

Under the exterior-sphere field model used by the exam, write each source-to-observer displacement vector before adding components. Add vectors rather than magnitudes; the later source note discusses the model's scope.

#### 中文

按试卷采用的球外场模型，先写出每个源指向观察点的位移矢量，再相加分量。电场是矢量，不能仅将两项大小相加；模型适用性见后面的核对说明。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

First, consider a conducting sphere of radius $a$ and total surface charge $Q$ located at the origin. Find the electric field due to this sphere for $r>a$ (outside the sphere).

#### 中文

首先考虑位于原点、半径为 $a$、总表面电荷为 $Q$ 的导体球。求该球在 $r>a$（球外）产生的电场。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with Gauss’s law on a concentric sphere of radius $R>a$ enclosing the isolated conducting sphere.

#### 中文

先写高斯定律，取包围孤立导体球的同心球面，半径 $R>a$。

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_{S_R}\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}=Q
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)

### Step 2

#### English

By spherical symmetry, substitute the radial field, constitutive relation and surface element.

#### 中文

由球对称性，代入径向电场、本构关系和面积元。

#### Equation

$$
\Phi_D=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi(\epsilon_0E_r(R)\hat{r})\cdot(\hat{r}R^2\sin\theta)\,d\theta d\phi
$$

#### References

- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)
- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 3

#### English

The dot product of the radial unit vectors is $1$. Taking the fixed radius and field outside leaves two angular integrals.

#### 中文

两个径向单位矢量的点积为 $1$。把固定半径和场强提出后，留下两个角积分。

#### Equation

$$
\Phi_D=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2\left(\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\right)\left(\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\right)
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Evaluate the antiderivative of each angular integrand. The polar-angle antiderivative is negative cosine, evaluated at $\pi$ and $0$; it is not the difference of the angle limits.

#### 中文

分别代入两个角积分的原函数。极角部分是负余弦，上限为 $\pi$、下限为 $0$，不是直接用角度相减。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi&=[\phi]_0^{2\pi}=2\pi\\\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta&=[-\cos\theta]_0^\pi\\&=-(-1)-(-1)=2\\\Phi_D&=4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)=Q\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Divide by spherical area and permittivity, then relabel $R$ as observation radius $r$.

#### 中文

两边除以球面积和介电常数，再把 $R$ 改记为观察半径 $r$。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}\hat{r}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}\hat{r}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the total electric field due to both of the conducting spheres in the figure above at the observation point $P=(0,0,z)$. You must express your answer in terms of cartesian coordinates.

#### 中文

求上图两个导体球在观察点 $P=(0,0,z)$ 产生的总电场。必须用笛卡尔坐标表示答案。

### Step 1

#### English

Start with Coulomb’s law and superposition, using the official approximation that each sphere acts as a point charge at its center.

#### 中文

从库仑定律和叠加原理出发。按官方解采用各球等效为球心点电荷的近似。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_i(P)=\frac{q_i}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{\vec{R}_i}{|\vec{R}_i|^3},\qquad\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sum_i\vec{E}_i
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)

### Step 2

#### English

Write both centers and the observation point, then subtract each center from $P$.

#### 中文

先写两个球心和观察点，再逐项相减得到从电荷到观察点的位移。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\vec{r}_+&=(-d,0,0),&\vec{R}_+&=(d,0,z)\\\vec{r}_-&=(d,0,0),&\vec{R}_-&=(-d,0,z)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The distances agree. Substitute $+Q$ and its displacement first.

#### 中文

两个距离相同。将正电荷 $+Q$ 及其位移代入。

#### Equation

$$
|\vec{R}_+|=|\vec{R}_-|=\sqrt{d^2+z^2},\qquad\vec{E}_+=\frac{Q(d\hat{x}+z\hat{z})}{4\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Include the negative charge sign explicitly; its $x$ component is positive and its $z$ component opposes that of $+Q$.

#### 中文

负电荷必须连同自身符号代入；其 $x$ 分量为正、$z$ 分量与正电荷的相反。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_-=\frac{(-Q)(-d\hat{x}+z\hat{z})}{4\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{Q(d\hat{x}-z\hat{z})}{4\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Add components: the $z$ components cancel and the $x$ components double.

#### 中文

逐分量相加：$z$ 分量抵消，$x$ 分量为两倍。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{Q[(d+d)\hat{x}+(z-z)\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Simplify to the requested Cartesian vector form.

#### 中文

化简为题目要求的笛卡尔坐标形式。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{Qd}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac{Qd}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

A point charge with charge $-Q$ is now placed at observation point $P$. What is the force on the point charge due to the two conducting spheres?

#### 中文

现在在观察点 $P$ 放置一个电荷量为 $-Q$ 的点电荷。两个导体球对该点电荷的作用力是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the force on a point charge, using the external field from the two source charges.

#### 中文

先写点电荷受力公式，使用前一问中两个源电荷产生的外场。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{F}=q_{\mathrm{test}}\vec{E}_{\mathrm{ext}}
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)

### Step 2

#### English

The inserted test charge is $-Q$; retain its sign rather than using only its magnitude.

#### 中文

题目放入的试探电荷是 $-Q$，不能只代入正的电荷大小。

#### Equation

$$
q_{\mathrm{test}}=-Q,\qquad\vec{E}_{\mathrm{ext}}=\frac{Qd}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Multiply explicitly to obtain $-Q^{2}$. Follow the specified external-field model without adding redistribution of the conducting-sphere charges.

#### 中文

完整相乘，电荷因子变为 $-Q^{2}$。按题目采用既定外场，不另计算试探电荷引起的球面电荷重分布。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{F}=(-Q)\frac{Qd}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The force points along $-x$.

#### 中文

力沿 $-x$ 方向。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{F}=-\frac{Q^2d}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{F}=-\frac{Q^2d}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\hat{x}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Set up an integral for calculating the potential difference between the origin $O=(0,0,0)$ and the observation point $P=(0,0,z)$. You do not need to solve it!

#### 中文

列出计算原点 $O=(0,0,0)$ 与观察点 $P=(0,0,z)$ 之间电势差的积分。不需要求解！

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the line integral for the potential difference between the endpoints.

#### 中文

先写两端点电势差的线积分原式。

#### Equation

$$
V(P)-V(O)=-\int_O^P\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Choose the straight $z$-axis path. Use $\zeta$ as the integration variable and reserve $z$ for the endpoint.

#### 中文

选择题目两点之间的 $z$ 轴直线路径，用 $\zeta$ 作为积分变量，$z$ 保留给终点。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{r}(\zeta)=(0,0,\zeta),\quad 0\le\zeta\le z,\quad d\vec{l}=\hat{z}\,d\zeta
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the field along the path, which has only an $x$ component. This already provides the complete integral requested.

#### 中文

把沿路径的电场代入；它始终只有 $x$ 分量。题目只要求列式，到这里即可给出完整积分。

#### Equation

$$
V(P)-V(O)=-\int_0^z\frac{Qd\hat{x}}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+\zeta^2)^{3/2}}\cdot\hat{z}\,d\zeta
$$

### Step 4

#### English

As an optional check, the path tangent is $\hat z$ while the field has only an $\hat x$ component. They are perpendicular, so the dot product vanishes at every point. The preceding integral already fully answers the question, which requests setup only.

#### 中文

额外核对：路径切向为 $\hat z$，而电场只有 $\hat x$ 分量。两者垂直，所以点积逐点为零。题目只要求列式，上一步的积分已经是完整作答。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\hat x\cdot\hat z&=0\\V(P)-V(O)&=-\int_0^z\frac{Qd(0)}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+\zeta^2)^{3/2}}\,d\zeta\\&=-\int_0^z0\,d\zeta=-[0\zeta]_0^z=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Answer

$$
V(P)-V(O)=-\int_0^z\frac{Qd\,\hat{x}\cdot\hat{z}}{2\pi\epsilon_0(d^2+\zeta^2)^{3/2}}\,d\zeta=0
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

In electrostatics, does the path of integration matter when calculating potential differences? Which of Maxwell’s equations for electrostatics tells us this?

#### 中文

静电学中，计算电势差时积分路径是否重要？静电学中的哪一个 Maxwell 方程说明了这一点？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the electrostatic Maxwell–Faraday equation: circulation around a closed path is zero.

#### 中文

先写静电场的 Maxwell–Faraday 方程：闭合路径上的环流为零。

#### Equation

$$
\oint\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}=0,\qquad\nabla\times\vec{E}=0
$$

#### References

- [conservative](formula:conservative)

### Step 2

#### English

Take two paths $C_{1}$ and $C_{2}$ from the same $A$ to the same $B$; reverse the second to form a closed loop.

#### 中文

取两条由同一起点 $A$ 到同一终点 $B$ 的路径 $C_{1}$、$C_{2}$，将第二条反向后闭合。

#### Equation

$$
0=\oint_{C_1-C_2}\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}=\int_{C_1}\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}-\int_{C_2}\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Rearrange to show equal path integrals, then apply the minus sign in the potential definition.

#### 中文

移项表明两条路径的积分相等，再乘电势定义中的负号。

#### Equation

$$
-\int_{C_1}\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}=-\int_{C_2}\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l}=V(B)-V(A)
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 4

#### English

The electrostatic potential difference therefore depends only on the endpoints; choose a path that simplifies the dot product.

#### 中文

因此静电电势差只由端点决定；允许选择使点积最简单的路径。

#### Equation

$$
\Delta V\ \text{is path independent}
$$

### Answer

$$
\Delta V\ \text{is path independent}
$$

### Answer text

#### English

It depends only on the endpoints.

#### 中文

不依赖路径，只依赖端点。

## Source correction

### English

Model limitation: real conducting spheres polarize one another, so their exact fields are not generally center-point-charge fields. This follows the exam model; it is a useful approximation when $a\ll d$.

### 中文

适用范围：两个真实导体球会相互极化，严格外场一般不等于球心点电荷叠加。这里沿用原卷模型；相距很远 $a\ll d$ 时可作近似。
