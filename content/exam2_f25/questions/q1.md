## Question

### English

In this problem, you will use Gauss’s law to solve for the electric field in each region.

The system below consists of:
• A sphere of constant volumetric charge density $\rho_0$ and radius $a$.
• A conducting shell of radius $b$ and surface charge density $\rho_{sb}$.

The total charge on the conducting shell is equal in magnitude to the total charge contained in the sphere, but opposite in sign. The region $a<r<b$ contains no free charge $\rho$ and the permittivity is $\varepsilon_0$.

Note: All diagrams must include the shape and position of the Gaussian surface used in your solution.

### 中文

本题将使用高斯定律，求各区域的电场。

下方系统由以下部分组成：
• 半径为 $a$、具有恒定体电荷密度 $\rho_0$ 的球体。
• 半径为 $b$、面电荷密度为 $\rho_{sb}$ 的导体壳。

导体壳的总电荷与球内总电荷大小相等、符号相反。区域 $a<r<b$ 中没有自由电荷 $\rho$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$。

注意：所有图都必须包含解题所用高斯面的形状和位置。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem uses spherical symmetry in Gauss's law and examines how dielectric material changes field and potential difference.

#### 中文

本题考查球对称高斯定律，以及改变介质后电场与电势差的变化。

### Givens

#### English

A sphere of radius $a$ has uniform charge density $\rho_0$. The conducting shell at radius $b$ carries the opposite total charge; the gap initially has permittivity $\varepsilon_0$.

#### 中文

半径 $a$ 的球有均匀体电荷密度 $\rho_0$；半径 $b$ 的导体壳带等量异号总电荷，初始夹层介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$。

### Goal

#### English

Choose Gaussian surfaces and find the field in three radial regions, then explain the change in potential difference for a higher-permittivity dielectric.

#### 中文

为三个径向区域分别选取高斯面并求电场，再解释填入更高介电常数材料后电势差如何变化。

### Approach

#### English

Determine the enclosed charge separately for each observation radius, then use spherical symmetry to simplify flux. Cancellation of the two total charges applies directly only to a surface enclosing both.

#### 中文

先按观察半径列出实际包围的电荷，再利用球面对称性简化通量。导体壳与球的总电荷相消，只能直接用于同时包围二者的高斯面。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Draw the diagram you will use for finding the electric field in the region $0<r<a$.

#### 中文

画出用于求区域 $0<r<a$ 内电场的示意图。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib equation: displacement flux through a closed surface equals its enclosed free charge.

#### 中文

先写公式表中的高斯定律：闭合面的电位移通量等于面内自由电荷。

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_S\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=\iiint_{\mathcal V}\rho\,d\mathcal V=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)

### Step 2

#### English

Spherical symmetry makes the field radial and constant at a fixed radius. Use $R$ for the fixed Gaussian radius, distinct from integration coordinates.

#### 中文

球对称使电场沿径向；同一半径上的场强相同。这里用 $R$ 表示固定的高斯面半径，避免和积分变量混淆。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(R)=E_r(R)\hat{r},\qquad \vec{D}(R)=\epsilon_0 E_r(R)\hat{r}
$$

#### References

- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 3

#### English

Draw the charged solid sphere of radius $a$ and the conducting shell of radius $b$, then a concentric dashed Gaussian sphere inside the solid sphere.

#### 中文

先画半径 $a$ 的带电实心球和半径 $b$ 的导体薄壳，再在实心球内画与二者同心的虚线球面。

#### Equation

$$
0<R<a<b,\qquad S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Start all three radius arrows $a$, $b$ and $R$ at the same center. End $R$ on the dashed surface and draw its outward normal from that surface.

#### 中文

在同一个球心上标出 $a$、$b$、$R$ 三根半径箭头；$R$ 箭头必须止于虚线面，法向从该面向外。

#### Equation

$$
d\vec{S}=\hat{r}\,R^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi,\quad 0\le\theta\le\pi,\quad 0\le\phi<2\pi
$$

#### References

- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 5

#### English

The closed surface encloses only the volume charge within $R$. The radii $a$ and $b$ are material boundaries, not the chosen Gaussian surface.

#### 中文

该闭合面只包围半径 $R$ 内的体电荷；$a$ 与 $b$ 是材料边界，不是此问选取的高斯面。

#### Equation

$$
S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R,\quad 0<R<a
$$

### Answer

$$
S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R,\quad 0<R<a
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field in the region $0<r<a$.

#### 中文

求区域 $0<r<a$ 内的电场。

### Step 1

#### English

Start from Gauss’s law and substitute the constitutive relation for $\vec D$.

#### 中文

从原始高斯定律开始，并用本构关系把 $\vec D$ 换成电场。

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_{S_R}\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}},\qquad \vec{D}=\epsilon_0\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

On the sphere of fixed radius $R$, substitute the radial field and spherical area element, retaining the dot product.

#### 中文

在半径固定为 $R$ 的球面上代入径向场和球坐标面积元，先保留点积。

#### Equation

$$
\Phi_D=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi [\epsilon_0E_r(R)\hat{r}]\cdot[\hat{r}R^2\sin\theta]\,d\theta\,d\phi
$$

#### References

- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 3

#### English

The unit normals have dot product one. The field is angle independent, so take it outside the integrals.

#### 中文

两个单位法向的点积为 1；场强不随角度变化，可移到积分号外。

#### Equation

$$
\Phi_D=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2\left(\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\right)\left(\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\right)
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Evaluate the azimuthal integral first. Its integrand is $1$, whose antiderivative is the angle itself; subtract the lower-limit value from the upper-limit value.

#### 中文

先算方位角积分。被积函数是 $1$，原函数就是方位角本身，代入上限再减下限。

#### Equation

$$
\int_0^{2\pi}1\,d\phi=[\phi]_0^{2\pi}=2\pi-0=2\pi
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Next evaluate the polar-angle integral. Since $\frac{d}{d\theta}(-\cos\theta)=\sin\theta$, an antiderivative is $-\cos\theta$. Subtracting the negative lower-limit value gives a plus sign.

#### 中文

再算极角积分。因为 $\frac{d}{d\theta}(-\cos\theta)=\sin\theta$，原函数是 $-\cos\theta$；减去下限处的负数会变成加号。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta&=[-\cos\theta]_0^\pi\\&=(-\cos\pi)-(-\cos0)\\&=-(-1)+1=2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Substitute both angular integrals into the flux expression. Their product is $4\pi$; the fixed surface radius and field remain outside the integrals.

#### 中文

把两个角积分代回通量式。它们的乘积为 $4\pi$；球面上的固定半径和场强始终在积分号外。

#### Equation

$$
\Phi_D=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2(2\pi)(2)=4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Compute enclosed charge separately. Density is constant and the radial limit is $R$; use $r'$ as the volume integration variable.

#### 中文

另算包围电荷：密度为常数，径向积分从 0 到高斯半径 $R$。用 $r'$ 作为体积分变量。

#### Equation

$$
Q_{\mathrm{enc}}=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\!\int_0^R \rho_0(r^\prime)^2\sin\theta\,dr^\prime d\theta d\phi
$$

#### References

- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 8

#### English

Take the constant density outside. The radial, polar-angle and azimuthal-angle bounds are independent, so the triple integral separates into three single integrals.

#### 中文

把不依赖积分变量的密度提出。径向、极角和方位角的积分范围彼此独立，所以可以写成三个单积分的乘积。

#### Equation

$$
Q_{\mathrm{enc}}=\rho_0\left(\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\right)\left(\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\right)\left(\int_0^R(r^\prime)^2\,dr^\prime\right)
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Use the power rule for the radial integral: add one to the exponent and divide by the new exponent. The integration variable is $r^\prime$; the fixed boundary radius is a limit, not an integration variable.

#### 中文

径向部分使用幂函数积分规则：指数加一，再除以新的指数。这里积分变量是 $r^\prime$，固定边界半径不会参与求原函数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^R(r^\prime)^2\,dr^\prime&=\left[\frac{(r^\prime)^3}{3}\right]_0^R\\&=\frac{R^3}{3}-\frac{0^3}{3}\\&=\frac{R^3}{3}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 10

#### English

Multiply the radial integral by the two angular integrals already evaluated. Charges outside this Gaussian surface are excluded.

#### 中文

把径向积分和两个已求出的角积分相乘。此高斯面以外的电荷不计入包围电荷。

#### Equation

$$
Q_{\mathrm{enc}}=\rho_0(2\pi)(2)\frac{R^3}{3}=\frac{4\pi\rho_0R^3}{3}
$$

### Step 11

#### English

Equate flux and enclosed charge, then divide by $4\pi\epsilon_{0}R^{2}$; $R^{3}/R^{2}$ leaves $R$.

#### 中文

令通量等于包围电荷，两边除以 $4\pi\epsilon_{0}R^{2}$；$R^{3}/R^{2}$ 留下 $R$。

#### Equation

$$
4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)=\frac{4\pi\rho_0R^3}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_r(R)=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{3\epsilon_0R^2}
$$

### Step 12

#### English

Relabel the arbitrary Gaussian radius $R$ as the observation radius $r$ and restore the radial unit vector.

#### 中文

最后把任意高斯半径 $R$ 改记为观察半径 $r$，并补回径向单位矢量。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{\rho_0 r}{3\epsilon_0}\hat{r}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{\rho_0 r}{3\epsilon_0}\hat{r}
$$

The surface normal remains outward. The zero field follows from charge cancellation in the next part, not from changing the surface geometry.

#### 中文

面法向仍向外。零场结论来自下一问的电荷相消，而不是改变高斯面形状。

#### Equation

$$
S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R,\quad R>b
$$

### Answer

## Part f

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field in the region $r>b$.

#### 中文

求区域 $r>b$ 内的电场。

### Step 1

#### English

Apply Gauss’s law outside the shell and substitute the vacuum constitutive relation.

#### 中文

在壳外同心球面应用高斯定律，并代入真空本构关系。

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_{S_R}\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}},\qquad\vec{D}=\epsilon_0\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

The exterior surface is still a concentric sphere. Evaluate the two angular antiderivatives at the same bounds as before, multiply them, and then multiply by the fixed factor $\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2$.

#### 中文

壳外仍是同心球面，两个端点代入与前面相同。写出两个角积分的原函数后相乘，再乘固定的 $\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Phi_D&=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2\left(\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\right)\left(\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\right)\\&=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2[\phi]_0^{2\pi}[-\cos\theta]_0^\pi\\&=\epsilon_0E_r(R)R^2(2\pi-0)[-(-1)-(-1)]\\&=4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Write the solid-sphere charge and the equal, opposite shell charge separately before adding them.

#### 中文

将内球体电荷与外壳等量异号的电荷分别写出，再相加。

#### Equation

$$
Q_a=\frac{4\pi a^3\rho_0}{3},\quad Q_b=-\frac{4\pi a^3\rho_0}{3},\quad Q_{\mathrm{enc}}=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute and divide by nonzero $4\pi\epsilon_{0}R^{2}$. With spherical symmetry, zero flux here implies zero field itself.

#### 中文

代入定律并除以非零的 $4\pi\epsilon_{0}R^{2}$。由球对称性，零总通量在这里推出场本身为零。

#### Equation

$$
4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_r(R)=0
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Restore vector notation and state the domain.

#### 中文

恢复矢量形式并注明适用区域。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\mathbf 0\quad(r>b)
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\mathbf 0\quad(r>b)
$$

## Part g

### Question

#### English

If you were to replace the material in the region $a<r<b$ by a dielectric with $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_0$, how would this affect the potential difference between $r=b$ and $r=a$? Explain this change in the context of electric materials and polarization.

#### 中文

若将区域 $a<r<b$ 中的材料替换为介电常数满足 $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_0$ 的电介质，$r=b$ 与 $r=a$ 之间的电势差会怎样变化？结合电介质材料和极化解释这一变化。

### Step 1

#### English

Before comparing potentials, write the crib potential integral and constitutive relation.

#### 中文

比较电势差前，先写原表的电势积分及本构关系。

#### Equation

$$
V(b)-V(a)=-\int_a^b\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l},\qquad\vec{D}=\epsilon\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

Replacing the dielectric leaves the specified free charges fixed, so $\vec D$ on a given Gaussian surface is set by the same enclosed charge.

#### 中文

更换介质时保持题设自由电荷不变，因此同一高斯面上的 $\vec D$ 仍由同一个包围电荷确定。

#### Equation

$$
D_r(r)=\frac{Q_a}{4\pi r^2}=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3r^2}
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)

### Step 3

#### English

Divide by the new and old permittivities to obtain the fields; $\vec D$ does not decrease along with $\vec E$.

#### 中文

分别除以新旧介电常数得到两个电场；不能把 $\vec D$ 和 $\vec E$ 同时按相同比例缩小。

#### Equation

$$
E_\epsilon(r)=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon r^2}=\frac{\epsilon_0}{\epsilon}E_{\mathrm{vac}}(r)
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Integrate radially from $a$ to $b$. For $V(a)-V(b)$, reverse the leading sign accordingly.

#### 中文

沿径向从 $a$ 到 $b$ 积分。若用 $V(a)-V(b)$，电势积分外侧负号相应反转。

#### Equation

$$
V(a)-V(b)=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\int_a^b r^{-2}\,dr
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Find the antiderivative first: increase the power from $-2$ to $-1$ and divide by $-1$, giving a negative reciprocal.

#### 中文

先求原函数：幂函数的指数从 $-2$ 变成 $-1$，再除以 $-1$，得到负的倒数。

#### Equation

$$
\int r^{-2}\,dr=\frac{r^{-1}}{-1}+C=-\frac1r+C
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The same integration constant cancels in a definite integral. Substitute upper minus lower, keeping the negative sign at the lower limit.

#### 中文

定积分中同一个积分常数会相消。按“上限减下限”代入，注意减去下限处的负号。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_a^b r^{-2}\,dr&=\left[-\frac1r\right]_a^b\\&=\left(-\frac1b\right)-\left(-\frac1a\right)\\&=\frac1a-\frac1b\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Multiply the definite-integral value by the field coefficient to obtain the potential difference before comparing with vacuum.

#### 中文

把定积分的值乘回场强中的常数系数，得到电势差，再与真空结果比较。

#### Equation

$$
V(a)-V(b)=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\left(\frac1a-\frac1b\right)
$$

### Step 8

#### English

The induced-dipole polarization response reduces the field for the same free charge. Compare magnitudes so the conclusion is independent of the sign of $\rho_{0}$.

#### 中文

诱导偶极的极化响应减小同一自由电荷产生的电场。取绝对值比较，结论不依赖 $\rho_{0}$ 的符号。

#### Equation

$$
|\Delta V_{\epsilon}|=\frac{\epsilon_0}{\epsilon}|\Delta V_{\mathrm{vac}}|<|\Delta V_{\mathrm{vac}}|
$$

### Answer

$$
|\Delta V_{\epsilon}|=\frac{\epsilon_0}{\epsilon}|\Delta V_{\mathrm{vac}}|<|\Delta V_{\mathrm{vac}}|
$$
