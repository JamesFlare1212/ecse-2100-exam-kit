## Question

### English

In this problem, you will use Gauss’s law to solve for the electric field in each region.

The system below consists of:
• A sphere of constant volumetric charge density $\rho_0$ and radius $a$.
• A conducting shell of radius $b$ and surface charge density $\rho_{sb}$.

The total charge on the conducting shell is equal in magnitude to the total charge contained in the sphere, but opposite in sign. The region $a<r<b$ contains no free charge $\rho$ and the permittivity is $\varepsilon_0$.

Note: All diagrams must include the shape and position of the Gaussian surface used in your solution.

### 中文

本题将使用高斯定律，求各区域的电场。

下方系统由以下部分组成：
• 半径为 $a$、具有恒定体电荷密度 $\rho_0$ 的球体。
• 半径为 $b$、面电荷密度为 $\rho_{sb}$ 的导体壳。

导体壳的总电荷与球内总电荷大小相等、符号相反。区域 $a<r<b$ 中没有自由电荷 $\rho$，其介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$。

注意：所有图都必须包含解题所用高斯面的形状和位置。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem uses spherical symmetry in Gauss's law and examines how dielectric material changes field and potential difference.

#### 中文

本题考查球对称高斯定律，以及改变介质后电场与电势差的变化。

### Givens

#### English

A sphere of radius $a$ has uniform charge density $\rho_0$. The conducting shell at radius $b$ carries the opposite total charge; the gap initially has permittivity $\varepsilon_0$.

#### 中文

半径 $a$ 的球有均匀体电荷密度 $\rho_0$；半径 $b$ 的导体壳带等量异号总电荷，初始夹层介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$。

### Goal

#### English

Choose Gaussian surfaces and find the field in three radial regions, then explain the change in potential difference for a higher-permittivity dielectric.

#### 中文

为三个径向区域分别选取高斯面并求电场，再解释填入更高介电常数材料后电势差如何变化。

### Approach

#### English

Determine the enclosed charge separately for each observation radius, then use spherical symmetry to simplify flux. Cancellation of the two total charges applies directly only to a surface enclosing both.

#### 中文

先按观察半径列出实际包围的电荷，再利用球面对称性简化通量。导体壳与球的总电荷相消，只能直接用于同时包围二者的高斯面。

### Step 1

#### English

Use [Gauss’s law](formula:gauss) from the crib sheet:

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_S\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=\iiint_{\mathcal V}\rho\,d\mathcal V=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given sphere radius $a$ and shell radius $b$, construct a Gaussian surface in the inner region. Choose a concentric sphere with $0<R<a$ and use the [constitutive relation](formula:constitutive) for the radial fields, obtain the fixed-radius area element from the [spherical volume element](formula:geometry), and mark the outward normal and concentric radii:

#### 中文

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \vec{E}(R)=E_r(R)\hat{r},\qquad \vec{D}(R)=\epsilon_0 E_r(R)\hat{r}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle 0<R<a<b,\qquad S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R\\[4pt]&\displaystyle d\vec{S}=\hat{r}\,R^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi,\quad 0\le\theta\le\pi,\quad 0\le\phi<2\pi\end{aligned}
$$

#### 中文

该面仅包围 $R$ 内的体电荷，得图示高斯面：

#### Equation

$$
S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R,\quad 0<R<a
$$

### Answer

$$
S_R:\ |\vec{x}|=R,\quad 0<R<a
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field in the region $0<r<a$.

#### 中文

求区域 $0<r<a$ 内的电场。

### Step 1

#### English

Use [Gauss’s law](formula:gauss) and the [constitutive relation](formula:constitutive) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[高斯定律](formula:gauss)与[本构关系](formula:constitutive)：

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_{S_R}\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}},\qquad \vec{D}=\epsilon_0\vec{E}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Given uniform volume density $\rho_0$, find the field for $0<r<a$. Choose $0<R<a$ and substitute the radial field and [spherical area element](formula:geometry) into the flux. At fixed $R$, $\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)$ is independent of the angles. Integrate in order using $\hat r\cdot\hat r=1$, $\cos\pi=-1$ and $\cos0=1$:

#### 中文

已知内球体密度 $\rho_0$，求 $0<r<a$ 的电场。取 $0<R<a$，把径向场和[球面面积元](formula:geometry)代入通量。固定 $R$ 时，$\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)$ 与角度无关；用 $\hat r\cdot\hat r=1$、$\cos\pi=-1$、$\cos0=1$ 逐层积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_D&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi(\epsilon_0E_r(R)\hat r)\cdot(\hat r R^2\sin\theta)\,d\theta d\phi\\
&=\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi(\hat r\cdot\hat r)\sin\theta\,d\theta d\phi\\
&=\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\int_0^{2\pi}\left(\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\right)d\phi\\
&=\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\int_0^{2\pi}[-\cos\theta]_0^\pi\,d\phi\\
&=\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\int_0^{2\pi}[(-\cos\pi)-(-\cos0)]\,d\phi\\
&=\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)\int_0^{2\pi}2\,d\phi\\
&=2\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)[\phi]_0^{2\pi}\\
&=2\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)(2\pi-0)\\
&=4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $\rho_0$ and the [spherical volume element](formula:geometry) from the crib sheet into the enclosed-charge integral. Since $\rho_0$ is constant, integrate first over $r^\prime$, then over $\theta$ and $\phi$:

#### 中文

把 $\rho_0$ 和 crib sheet 的[球坐标体积元](formula:geometry)代入包围电荷积分。$\rho_0$ 为常数，先算 $r^\prime$ 的积分，再算 $\theta$ 和 $\phi$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{\mathrm{enc}}&=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\!\int_0^R\rho_0(r^\prime)^2\sin\theta\,dr^\prime d\theta d\phi\\
&=\rho_0\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\left(\int_0^R(r^\prime)^2\,dr^\prime\right)d\theta d\phi\\
&=\rho_0\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\left[\frac{(r^\prime)^3}{3}\right]_0^R\,d\theta d\phi\\
&=\rho_0\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\left(\frac{R^3}{3}-\frac{0^3}{3}\right)d\theta d\phi\\
&=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{3}\int_0^{2\pi}[-\cos\theta]_0^\pi\,d\phi\\
&=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{3}\int_0^{2\pi}[(-\cos\pi)-(-\cos0)]\,d\phi\\
&=\frac{2\rho_0R^3}{3}[\phi]_0^{2\pi}\\
&=\frac{2\rho_0R^3}{3}(2\pi-0)=\frac{4\pi\rho_0R^3}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute the flux and charge into Gauss’s law and write $R$ as $r$ to obtain

#### 中文

把通量与电荷代入高斯定律，将 $R$ 写作 $r$，得

#### Equation

$$
4\pi\epsilon_0R^2E_r(R)=\frac{4\pi\rho_0R^3}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_r(R)=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{3\epsilon_0R^2}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{\rho_0 r}{3\epsilon_0}\hat{r}
$$

## Part g

### Question

#### English

If you were to replace the material in the region $a<r<b$ by a dielectric with $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_0$, how would this affect the potential difference between $r=b$ and $r=a$? Explain this change in the context of electric materials and polarization.

#### 中文

若将区域 $a<r<b$ 中的材料替换为介电常数满足 $\varepsilon>\varepsilon_0$ 的电介质，$r=b$ 与 $r=a$ 之间的电势差会怎样变化？结合电介质材料和极化解释这一变化。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [potential-difference](formula:potential) and [constitutive relations](formula:constitutive) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电势差](formula:potential)与[本构关系](formula:constitutive)：

#### Equation

$$
V(b)-V(a)=-\int_a^b\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l},\qquad\vec{D}=\epsilon\vec{E}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Given unchanged free charge and $\epsilon>\epsilon_0$, determine the change in potential difference. [Gauss’s law](formula:gauss) fixes $D_r$; divide it by the new permittivity:

#### 中文

已知自由电荷不变、$\epsilon>\epsilon_0$，求电势差如何变化。用[高斯定律](formula:gauss)保持 $D_r$ 不变，再除以新介电常数：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle D_r(r)=\frac{Q_a}{4\pi r^2}=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3r^2}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle E_\epsilon(r)=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon r^2}=\frac{\epsilon_0}{\epsilon}E_{\mathrm{vac}}(r)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the new field into the radial potential integral:

#### 中文

把新电场代入径向电势积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
V(a)-V(b)&=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\int_a^b r^{-2}\,dr\\
&=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\left[-\frac1r\right]_a^b\\
&=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\left[\left(-\frac1b\right)-\left(-\frac1a\right)\right]\\
&=\frac{\rho_0a^3}{3\epsilon}\left(\frac1a-\frac1b\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Polarization reduces the field and hence the magnitude of the potential difference:

#### 中文

极化响应减小电场，故电势差的大小也减小，得

## Source correction

### English

The field jumps at the conducting shell. Interpret the $a\le r\le b$ expression as a dielectric-side limit, not the field inside the conductor.

### 中文

场在导体薄壳处有跳变：$a\le r\le b$ 的公式应理解为介质内极限，不能把介质中的场值当成导体内的场。
