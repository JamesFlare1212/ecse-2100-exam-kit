## Question

### English

The coaxial cable below consists of a grounded, inner conductor of radius $a$ and an outer conducting shell of radius $b$, which is held at a constant potential $-V_0$. The space between the conductors $a<r<b$ has no free charge and is filled by a dielectric with permittivity $\varepsilon$.

### 中文

下方同轴电缆由一个半径为 $a$ 的接地内导体，以及一个半径为 $b$、保持恒定电势 $-V_0$ 的外导体壳组成。导体之间的区域 $a<r<b$ 没有自由电荷，并填充了介电常数为 $\varepsilon$ 的电介质。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem solves a cylindrically symmetric Laplace equation, then obtains surface charge and capacitance from the potential.

#### 中文

本题考查柱对称 Laplace 方程，从电势边界条件一路求到表面电荷与电容。

### Givens

#### English

The coaxial conductors have radii $a$ and $b$, with $V(a)=0$ and $V(b)=-V_0$. The gap has permittivity $\varepsilon$ and no free volume charge.

#### 中文

同轴内、外导体半径为 $a$、$b$，有 $V(a)=0$、$V(b)=-V_0$；介质介电常数为 $\varepsilon$，间隙内没有自由体电荷。

### Goal

#### English

Find potential, electric displacement, both conductor surface-charge densities and capacitance per unit length.

#### 中文

求间隙内电势、电位移、两导体的表面电荷密度和单位长度电容。

### Approach

#### English

Use symmetry to make potential a function of radius, then determine integration constants from both boundary potentials. Differentiate to obtain the field and check the outward normal separately at each conductor.

#### 中文

先用对称性把电势写成半径的函数，再用两个电势条件确定积分常数。由电势求场后，分别检查内、外导体表面法向的符号。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Use Laplace’s or Poisson’s equation to find $V(\vec r)$ in the region $a\le r\le b$.

#### 中文

使用 Laplace 方程或 Poisson 方程，求区域 $a\le r\le b$ 内的 $V(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use [Laplace’s equation](formula:laplace) from the crib sheet; with no free volume charge, [Poisson’s equation](formula:poisson) reduces to:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的 [Laplace 方程](formula:laplace)；无自由体电荷时 [Poisson 方程](formula:poisson)化为此式：

#### Equation

$$
\nabla^2V=0
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $V(a)=0$, $V(b)=-V_0$ and cylindrical symmetry, find $V(r)$. Retain the radial term and integrate twice:

#### 中文

已知 $V(a)=0$、$V(b)=-V_0$ 且柱对称，求 $V(r)$。保留径向项并积分两次：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac1r\frac d{dr}\left(r\frac{dV}{dr}\right)&=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r\frac{dV}{dr}=A,\\V(r)&=A\int_a^r\frac1s\,ds+B\\&=A[\ln(s/a)]_a^r+B=A\ln(r/a)+B\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the inner and outer conductor potentials into the general solution to determine $B$ and $A$:

#### 中文

把内、外导体的边界电位代入通解，确定 $B$、$A$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V(a)=A\ln1+B=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad B=0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V(b)=A\ln(b/a)=-V_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A=-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substituting both constants gives

#### 中文

把两个常数代回，得

#### Equation

$$
V(r)=-V_0\frac{\ln(r/a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
V(r)=-V_0\frac{\ln(r/a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the displacement field $\vec D(\vec r)$ in the region $a\le r\le b$.

#### 中文

求区域 $a\le r\le b$ 内的电位移场 $\vec D(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [potential-gradient](formula:gradient) and [constitutive relations](formula:constitutive) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电势梯度](formula:gradient)与[本构关系](formula:constitutive)：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=-\nabla V,\qquad \vec{D}=\epsilon\vec{E}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $V(r)$ from (a), find $\vec D(r)$. Substitute the potential into the radial derivative, using $d\ln(r/a)/dr=1/r$:

#### 中文

已知 (a) 的 $V(r)$，求 $\vec D(r)$。把电势代入径向导数，其中 $d\ln(r/a)/dr=1/r$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \vec{E}=-\frac d{dr}\left[-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\ln(r/a)\right]\hat{r}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}\vec E&=-\left[-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\right]\frac1r\hat r\\&=\frac{V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat r\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Multiplying by $\epsilon$ gives the displacement, directed along $+\hat r$ for $V_0>0$:

#### 中文

再乘 $\epsilon$，得；$V_0>0$ 时方向为 $+\hat r$。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{D}(r)=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat{r}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{D}(r)=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat{r}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the charge density on the inner and outer conductors.

#### 中文

求内导体和外导体上的电荷密度。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [normal-displacement jump](formula:boundary) from the crib sheet; the field inside a conductor is zero:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[法向电位移跳变](formula:boundary)；导体内场为零：

#### Equation

$$
D_{1n}-D_{2n}=\rho_s,\qquad \rho_s=\vec{D}_{\mathrm{dielectric}}\cdot\hat{n}_{\mathrm{out}}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $\vec D(r)$ from (b), find both surface charge densities. At $a^+$ and $b^-$, take the dot product with each conductor’s outward normal into the dielectric:

#### 中文

已知 (b) 的 $\vec D(r)$，求两导体面电荷密度。把 $a^+$、$b^-$ 的电位移分别与导体朝向介质的外法向点乘：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \hat{n}_a=+\hat{r},\qquad \rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}(\hat{r}\cdot\hat{r})\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \hat{n}_b=-\hat{r},\qquad \rho_{sb}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}(\hat{r}\cdot[-\hat{r}])\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives equal and opposite charge per unit length, with different densities on the two radii:

#### 中文

得；两表面每单位长度电荷等量异号，密度按各自半径不同。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)},\quad\rho_{sb}=-\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)},\quad\rho_{sb}=-\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Find the capacitance per unit length of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

求同轴电缆的单位长度电容。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [capacitance](formula:capacitance) and [potential-difference definitions](formula:potential) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电容](formula:capacitance)与[电势差](formula:potential)定义：

#### Equation

$$
C=\frac Q{\Delta V},\qquad\Delta V=V(a)-V(b)=V_0
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $\rho_{sa}$ from (c) and potential difference $V_0$, find the capacitance per unit length. Substitute the surface density into the inner-conductor charge integral:

#### 中文

已知 (c) 的 $\rho_{sa}$ 和电势差 $V_0$，求单位长度电容。把面密度代入内导体表面积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Q&=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\rho_{sa}a\,d\phi\,dz\\&=\rho_{sa}a[\phi]_0^{2\pi}[z]_0^\ell=2\pi a\ell\rho_{sa}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $\rho_{sa}$ and divide by the length and potential difference:

#### 中文

把 $\rho_{sa}$ 代入，除以长度和电势差：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Q^\prime=\frac Q\ell=2\pi a\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle C^\prime=\frac{Q^\prime}{V_0}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon V_0}{V_0\ln(b/a)}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$

### Answer

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$
