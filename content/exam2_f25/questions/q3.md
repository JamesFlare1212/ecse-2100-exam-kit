## Question

### English

The coaxial cable below consists of a grounded, inner conductor of radius $a$ and an outer conducting shell of radius $b$, which is held at a constant potential $-V_0$. The space between the conductors $a<r<b$ has no free charge and is filled by a dielectric with permittivity $\varepsilon$.

### 中文

下方同轴电缆由一个半径为 $a$ 的接地内导体，以及一个半径为 $b$、保持恒定电势 $-V_0$ 的外导体壳组成。导体之间的区域 $a<r<b$ 没有自由电荷，并填充了介电常数为 $\varepsilon$ 的电介质。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem solves a cylindrically symmetric Laplace equation, then obtains surface charge and capacitance from the potential.

#### 中文

本题考查柱对称 Laplace 方程，从电势边界条件一路求到表面电荷与电容。

### Givens

#### English

The coaxial conductors have radii $a$ and $b$, with $V(a)=0$ and $V(b)=-V_0$. The gap has permittivity $\varepsilon$ and no free volume charge.

#### 中文

同轴内、外导体半径为 $a$、$b$，有 $V(a)=0$、$V(b)=-V_0$；介质介电常数为 $\varepsilon$，间隙内没有自由体电荷。

### Goal

#### English

Find potential, electric displacement, both conductor surface-charge densities and capacitance per unit length.

#### 中文

求间隙内电势、电位移、两导体的表面电荷密度和单位长度电容。

### Approach

#### English

Use symmetry to make potential a function of radius, then determine integration constants from both boundary potentials. Differentiate to obtain the field and check the outward normal separately at each conductor.

#### 中文

先用对称性把电势写成半径的函数，再用两个电势条件确定积分常数。由电势求场后，分别检查内、外导体表面法向的符号。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Use Laplace’s or Poisson’s equation to find $V(\vec r)$ in the region $a\le r\le b$.

#### 中文

使用 Laplace 方程或 Poisson 方程，求区域 $a\le r\le b$ 内的 $V(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib Laplace equation. With no free volume charge, the right side of Poisson’s equation vanishes.

#### 中文

先写原表的 Laplace 方程。介质中没有自由体电荷，所以 Poisson 方程的右侧为零。

#### Equation

$$
\nabla^2V=0
$$

#### References

- [laplace](formula:laplace)
- [poisson](formula:poisson)

### Step 2

#### English

Neglecting end effects, cylindrical symmetry makes $V$ depend only on $r$; the $\phi$ and $z$ derivative terms vanish.

#### 中文

忽略端部效应，柱对称使电位只依赖 $r$，$\phi$ 与 $z$ 的导数项都为零。

#### Equation

$$
\nabla^2V=\frac1r\frac{d}{dr}\left(r\frac{dV}{dr}\right)=0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Since $r>0$, multiply both sides by $r$. Treat $r\,dV/dr$ as one quantity: its derivative is zero, so that quantity is a constant.

#### 中文

因为 $r>0$，两边先乘 $r$。把 $r\,dV/dr$ 暂时看成一个整体：这个整体的导数为零，所以它等于一个常数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r\frac{dV}{dr}\right)&=0\\\int\frac{d}{dr}\left(r\frac{dV}{dr}\right)dr&=\int0\,dr\\r\frac{dV}{dr}&=A\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Divide by $r$ to isolate the potential derivative. Use $s$ for the second integration so it is distinct from the observation radius $r$.

#### 中文

再除以 $r$，将电势对半径的导数单独留在左边。用 $s$ 作为第二次积分的变量，避免与观察点 $r$ 混淆。

#### Equation

$$
\frac{dV}{dr}=\frac Ar,\qquad V(r)-V(a)=\int_a^r\frac As\,ds
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Take the constant $A$ outside. For positive radii, an antiderivative of $1/s$ is a logarithm. The definite integral gives a difference of logarithms, which combines into the logarithm of a dimensionless ratio.

#### 中文

常数 $A$ 提到积分号外。正半径区间内，$1/s$ 的原函数是对数；定积分给出两个对数之差，并可合并为无量纲比值的对数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(r)-V(a)&=A\int_a^r\frac1s\,ds\\&=A[\ln(s/a)]_a^r\\&=A[\ln(r/a)-\ln(a/a)]\\&=A\ln(r/a)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Write the as-yet unspecified inner-conductor potential as a constant $B$. This gives a general solution containing two constants, to be fixed by the boundary conditions.

#### 中文

把内导体处尚待确定的电势记为常数 $B$，写成含两个常数的通解，再逐一使用边界条件。

#### Equation

$$
B=V(a),\qquad V(r)=A\ln(r/a)+B
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Apply the grounded inner-conductor boundary first to determine $B$.

#### 中文

先代入内导体接地边界，解出 $B$。

#### Equation

$$
V(a)=A\ln1+B=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad B=0
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Apply the outer-conductor potential next to determine $A$.

#### 中文

再代入外导体电位，解出 $A$。

#### Equation

$$
V(b)=A\ln(b/a)=-V_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A=-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Substitute both constants into the general solution; the boundary values are 0 at $a$ and $-V_0$ at $b$.

#### 中文

把两个常数代回通解；在 $a$ 和 $b$ 分别得到 0 与 $-V_{0}$。

#### Equation

$$
V(r)=-V_0\frac{\ln(r/a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
V(r)=-V_0\frac{\ln(r/a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the displacement field $\vec D(\vec r)$ in the region $a\le r\le b$.

#### 中文

求区域 $a\le r\le b$ 内的电位移场 $\vec D(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib gradient and constitutive relations.

#### 中文

先写原表的梯度关系与本构关系。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=-\nabla V,\qquad \vec{D}=\epsilon\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [gradient](formula:gradient)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

The potential depends only on $r$, so retain only the radial gradient component.

#### 中文

电位只依赖 $r$，梯度只保留径向分量。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=-\frac{dV}{dr}\hat{r}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the preceding $V(r)$, keeping the leading minus sign.

#### 中文

把上问的 $V(r)$ 代入，保留外侧负号。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=-\frac d{dr}\left[-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\ln(r/a)\right]\hat{r}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Apply the chain rule to the composite logarithm. Since $a$ is a fixed radius, the derivative of $r/a$ is $1/a$.

#### 中文

对复合函数使用链式法则：先对对数内部的量求导。因为 $a$ 是固定半径，$r/a$ 的导数为 $1/a$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac d{dr}\ln(r/a)&=\frac1{r/a}\frac d{dr}(r/a)\\&=\frac ar\frac1a\\&=\frac1r\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The minus sign in the potential and the minus sign in the electric-field definition multiply to a plus sign; then insert the derivative just calculated.

#### 中文

电势中的负号与电场定义前面的负号相乘为正，再乘刚求出的导数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\vec E&=-\left[-\frac{V_0}{\ln(b/a)}\right]\frac1r\hat r\\&=\frac{V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat r\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Multiply by permittivity to obtain $\vec D$, pointing from the inner toward the outer conductor when $V_{0}>0$.

#### 中文

最后乘以介电常数，得到 $\vec D$；$V_{0}>0$ 时方向由内导体指向外导体。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{D}(r)=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat{r}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{D}(r)=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{r\ln(b/a)}\hat{r}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the charge density on the inner and outer conductors.

#### 中文

求内导体和外导体上的电荷密度。

### Step 1

#### English

Start with the normal-$\vec D$ jump condition. The electrostatic field inside a conductor is zero, leaving the dielectric-side normal $\vec D$.

#### 中文

从法向 $\vec D$ 的跳变条件开始。导体内部静电场为零，因此表面电荷由介质侧的法向 $\vec D$ 决定。

#### Equation

$$
D_{1n}-D_{2n}=\rho_s,\qquad \rho_s=\vec{D}_{\mathrm{dielectric}}\cdot\hat{n}_{\mathrm{out}}
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)

### Step 2

#### English

The inner conductor’s normal into the dielectric is $+\hat{r}$. Evaluate the preceding $\vec D$ at $a^{+}$.

#### 中文

内导体朝向介质的外法向为 $+\hat{r}$；将上问的 $\vec D$ 取在 $a^{+}$。

#### Equation

$$
\hat{n}_a=+\hat{r},\qquad \rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}(\hat{r}\cdot\hat{r})
$$

### Step 3

#### English

At the outer conductor’s inner surface the normal into the dielectric is $-\hat{r}$. Evaluating at $b^{-}$ therefore introduces a minus sign.

#### 中文

外导体的内表面朝向介质的法向为 $-\hat{r}$；取 $b^{-}$ 后点积产生负号。

#### Equation

$$
\hat{n}_b=-\hat{r},\qquad \rho_{sb}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}(\hat{r}\cdot[-\hat{r}])
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Check that the charges per unit length cancel; the two surface densities themselves need not have equal magnitudes.

#### 中文

作为核对，两柱面的每单位长度电荷相加为零，而不是两处面密度数值相反。

#### Equation

$$
2\pi a\rho_{sa}+2\pi b\rho_{sb}=0
$$

### Step 5

#### English

State both surface charge densities, each in $\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{m}^{2}$.

#### 中文

写出两个表面电荷密度，单位均为 $\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{m}^{2}$。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)},\quad\rho_{sb}=-\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)},\quad\rho_{sb}=-\frac{\epsilon V_0}{b\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Find the capacitance per unit length of the coaxial cable.

#### 中文

求同轴电缆的单位长度电容。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the capacitance definition, using the potential difference between conductors.

#### 中文

先写电容定义，并明确分母是两导体之间的电势差。

#### Equation

$$
C=\frac Q{\Delta V},\qquad\Delta V=V(a)-V(b)=V_0
$$

#### References

- [capacitance](formula:capacitance)
- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Integrate the inner-conductor surface charge first. Its area element uses the inner-conductor radius $a$; both the surface density and radius are constant.

#### 中文

先积分内导体表面电荷，面积元必须用内导体半径 $a$。均匀面密度与半径都是常数。

#### Equation

$$
Q=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\rho_{sa}a\,d\phi\,dz
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Do the angular integral first, showing its antiderivative and endpoints. Then insert its result as a constant in the length integral.

#### 中文

先做方位角积分，写出原函数及端点；再把结果作为常数放入长度方向的积分。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^{2\pi}\rho_{sa}a\,d\phi&=\rho_{sa}a[\phi]_0^{2\pi}\\&=2\pi a\rho_{sa}\\Q&=\int_0^\ell2\pi a\rho_{sa}\,dz\\&=2\pi a\rho_{sa}[z]_0^\ell\\&=2\pi a\rho_{sa}(\ell-0)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute the surface density and divide by length to obtain charge per unit length.

#### 中文

代入上问的面密度，再除以长度，得到每单位长度电荷。

#### Equation

$$
Q^\prime=\frac Q\ell=2\pi a\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon V_0}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Use the same definition per unit length and cancel $V_{0}$.

#### 中文

对每单位长度使用同一电容定义，约去电压 $V_{0}$。

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac{Q^\prime}{V_0}=\frac{2\pi\epsilon V_0}{V_0\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The resulting dimension is $\mathrm{F}/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

所得量纲为 $\mathrm{F}/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$

### Answer

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$
