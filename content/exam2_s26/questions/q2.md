## Question

### English

Shown below is a stack of three dielectric materials:
• Dielectric 1: occupies the region $y>0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_1=2\varepsilon_0$
• Dielectric 2: occupies the region $0<y<0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_2=5\varepsilon_0$
• Dielectric 3: occupies the region $y<0$ and has permittivity $\varepsilon_3=2\varepsilon_0$

The electric field in dielectric 1 is given by:
$$\vec E_1=100\hat x-100\hat y$$

### 中文

下图是一组由三种电介质组成的叠层：
• 电介质 1：占据 $y>0.01\,\mathrm{m}$，介电常数为 $\varepsilon_1=2\varepsilon_0$
• 电介质 2：占据 $0<y<0.01\,\mathrm{m}$，介电常数为 $\varepsilon_2=5\varepsilon_0$
• 电介质 3：占据 $y<0$，介电常数为 $\varepsilon_3=2\varepsilon_0$

电介质 1 中的电场给定为：
$$\vec E_1=100\hat x-100\hat y$$

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem applies tangential-field and normal-displacement boundary conditions to layered dielectrics and interprets them through polarization.

#### 中文

本题考查分层介质中切向电场与法向电位移的边界条件，以及介质极化的解释。

### Givens

#### English

The layers have permittivities $2\varepsilon_0$, $5\varepsilon_0$ and $2\varepsilon_0$, with a $0.01\,\mathrm m$ middle layer. The first-layer field is $\vec E_1=100\hat x-100\hat y$.

#### 中文

三层介电常数依次为 $2\varepsilon_0$、$5\varepsilon_0$、$2\varepsilon_0$，中间层厚 $0.01\,\mathrm m$；已给出第一层的 $\vec E_1=100\hat x-100\hat y$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the fields in layers two and three and the voltage across the middle layer, then explain the field-magnitude differences through polarization.

#### 中文

求第二、第三层电场和中间层两端的电势差，并用极化解释电场大小的差异。

### Approach

#### English

Split the field into tangential and normal components. At a dielectric interface without free surface charge, preserve tangential electric field and normal displacement, then integrate along the normal to obtain the voltage.

#### 中文

先按界面方向把电场分为切向与法向。在无自由面电荷的介质界面，分别使用切向电场连续和法向电位移连续，再沿法向路径计算电势差。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field in dielectric 2, $\vec E_2$.

#### 中文

求电介质 2 中的电场 $\vec E_2$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with both crib electrostatic boundary conditions and the constitutive relation.

#### 中文

先写原表的两条静电边界条件和本构关系。

#### Equation

$$
D_{1n}-D_{2n}=\rho_s,\quad E_{1t}=E_{2t},\quad\vec{D}_i=\epsilon_i\vec{E}_i
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

The interface is $y=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$, with normal along $y$ and tangent along $x$. Decompose the given field.

#### 中文

分界面是 $y=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$，法向沿 $y$、切向沿 $x$。先将已知电场分解。

#### Equation

$$
E_{1t}=E_{1x}=100\,\mathrm{V/m},\quad E_{1n}=E_{1y}=-100\,\mathrm{V/m},\quad E_{1z}=0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Tangential $\vec E$ is continuous, so the $x$ component carries across and the $z$ component remains zero.

#### 中文

切向 $\vec E$ 连续，因此 $x$ 分量直接传递，$z$ 分量仍为零。

#### Equation

$$
E_{2x}=E_{1x}=100\,\mathrm{V/m},\qquad E_{2z}=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

There is no free surface charge. Set the normal-$\vec D$ jump to zero and solve for $E_{2y}$.

#### 中文

无自由面电荷，令法向 $\vec D$ 的跳变为零，再解出 $E_{2y}$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\epsilon_1E_{1y}-\epsilon_2E_{2y}&=0\\\epsilon_1E_{1y}&=\epsilon_2E_{2y}\\E_{2y}&=\frac{\epsilon_1E_{1y}}{\epsilon_2}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Insert both permittivities, cancel $\epsilon_{0}$ and retain the negative $y$ component.

#### 中文

代入两个介电常数，约去 $\epsilon_{0}$，保留已知 $y$ 分量的负号。

#### Equation

$$
E_{2y}=\frac{2\epsilon_0}{5\epsilon_0}(-100)=-40\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Combine all three components into the vector.

#### 中文

把三个分量合成为矢量。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_2=(100\hat{x}-40\hat{y})\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_2=(100\hat{x}-40\hat{y})\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the electric field in dielectric 3, $\vec E_3$.

#### 中文

求电介质 3 中的电场 $\vec E_3$。

### Step 1

#### English

At the second dielectric interface $y=0$, again use continuity of tangential $\vec E$ and normal $\vec D$.

#### 中文

在 $y=0$ 的第二个介质界面再次使用切向 $\vec E$ 连续和法向 $\vec D$ 连续。

#### Equation

$$
E_{2t}=E_{3t},\qquad D_{2n}-D_{3n}=0
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)

### Step 2

#### English

The preceding result gives $E_{2x}=100$ and $E_{2y}=-40$; $y$ remains normal and $x$ tangential.

#### 中文

上问给出 $E_{2x}=100$、$E_{2y}=-40$，法向和切向仍分别为 $y$ 与 $x$。

#### Equation

$$
E_{3x}=E_{2x}=100\,\mathrm{V/m},\qquad E_{3z}=0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the constitutive relation and isolate $E_{3y}$.

#### 中文

先代入本构关系，再把 $E_{3y}$ 单独移到一侧。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\epsilon_2E_{2y}&=\epsilon_3E_{3y}\\\frac{\epsilon_2E_{2y}}{\epsilon_3}&=\frac{\epsilon_3E_{3y}}{\epsilon_3}\\E_{3y}&=\frac{\epsilon_2}{\epsilon_3}E_{2y}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute $\epsilon_{2}=5\epsilon_{0}$, $\epsilon_{3}=2\epsilon_{0}$ and $-40\,\mathrm{V}/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

代入 $\epsilon_{2}=5\epsilon_{0}$、$\epsilon_{3}=2\epsilon_{0}$ 和 $-40\,\mathrm{V}/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
E_{3y}=\frac{5\epsilon_0}{2\epsilon_0}(-40)=-100\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Restore the complete vector. Since $\epsilon_{3}=\epsilon_{1}$, the field agrees with region 1.

#### 中文

恢复完整矢量。因为 $\epsilon_{3}=\epsilon_{1}$，最终场与区域 1 一致。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_3=(100\hat{x}-100\hat{y})\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_3=(100\hat{x}-100\hat{y})\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the total potential difference across dielectric 2, $V_0$, (i.e. between $y=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ and $y=0$).

#### 中文

计算电介质 2 两端的总电势差 $V_0$（即 $y=0.01\,\mathrm{m}$ 与 $y=0$ 之间）。

### Step 1

#### English

Specify the order of the potential difference before writing the crib negative line integral.

#### 中文

先明确电势差的次序，再写原表的负线积分。

#### Equation

$$
V_0=V(y=0.01)-V(y=0)=-\int_{y=0}^{y=0.01}\vec{E}_2\cdot d\vec{l}
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Choose a straight path at fixed $x$ and $z$, leaving only a $dy$ component.

#### 中文

选 $x$、$z$ 不变的直线路径，仅有 $dy$ 分量。

#### Equation

$$
d\vec{l}=\hat{y}\,dy,\qquad \vec{E}_2=(100\hat{x}-40\hat{y})\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Expand the dot product: $\hat x\cdot\hat y=0$ and $\hat y\cdot\hat y=1$, so the tangential $100\,\mathrm{V}/\mathrm{m}$ does not contribute.

#### 中文

展开点积，$\hat x\cdot\hat y=0$，$\hat y\cdot\hat y=1$，因此切向的 $100\,\mathrm{V}/\mathrm{m}$ 不参与本次电势差。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_2\cdot d\vec{l}=[100(0)-40(1)]\,dy=-40\,dy
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Handle the leading minus sign first. The normal field component is negative, so the potential-definition minus sign makes the integrand positive. Keep units in the path limits.

#### 中文

先处理积分前的负号。电场法向分量为负，因此电势定义的负号与它相乘为正；长度变量仍用带单位的上下限。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V_0&=-\int_0^{0.01\,\mathrm m}(-40\,\mathrm{V/m})\,dy\\&=(40\,\mathrm{V/m})\int_0^{0.01\,\mathrm m}1\,dy\\&=(40\,\mathrm{V/m})[y]_0^{0.01\,\mathrm m}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Explicitly subtract the lower-limit value from the upper-limit value. Meters cancel the per-meter factor, leaving volts.

#### 中文

明确代入上限减下限。米与每米相消，最后的单位为伏特。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V_0&=(40\,\mathrm{V/m})(0.01\,\mathrm m-0\,\mathrm m)\\&=(40)(0.01)\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The requested difference is $+0.40\,\mathrm{V}$, meaning the upper surface is at higher potential.

#### 中文

所求电势差为 $+0.40\,\mathrm{V}$，表示上表面的电位较高。

#### Equation

$$
V_0=+0.40\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_0=+0.40\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Using the concept of polarization in dielectric materials, briefly explain why the magnitude of the electric field in dielectric 2 is different from the magnitudes of the electric fields in dielectric 1 and dielectric 3. Be sure to comment on the relative magnitudes: why is one larger/smaller than the others?

#### 中文

利用电介质材料极化的概念，简要解释为什么电介质 2 中的电场大小与电介质 1 和电介质 3 中的电场大小不同。必须讨论相对大小：为什么其中一个比另外的更大或更小？

### Step 1

#### English

Start from the boundary conditions and $\vec D=\epsilon\vec E$. The entire field does not scale uniformly as $1/\epsilon$.

#### 中文

用边界条件和 $\vec D=\epsilon\vec E$ 作为解释起点；不是把全部电场都按 $1/\epsilon$ 缩放。

#### Equation

$$
D_{1n}=D_{2n}=D_{3n},\qquad E_{1t}=E_{2t}=E_{3t}
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

For the same normal $\vec D$, region 2 has the smallest normal $\vec E$ because its permittivity is largest.

#### 中文

相同法向 $\vec D$ 下，介电常数最大的区域 2 具有最小的法向 $\vec E$。

#### Equation

$$
E_{2n}=\frac{D_n}{5\epsilon_0},\qquad E_{1n}=E_{3n}=\frac{D_n}{2\epsilon_0}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Polarization of material dipoles reduces the normal net field for the given free charge, while tangential continuity constrains the tangential field.

#### 中文

材料中的电偶极被极化，其响应使给定自由电荷产生的法向净场减弱；切向场仍由连续条件限定。

#### Equation

$$
|E_{2n}|=40<100=|E_{1n}|=|E_{3n}|\quad[\mathrm{V/m}]
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Compare total field magnitudes using the sum of squared components, rather than the $y$ component alone.

#### 中文

比较题目问到的总场大小，必须使用各分量平方和，而不是只比较 $y$ 分量。

#### Equation

$$
|\vec{E}_2|=\sqrt{100^2+40^2}\approx107.7\,\mathrm{V/m},\quad |\vec{E}_1|=|\vec{E}_3|=\sqrt{100^2+100^2}\approx141.4\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Region 2 therefore has the smaller total field, while regions 1 and 3 agree.

#### 中文

因此区域 2 的总场较小，区域 1 和 3 的场强相同。

#### Equation

$$
|\vec{E}_2|<|\vec{E}_1|=|\vec{E}_3|
$$

### Answer

$$
|\vec{E}_2|<|\vec{E}_1|=|\vec{E}_3|
$$

### Answer text

#### English

The normal component decreases; do not scale every field component inversely with permittivity.

#### 中文

减小的是法向分量；不能把所有电场分量都按介电常数反比缩放。
