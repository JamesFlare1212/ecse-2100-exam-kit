## Question

### English

As discussed in class, due to the skin effect, current prefers to flow closer to the surfaces of a conductor at higher frequencies. Consider the microstrip line below, which has the following properties:
• Width (in $y$-dimension): $W$
• Height (in $z$-dimension): $h$
• Length (in $x$-dimension): infinite
• Conductivity: $\sigma$

The current density distribution in the microstrip line is given by:
$$\vec J(z)=\begin{cases}J_0\hat x&\text{for }0.4h\le z<0.5h\\0&\text{for }-0.4h<z<0.4h\\J_0\hat x&\text{for }-0.5h<z\le-0.4h\end{cases}$$
Ignore any electric field fringing effects due to the finite width of the microstrip line.

### 中文

如课堂所述，由于趋肤效应，频率较高时电流倾向于靠近导体表面流动。考虑下方微带线，其性质如下：
• 宽度（$y$ 方向）：$W$
• 高度（$z$ 方向）：$h$
• 长度（$x$ 方向）：无限长
• 电导率：$\sigma$

微带线中的电流密度分布给定为：
$$\vec J(z)=\begin{cases}J_0\hat x&\text{当 }0.4h\le z<0.5h\\0&\text{当 }-0.4h<z<0.4h\\J_0\hat x&\text{当 }-0.5h<z\le-0.4h\end{cases}$$
忽略由微带线有限宽度引起的电场边缘效应。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects current-density flux, microscopic Ohm's law and the resistance increase caused by skin effect.

#### 中文

本题考查电流密度的通量积分、微观欧姆定律，以及趋肤效应为什么会增大电阻。

### Givens

#### English

The strip has width $W$, thickness $h$ and conductivity $\sigma$, with current along $x$. The given piecewise $\vec J(z)$ is nonzero in the top and bottom surface layers and zero in the middle.

#### 中文

微带线宽为 $W$、厚为 $h$、电导率为 $\sigma$，电流沿 $x$ 方向。题设分段给出 $\vec J(z)$：上下表层有电流，中间区域为零。

### Goal

#### English

Find total current $I$, voltage drop per unit length $V^\prime$ and resistance per unit length $R^\prime$, then explain the effects of conductivity and current distribution.

#### 中文

依次求总电流 $I$、单位长度电势降 $V^\prime$ 和单位长度电阻 $R^\prime$，再解释电导率与电流分布的影响。

### Approach

#### English

Identify the current-carrying regions before integrating over a cross-section perpendicular to the current. Then use $\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}$ to connect field and voltage drop, and apply the definition of resistance.

#### 中文

先认清哪些区域真正载流，再选垂直于电流的横截面积分。得到总电流后，用 $\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}$ 联系电场与电势降，最后回到电阻定义。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the total current $I$ flowing through the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算流过微带线的总电流 $I$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the current-flux formula from the crib sheet, retaining the direction of $\vec J$:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的电流通量公式，保留 $\vec J$ 的方向：

#### Equation

$$
I=\iint_S\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S}
$$

#### References

- [current](formula:current)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $\vec J=J_0\hat x$ in the upper and lower layers and zero in the middle, find total current $I$. Use the $yz$ cross-section with normal $+\hat x$:

#### 中文

已知上下表层 $\vec J=J_0\hat x$、中间区域为零，求总电流 $I$。取法向为 $+\hat x$ 的 $yz$ 截面：

#### Equation

$$
d\vec{S}=\hat{x}\,dy\,dz,\qquad \hat{x}\cdot\hat{x}=1
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the piecewise current density and integrate over the lower, middle and upper layers:

#### 中文

把分段电流密度代入，分别积分下层、中层和上层：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I&=I_{\mathrm{bottom}}+I_{\mathrm{middle}}+I_{\mathrm{top}}\\I_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=\int_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}\!\int_0^W J_0\,dy\,dz\\I_{\mathrm{middle}}&=\int_{-0.4h}^{0.4h}\!\int_0^W 0\,dy\,dz=0\\I_{\mathrm{top}}&=\int_{0.4h}^{0.5h}\!\int_0^W J_0\,dy\,dz\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Integrate over $y$, evaluate the $z$ endpoints for both layers and add to obtain

#### 中文

先对 $y$ 积分，再代入两层的 $z$ 上下限，相加得

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^W J_0\,dy&=[J_0y]_0^W=J_0W,\\I_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=J_0W[z]_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}=J_0W(-0.4h+0.5h)=0.1J_0Wh,\\I_{\mathrm{top}}&=J_0W[z]_{0.4h}^{0.5h}=J_0W(0.5h-0.4h)=0.1J_0Wh,\\I&=(0.1+0+0.1)J_0Wh\end{aligned}
$$

### Answer

$$
I=0.2J_0Wh
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Calculate the magnitude of the potential drop per unit length $V^{\prime}$ of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算微带线单位长度电势降的大小 $V^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the potential-difference formula and microscopic Ohm’s law from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的电势差公式与微观欧姆定律：

#### Equation

$$
V(B)-V(A)=-\int_A^B\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l},\qquad\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)
- [ohm](formula:ohm)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $\vec J=J_0\hat x$ in a conducting layer and conductivity $\sigma$, find the potential-drop magnitude per unit length. Substitute into Ohm’s law and integrate along $+x$:

#### 中文

已知载流表层 $\vec J=J_0\hat x$、电导率 $\sigma$，求单位长度电势降大小。把电流密度代入欧姆定律，沿 $+x$ 积分：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=\frac{\vec{J}}{\sigma}=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\hat{x},\qquad d\vec{l}=\hat{x}\,dx
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the field and path element into the line integral:

#### 中文

把电场和路径元代入线积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(\ell)-V(0)&=-\int_0^\ell\frac{J_0}{\sigma}(\hat x\cdot\hat x)\,dx\\&=-\frac{J_0}{\sigma}[x]_0^\ell=-\frac{J_0\ell}{\sigma}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Taking the drop magnitude and dividing by $\ell$ gives

#### 中文

取电势降大小并除以 $\ell$，得

#### Equation

$$
V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\quad[\mathrm{V/m}]
$$

### Answer

$$
V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\quad[\mathrm{V/m}]
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the resistance per unit length $R^{\prime}$ of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算微带线的单位长度电阻 $R^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the resistance definition from the crib sheet and divide by length:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的电阻定义，并除以长度：

#### Equation

$$
R=\frac{\Delta V}{I},\qquad R^\prime=\frac R\ell=\frac{\Delta V/\ell}{I}=\frac{V^\prime}{I}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 2

#### English

Using $I$ and $V^\prime$ from (a) and (b), find $R^\prime$. Substitute both results:

#### 中文

已知 (a)、(b) 的 $I$ 和 $V^\prime$，求 $R^\prime$。把两个结果代入：

#### Equation

$$
I=0.2J_0Wh,\qquad V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma},\qquad R^\prime=\frac{J_0/\sigma}{0.2J_0Wh}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Cancelling $J_0$ gives

#### 中文

约去 $J_0$，得

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime=\frac{5}{\sigma Wh}\quad[\Omega/\mathrm m]
$$

### Answer

$$
R^\prime=\frac{5}{\sigma Wh}\quad[\Omega/\mathrm m]
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

For the same current density as above, use physical reasoning to explain why increasing the conductivity $\sigma$ would lead to a decrease in the resistance of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

在电流密度与上文相同的情况下，用物理推理解释为什么增大电导率 $\sigma$ 会降低微带线的电阻。

### Step 1

#### English

Use microscopic Ohm’s law and current flux from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的微观欧姆定律与电流通量：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E},\qquad I=\iint\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S}
$$

#### References

- [ohm](formula:ohm)
- [current](formula:current)

### Step 2

#### English

The current-density distribution and cross-section stay fixed. Determine the effect of increasing $\sigma$ by substituting the fixed $I$ and $E_x=J_0/\sigma$:

#### 中文

已知电流密度分布和截面不变，求增大 $\sigma$ 对电阻的影响。把固定的 $I$ 与 $E_x=J_0/\sigma$ 代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle I=0.2J_0Wh=\text{constant},\qquad E_x=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle R^\prime=\frac{V^\prime}{I}=\frac{J_0/\sigma}{0.2J_0Wh}=\frac5{\sigma Wh}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\sigma\uparrow\ \Longrightarrow\ R^\prime\downarrow
$$

### Answer

$$
\sigma\uparrow\ \Longrightarrow\ R^\prime\downarrow
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Higher conductivity requires a smaller driving field for the same current.

#### 中文

更高电导率意味着维持同样电流只需更小的驱动电场。

## Part e

### Question

#### English

For the same total current flowing through the microstrip line, how would the resistance of the microstrip line differ if the current density were distributed uniformly over the cross-section instead of being concentrated near the surfaces? Briefly explain using physical reasoning.

#### 中文

在流过微带线的总电流相同的情况下，如果电流密度均匀分布在整个横截面上，而不是集中在表面附近，电阻会有什么不同？用物理推理简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Use current flux and microscopic Ohm’s law from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的电流通量与微观欧姆定律：

#### Equation

$$
I=\iint\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S},\qquad\vec{E}=\frac{\vec{J}}{\sigma}
$$

#### References

- [current](formula:current)
- [ohm](formula:ohm)

### Step 2

#### English

Keep total current $I$ fixed and find the resistance for a uniform distribution. Integrate the uniform density over the full $Wh$ cross-section:

#### 中文

已知总电流 $I$ 不变，求电流均匀分布时的电阻。把均匀密度代入整个 $Wh$ 截面积分：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}I&=\int_{-h/2}^{h/2}\!\int_0^W J_{\mathrm{uniform}}\,dy\,dz\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}[y]_0^W[z]_{-h/2}^{h/2}\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}(W-0)[h/2-(-h/2)]\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}Wh\end{aligned}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle J_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac I{Wh}=\frac{0.2J_0Wh}{Wh}=0.2J_0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the new density into Ohm’s law and $R^\prime=V^\prime/I$:

#### 中文

把新密度代入欧姆定律和 $R^\prime=V^\prime/I$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle E_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac{0.2J_0}{\sigma}=0.2E_{\mathrm{skin}},\qquad V^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=0.2V^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac{I/(\sigma Wh)}{I}=\frac1{\sigma Wh},\qquad R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}=\frac5{\sigma Wh}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 4

#### English

The conducting area increases fivefold, giving one fifth of the original resistance.

#### 中文

载流面积扩大五倍，得电阻为原来的五分之一。

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac15 R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}
$$

### Answer

$$
R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac15 R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}
$$
