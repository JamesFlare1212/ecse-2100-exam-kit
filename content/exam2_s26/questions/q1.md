## Question

### English

As discussed in class, due to the skin effect, current prefers to flow closer to the surfaces of a conductor at higher frequencies. Consider the microstrip line below, which has the following properties:
• Width (in $y$-dimension): $W$
• Height (in $z$-dimension): $h$
• Length (in $x$-dimension): infinite
• Conductivity: $\sigma$

The current density distribution in the microstrip line is given by:
$$\vec J(z)=\begin{cases}J_0\hat x&\text{for }0.4h\le z<0.5h\\0&\text{for }-0.4h<z<0.4h\\J_0\hat x&\text{for }-0.5h<z\le-0.4h\end{cases}$$
Ignore any electric field fringing effects due to the finite width of the microstrip line.

### 中文

如课堂所述，由于趋肤效应，频率较高时电流倾向于靠近导体表面流动。考虑下方微带线，其性质如下：
• 宽度（$y$ 方向）：$W$
• 高度（$z$ 方向）：$h$
• 长度（$x$ 方向）：无限长
• 电导率：$\sigma$

微带线中的电流密度分布给定为：
$$\vec J(z)=\begin{cases}J_0\hat x&\text{当 }0.4h\le z<0.5h\\0&\text{当 }-0.4h<z<0.4h\\J_0\hat x&\text{当 }-0.5h<z\le-0.4h\end{cases}$$
忽略由微带线有限宽度引起的电场边缘效应。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects current-density flux, microscopic Ohm's law and the resistance increase caused by skin effect.

#### 中文

本题考查电流密度的通量积分、微观欧姆定律，以及趋肤效应为什么会增大电阻。

### Givens

#### English

The strip has width $W$, thickness $h$ and conductivity $\sigma$, with current along $x$. The given piecewise $\vec J(z)$ is nonzero in the top and bottom surface layers and zero in the middle.

#### 中文

微带线宽为 $W$、厚为 $h$、电导率为 $\sigma$，电流沿 $x$ 方向。题设分段给出 $\vec J(z)$：上下表层有电流，中间区域为零。

### Goal

#### English

Find total current $I$, voltage drop per unit length $V^\prime$ and resistance per unit length $R^\prime$, then explain the effects of conductivity and current distribution.

#### 中文

依次求总电流 $I$、单位长度电势降 $V^\prime$ 和单位长度电阻 $R^\prime$，再解释电导率与电流分布的影响。

### Approach

#### English

Identify the current-carrying regions before integrating over a cross-section perpendicular to the current. Then use $\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}$ to connect field and voltage drop, and apply the definition of resistance.

#### 中文

先认清哪些区域真正载流，再选垂直于电流的横截面积分。得到总电流后，用 $\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}$ 联系电场与电势降，最后回到电阻定义。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the total current $I$ flowing through the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算流过微带线的总电流 $I$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib current-density flux equation: integrate the normal component of $\vec J$ over the cross-section.

#### 中文

先写公式表中的电流密度通量公式，总电流是穿过截面的 $\vec J$ 的法向分量积分。

#### Equation

$$
I=\iint_S\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S}
$$

#### References

- [current](formula:current)

### Step 2

#### English

Current flows along $+x$, so choose a $yz$ cross-section with normal $+\hat{x}$. As drawn, $y$ runs from 0 to $W$ and $z$ from $-0.5h$ to $0.5h$.

#### 中文

电流沿 $+x$，所以选 $yz$ 截面、外法向 $+\hat{x}$；按原图 $y$ 从 0 到 $W$，$z$ 从 $-0.5h$ 到 $0.5h$。

#### Equation

$$
d\vec{S}=\hat{x}\,dy\,dz,\qquad \hat{x}\cdot\hat{x}=1
$$

### Step 3

#### English

First reduce the dot product to a scalar. In each conducting layer the current density and surface normal point in the same direction, so the unit-vector dot product is $1$. The middle layer contributes zero.

#### 中文

先把点积写成标量。载流层中电流密度和面法向同向，所以点积中的两个单位矢量相乘为 $1$；中间层的贡献为零。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(J_0\hat x)\cdot(\hat x\,dy\,dz)&=J_0(\hat x\cdot\hat x)\,dy\,dz\\&=J_0\,dy\,dz\\\vec 0\cdot d\vec S&=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Split the thickness into the bottom layer, middle layer and top layer, then add their integrals. The two conducting intervals cannot be replaced by the full conductor thickness.

#### 中文

按厚度方向分成下表层、中间层和上表层，分别列出积分后相加。两个载流层的积分限不能合成整块导体的厚度。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I&=I_{\mathrm{bottom}}+I_{\mathrm{middle}}+I_{\mathrm{top}}\\I_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=\int_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}\!\int_0^W J_0\,dy\,dz\\I_{\mathrm{middle}}&=\int_{-0.4h}^{0.4h}\!\int_0^W 0\,dy\,dz=0\\I_{\mathrm{top}}&=\int_{0.4h}^{0.5h}\!\int_0^W J_0\,dy\,dz\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Evaluate the inner $y$ integral first. The quantity $J_0$ is constant with respect to $y$, so an antiderivative is $J_0y$. Brackets mean the value at the upper limit minus the value at the lower limit.

#### 中文

先算最里面的 $y$ 积分。对 $y$ 来说，$J_0$ 是常数；常数的原函数是这个常数乘积分变量。方括号表示“上限值减下限值”。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^W J_0\,dy&=[J_0y]_0^W\\&=J_0W-J_0(0)\\&=J_0W\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Insert the inner-integral result into the bottom-layer integral. The quantity $J_0W$ is also independent of $z$, so take it outside and write the antiderivative.

#### 中文

将内层积分的结果放回下表层的外层积分。$J_0W$ 也不随 $z$ 变化，因此可以提出积分号，再写出原函数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=\int_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}J_0W\,dz\\&=J_0W\int_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}1\,dz\\&=J_0W[z]_{-0.5h}^{-0.4h}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Substitute the upper limit and subtract the lower limit. Because the lower limit is negative, subtracting it gives a plus sign; the bottom-layer thickness is positive.

#### 中文

先代入上限，再减去下限。下限本身为负数，因此减去它要变成加号；下表层厚度仍为正。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=J_0W[(-0.4h)-(-0.5h)]\\&=J_0W(-0.4h+0.5h)\\&=0.1J_0Wh\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Do the same outer integral for the top layer, explicitly showing the antiderivative and both endpoints. Both limits are positive here.

#### 中文

对上表层做同样的外层积分，明确写出原函数和两个端点。这里两个端点都为正。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I_{\mathrm{top}}&=\int_{0.4h}^{0.5h}J_0W\,dz\\&=J_0W[z]_{0.4h}^{0.5h}\\&=J_0W(0.5h-0.4h)\\&=0.1J_0Wh\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Now add the three currents and factor their common term. Both surface-layer currents point in the same direction, so they add; the negative coordinate of the bottom layer does not reverse its current.

#### 中文

现在把三部分电流相加，再提取共同因子。上下表层的电流方向相同，因此是相加，不会因下表层的坐标为负而相减。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I&=0.1J_0Wh+0+0.1J_0Wh\\&=(0.1+0.1)J_0Wh\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 10

#### English

Each layer is $0.1h$ thick, giving total conducting area $0.2Wh$ and current in amperes.

#### 中文

每层厚度是 $0.1h$，总载流面积为 $0.2Wh$；电流量纲为 $\mathrm{A}$。

#### Equation

$$
I=J_0W(0.1h+0.1h)=0.2J_0Wh
$$

### Answer

$$
I=0.2J_0Wh
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Calculate the magnitude of the potential drop per unit length $V^{\prime}$ of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算微带线单位长度电势降的大小 $V^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the potential integral and microscopic Ohm’s law. Define the voltage-drop sign before taking its magnitude.

#### 中文

先写电势差原式与微观欧姆定律。题目要求电压降的大小，符号约定应先明确。

#### Equation

$$
V(B)-V(A)=-\int_A^B\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l},\qquad\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)
- [ohm](formula:ohm)

### Step 2

#### English

Choose an $x$-directed path in a current-carrying surface layer. Its electric field follows from $J_{0}/\sigma$.

#### 中文

沿题设承载电流的表层取一条 $x$ 方向路径；这一层的电场由 $J_{0}/\sigma$ 给出。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=\frac{\vec{J}}{\sigma}=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\hat{x},\qquad d\vec{l}=\hat{x}\,dx
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute into the integral over a length $\ell$ and evaluate the unit-vector dot product.

#### 中文

在长度 $\ell$ 上代入积分并计算单位矢量点积。

#### Equation

$$
V(\ell)-V(0)=-\int_0^\ell\frac{J_0}{\sigma}(\hat{x}\cdot\hat{x})\,dx
$$

### Step 4

#### English

After the dot product becomes $1$, take the $x$-independent factor $J_0/\sigma$ outside. What remains is the integral of the constant $1$.

#### 中文

点积为 $1$ 后，把与 $x$ 无关的 $J_0/\sigma$ 提到积分号外。剩下的是对常数 $1$ 的积分。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(\ell)-V(0)&=-\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\int_0^\ell1\,dx\\&=-\frac{J_0}{\sigma}[x]_0^\ell\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Evaluate both endpoints. The potential change along the current is negative. Reversing the potential-difference order multiplies both sides by minus one.

#### 中文

代入上限和下限，得到沿电流方向的电势变化为负。若改写成“起点电势减终点电势”，整个式子两边都要乘负号。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(\ell)-V(0)&=-\frac{J_0}{\sigma}(\ell-0)\\&=-\frac{J_0\ell}{\sigma}\\V(0)-V(\ell)&=\frac{J_0\ell}{\sigma}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The voltage-drop magnitude per unit length is the drop magnitude divided by length. Substitute the potential difference just calculated, then cancel the common length.

#### 中文

单位长度电压降等于电压降大小除以长度。把刚算出的电势差完整代入，再约去共同的长度。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V^\prime&=\frac{|V(0)-V(\ell)|}{\ell}\\&=\frac{J_0\ell/\sigma}{\ell}\\&=\frac{J_0\ell}{\sigma\ell}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Divide by $\ell$ to obtain volts per meter.

#### 中文

除以长度 $\ell$，单位由 $\mathrm{V}$ 变为 $\mathrm{V}/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}
$$

### Answer

$$
V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\quad[\mathrm{V/m}]
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the resistance per unit length $R^{\prime}$ of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

计算微带线的单位长度电阻 $R^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the resistance definition and divide by conductor length to obtain its per-length form.

#### 中文

先写电阻定义，再将其除以导线长度，得到单位长度形式。

#### Equation

$$
R=\frac{\Delta V}{I},\qquad R^\prime=\frac R\ell=\frac{\Delta V/\ell}{I}=\frac{V^\prime}{I}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute both preceding results explicitly, distinguishing voltage drop per length from total voltage.

#### 中文

把 a)、b) 的两个结果完整代入，不要把电压降和总电压混淆。

#### Equation

$$
I=0.2J_0Wh,\qquad V^\prime=\frac{J_0}{\sigma},\qquad R^\prime=\frac{J_0/\sigma}{0.2J_0Wh}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Make the cancellation explicit: dividing by the denominator means multiplying by its reciprocal. Then place all factors in one fraction.

#### 中文

复合分式不能直接“看出”约分。先把除以分母改成乘它的倒数，再把所有因子放进同一个分式。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}R^\prime&=\frac{J_0/\sigma}{0.2J_0Wh}\\&=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}\frac1{0.2J_0Wh}\\&=\frac{J_0}{0.2\sigma J_0Wh}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Cancel the factor $J_0$ from numerator and denominator. Since $0.2=1/5$, dividing by $0.2$ multiplies by $5$.

#### 中文

分子和分母中的 $J_0$ 相消。再用 $0.2=1/5$，所以除以 $0.2$ 等于乘 $5$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}R^\prime&=\frac1{0.2\sigma Wh}\\&=\frac1{(1/5)\sigma Wh}\\&=\frac5{\sigma Wh}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Check units: $(\mathrm{S/m})\cdot\mathrm m^2$ is $\mathrm S\cdot\mathrm m$, whose reciprocal is $\Omega/\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

检查单位：$(\mathrm{S/m})\cdot\mathrm m^2=\mathrm S\cdot\mathrm m$，取倒数得到 $\Omega/\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime=\frac{5}{\sigma Wh}\quad[\Omega/\mathrm m]
$$

### Answer

$$
R^\prime=\frac{5}{\sigma Wh}\quad[\Omega/\mathrm m]
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

For the same current density as above, use physical reasoning to explain why increasing the conductivity $\sigma$ would lead to a decrease in the resistance of the microstrip line.

#### 中文

在电流密度与上文相同的情况下，用物理推理解释为什么增大电导率 $\sigma$ 会降低微带线的电阻。

### Step 1

#### English

Use microscopic Ohm’s law to identify what stays fixed: the specified $\vec J(z)$ distribution and conductor cross-section.

#### 中文

先用微观欧姆定律明确哪些量固定：题目保持同一个 $\vec J(z)$ 分布和同一截面。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{J}=\sigma\vec{E},\qquad I=\iint\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S}
$$

#### References

- [ohm](formula:ohm)
- [current](formula:current)

### Step 2

#### English

Fixed current density and area keep total current unchanged. Increasing $\sigma$ requires less driving field for that same $\vec J$.

#### 中文

固定电流密度与面积意味着总电流也不变。提高 $\sigma$ 后，同一 $\vec J$ 所需的驱动电场更小。

#### Equation

$$
I=0.2J_0Wh=\text{constant},\qquad E_x=\frac{J_0}{\sigma}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

For the same length, a smaller field gives a smaller voltage drop per length. Substitute that into the resistance definition.

#### 中文

长度相同，电场减小会让单位长度电压降减小；把它代入电阻定义。

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime=\frac{V^\prime}{I}=\frac{J_0/\sigma}{0.2J_0Wh}=\frac5{\sigma Wh}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 4

#### English

Increasing conductivity therefore reduces resistance because the same current requires a smaller voltage drop.

#### 中文

因此增加电导率会降低电阻；原因是维持相同电流所需的电压降减少。

#### Equation

$$
\sigma\uparrow\ \Longrightarrow\ R^\prime\downarrow
$$

### Answer

$$
\sigma\uparrow\ \Longrightarrow\ R^\prime\downarrow
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Higher conductivity requires a smaller driving field for the same current.

#### 中文

更高电导率意味着维持同样电流只需更小的驱动电场。

## Part e

### Question

#### English

For the same total current flowing through the microstrip line, how would the resistance of the microstrip line differ if the current density were distributed uniformly over the cross-section instead of being concentrated near the surfaces? Briefly explain using physical reasoning.

#### 中文

在流过微带线的总电流相同的情况下，如果电流密度均匀分布在整个横截面上，而不是集中在表面附近，电阻会有什么不同？用物理推理简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the current integral and Ohm’s law. Here total current $I$ is fixed, rather than the previous surface-layer density $J_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写电流积分和欧姆定律。这次固定的是总电流 $I$，而不是原来的表层密度 $J_{0}$。

#### Equation

$$
I=\iint\vec{J}\cdot d\vec{S},\qquad\vec{E}=\frac{\vec{J}}{\sigma}
$$

#### References

- [current](formula:current)
- [ohm](formula:ohm)

### Step 2

#### English

For a uniform distribution, evaluate the current integral explicitly. The density is constant in both directions, so the successive integrations give the width and thickness.

#### 中文

均匀分布时，先把电流积分真正算一遍：两个方向上的密度都不变，所以逐层积分分别给出宽度和厚度。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}I&=\int_{-h/2}^{h/2}\!\int_0^W J_{\mathrm{uniform}}\,dy\,dz\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}[y]_0^W[z]_{-h/2}^{h/2}\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}(W-0)[h/2-(-h/2)]\\&=J_{\mathrm{uniform}}Wh\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

A uniform distribution uses the entire $Wh$ cross-section. Compute the new current density first.

#### 中文

均匀分布使用整个 $Wh$ 截面；先求新的电流密度。

#### Equation

$$
J_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac I{Wh}=\frac{0.2J_0Wh}{Wh}=0.2J_0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute into Ohm’s law: both field and voltage drop per length become one fifth of their previous values.

#### 中文

代入欧姆定律，电场与每单位长度电压降都变为原来的五分之一。

#### Equation

$$
E_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac{0.2J_0}{\sigma}=0.2E_{\mathrm{skin}},\qquad V^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=0.2V^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

With total current unchanged, insert this voltage-drop ratio into $R'=V'/I$.

#### 中文

总电流保持不变，将电压降比代入 $R'=V'/I$。

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac{I/(\sigma Wh)}{I}=\frac1{\sigma Wh},\qquad R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}=\frac5{\sigma Wh}
$$

#### References

- [resistance](formula:resistance)

### Step 6

#### English

Resistance becomes one fifth, an $80\%$ reduction, because the available current-carrying area increases.

#### 中文

得到电阻减为五分之一，即下降 $80\%$；物理原因是可供载流的有效面积扩大。

#### Equation

$$
R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac15 R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}
$$

### Answer

$$
R^\prime_{\mathrm{uniform}}=\frac15 R^\prime_{\mathrm{skin}}
$$
