## Question

### English

Two immobile point charges are held at a height $z_0$ above a perfectly conducting plane that occupies the region $z<0$. A point charge $-q$ is located at $(-x_0,0,z_0)$ and a charge $+q$ is located at $(x_0,0,z_0)$. The region above the conductor $(z>0)$ has permittivity $\varepsilon_0$.

### 中文

两个固定不动的点电荷保持在理想导体平面上方高度 $z_0$ 处，导体占据区域 $z<0$。点电荷 $-q$ 位于 $(-x_0,0,z_0)$，点电荷 $+q$ 位于 $(x_0,0,z_0)$。导体上方区域 $(z>0)$ 的介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem combines image charges, Coulomb-field superposition and the surface-charge boundary condition at a perfect conductor.

#### 中文

本题考查镜像法、库仑场叠加和理想导体表面的电荷边界条件。

### Givens

#### English

The real charges $-q$ and $+q$ are at $(-x_0,0,z_0)$ and $(x_0,0,z_0)$. A perfect conductor occupies $z<0$, the upper region has permittivity $\varepsilon_0$, and the field is evaluated just above its surface.

#### 中文

真实电荷 $-q$、$+q$ 位于 $(-x_0,0,z_0)$、$(x_0,0,z_0)$；理想导体占据 $z<0$，上方介质的介电常数为 $\varepsilon_0$，观察点在表面上方。

### Goal

#### English

Construct the image geometry and field lines, find the surface field of one real charge and of the complete system, then obtain induced surface-charge density.

#### 中文

构造镜像几何与电场线，求单个真实电荷和完整系统的表面电场，再求感应面电荷密度。

### Approach

#### English

Assign each real charge its mirrored position and opposite sign, then write the displacement vectors. Check cancellation of tangential field at the conductor; the image region is a mathematical construction, not the physical field inside the conductor.

#### 中文

先为每个真实电荷确定镜像的位置和符号，再逐项写出位移矢量。叠加后检查导体表面的切向场是否抵消；镜像下半空间用于辅助计算，不是导体内部的真实电场。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Draw a diagram in the $x-z$ plane showing the geometry you will use for applying the method of images. Include the real charges, their image charges, their coordinates, and clearly mark the position of the conducting surface.

#### 中文

在 $x-z$ 平面内画出用于镜像法的几何示意图。包括真实电荷、镜像电荷及其坐标，并清楚标出导体表面的位置。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the conductor’s equipotential property and the tangential-field boundary condition from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用导体等势与 crib sheet 的切向场边界条件：

#### Equation

$$
V(x,y,0)=\text{constant},\qquad E_t(x,y,0^+)=0
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)
- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Given real charges $-q$ and $+q$ at $z=z_0$, construct the image geometry. Reflect each position across $z=0$ and reverse its charge:

#### 中文

已知真实电荷 $-q$、$+q$ 位于 $z=z_0$，求镜像几何。把每个位置关于 $z=0$ 反射，并将电荷反号：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle -q:\ (-x_0,0,z_0),\qquad +q:\ (x_0,0,z_0)\\[4pt]&\displaystyle q_{\mathrm{im},L}=+q,\qquad \vec{r}_{\mathrm{im},L}=(-x_0,0,-z_0)\\[4pt]&\displaystyle q_{\mathrm{im},R}=-q,\qquad\vec{r}_{\mathrm{im},R}=(x_0,0,-z_0)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Integrating the Coulomb field gives the point-charge potential. Each opposite-sign pair has equal distances to the plane, so its potentials cancel:

#### 中文

由库仑场的电势积分，每对异号电荷到平面上的距离相等，代入得电势相消：

#### Equation

$$
V_{\mathrm{pair}}(z=0)=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac{q_i}{R_i}-\frac{q_i}{R_i}\right)=0
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)
- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 4

#### English

This gives the image charges, with coordinates shown in the diagram:

#### 中文

得以下镜像电荷，坐标见图：

#### Equation

$$
q_{\mathrm{im},L}=+q,\qquad q_{\mathrm{im},R}=-q
$$

### Answer

$$
q_{\mathrm{im},L}=+q,\qquad q_{\mathrm{im},R}=-q
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Images reproduce the physical field only for $z>0$; $z<0$ is a mathematical extension.

#### 中文

镜像只用于求 $z>0$ 的真实场；$z<0$ 是数学延拓区域。

## Part b

### Question

#### English

In a new diagram, sketch the electric field lines between the charges you drew in part a), including those in the region $z<0$.

#### 中文

另画一张图，画出第 a) 问中各电荷之间的电场线，包括 $z<0$ 区域中的电场线。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the Coulomb field from the crib sheet and superpose all four charges:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的库仑场并叠加四个电荷：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(\vec{r})=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\sum_iq_i\frac{\vec{r}-\vec{r}_i}{|\vec{r}-\vec{r}_i|^3}
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)

### Step 2

#### English

Using the positions and signs from (a), draw the field lines. Substitute all four charges, direct arrows from positive to negative, and cancel the tangential field at the plane:

#### 中文

已知 (a) 的电荷位置与符号，求场线图。把四电荷代入，箭头从正电荷指向负电荷，平面处切向场相消：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle (x_i,z_i,q_i)=(-x_0,z_0,-q),\ (x_0,z_0,q),\ (-x_0,-z_0,q),\ (x_0,-z_0,-q)\\[4pt]&\displaystyle E_x(x,0,0)=0,\qquad \vec{E}(x,0,0)\parallel\hat{z}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \frac{d\vec{r}}{ds}\parallel\vec{E}(\vec{r})\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)

### Step 3

#### English

This gives the illustrated field lines, normal to the plane:

#### 中文

得图示场线，平面上满足：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(0^+)\parallel\hat{z}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(0^+)\parallel\hat{z}
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Arrows show direction; the physical field inside the conductor is zero.

#### 中文

图中箭头给出方向，导体内部真实电场为零。

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Determine the electric field $\vec E_{+q}$ at a point $P=(x,0,0)$ solely due to the point charge $+q$ located at $(x_0,0,z_0)$, ignoring the conducting plane.

#### 中文

忽略导体平面，求仅由位于 $(x_0,0,z_0)$ 的点电荷 $+q$ 在点 $P=(x,0,0)$ 产生的电场 $\vec E_{+q}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the point-charge field from the crib sheet, writing the unit vector as displacement divided by distance:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的点电荷电场，将单位矢量写成位移除以距离：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=\frac q{4\pi\epsilon_0R^2}\hat{R}=\frac{q\vec{R}}{4\pi\epsilon_0R^3}
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $+q$ at $(x_0,0,z_0)$ and P at $(x,0,0)$, find the single-charge field. Subtract source position from observation position to form the displacement and distance:

#### 中文

已知 $+q$ 在 $(x_0,0,z_0)$、P 在 $(x,0,0)$，求单电荷电场。用观察点减源点，代入位移与距离：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \vec{R}_+=(x,0,0)-(x_0,0,z_0)=(x-x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle R_+=\sqrt{(x-x_0)^2+z_0^2},\qquad \hat{R}_+=\frac{(x-x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}}{\sqrt{(x-x_0)^2+z_0^2}}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $q$, the displacement and distance. As requested, the conductor is ignored here:

#### 中文

把 $q$、位移和距离代入，得；本问按题意忽略导体。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_{+q}(P)=\frac{q[(x-x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0[(x-x_0)^2+z_0^2]^{3/2}}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_{+q}(P)=\frac{q[(x-x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0[(x-x_0)^2+z_0^2]^{3/2}}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Determine the total electric field $\vec E_{tot}$ at the point $P=(x,0,0)$ on the surface of the conductor, taking into account both real charges and the effect of the conducting plane.

#### 中文

考虑两个真实电荷及导体平面的影响，求导体表面点 $P=(x,0,0)$ 处的总电场 $\vec E_{tot}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the Coulomb field from the crib sheet and superpose the real and image charges:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的库仑场，叠加真实电荷和镜像电荷：

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\sum_{i=1}^4q_i\frac{\vec{R}_i}{R_i^3}
$$

#### References

- [coulomb](formula:coulomb)

### Step 2

#### English

Given all four charge positions, find the total field at P. Denote its distances to the left and right charge columns by $R_L$ and $R_R$:

#### 中文

已知四电荷位置，求 P 点总场。把 P 到左右电荷列的距离记为 $R_L$、$R_R$：

#### Equation

$$
R_L=\sqrt{(x+x_0)^2+z_0^2},\qquad R_R=\sqrt{(x-x_0)^2+z_0^2}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute each real–image pair into Coulomb’s law. The tangential components cancel and the normal components add:

#### 中文

把每列真实电荷和镜像电荷代入库仑式；切向分量相消，法向分量相加：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}\vec{E}_R&=\frac{q[(x-x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_R^3}\\&\quad-\frac{q[(x-x_0)\hat{x}+z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_R^3}\\&=-\frac{2qz_0}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_R^3}\hat{z}\end{aligned}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}\vec{E}_L&=-\frac{q[(x+x_0)\hat{x}-z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_L^3}\\&\quad+\frac{q[(x+x_0)\hat{x}+z_0\hat{z}]}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_L^3}\\&=+\frac{2qz_0}{4\pi\epsilon_0R_L^3}\hat{z}\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Adding the left and right columns gives

#### 中文

把左右两列相加，得

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}(P)=\frac{qz_0}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac1{R_L^3}-\frac1{R_R^3}\right)\hat{z}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}_{\mathrm{tot}}(P)=\frac{qz_0}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac1{R_L^3}-\frac1{R_R^3}\right)\hat{z}
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Using your result from part d), calculate the surface charge density $\rho_s(\vec r)$ on the conductor at the point $P=(x,0,0)$.

#### 中文

使用第 d) 问的结果，计算导体上点 $P=(x,0,0)$ 处的面电荷密度 $\rho_s(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the normal-displacement jump from the crib sheet, with the normal directed from the conductor into air:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的法向电位移跳变；法向从导体指向空气：

#### Equation

$$
D_{\mathrm{out},n}-D_{\mathrm{in},n}=\rho_s,\qquad\hat{n}=+\hat{z}
$$

#### References

- [boundary](formula:boundary)

### Step 2

#### English

Given zero field inside the conductor and the exterior field from (d), find $\rho_s$. Substitute $\vec D_{\mathrm{out}}=\epsilon_0\vec E(0^+)$:

#### 中文

已知导体内场为零、(d) 给出外侧电场，求 $\rho_s$。把 $\vec D_{\mathrm{out}}=\epsilon_0\vec E(0^+)$ 代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \rho_s=\epsilon_0\vec{E}(0^+)\cdot\hat{z}=\epsilon_0 E_z(0^+)\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \rho_s=\epsilon_0\frac{qz_0}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac1{R_L^3}-\frac1{R_R^3}\right)=\frac{qz_0}{2\pi}\left(\frac1{R_L^3}-\frac1{R_R^3}\right)\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 3

#### English

Expanding $R_L$ and $R_R$ gives the surface charge density in $\mathrm{C/m^2}$:

#### 中文

展开 $R_L$、$R_R$，得面电荷密度，单位为 $\mathrm{C/m^2}$。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_s(x)=\frac{qz_0}{2\pi}\left[\frac1{((x+x_0)^2+z_0^2)^{3/2}}-\frac1{((x-x_0)^2+z_0^2)^{3/2}}\right]
$$

### Answer

$$
\rho_s(x)=\frac{qz_0}{2\pi}\left[\frac1{((x+x_0)^2+z_0^2)^{3/2}}-\frac1{((x-x_0)^2+z_0^2)^{3/2}}\right]
$$
