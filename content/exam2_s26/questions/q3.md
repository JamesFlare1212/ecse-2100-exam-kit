## Question

### English

The coaxial cable to the right has a poor dielectric, which has allowed some charge to leak between the inner conductor and outer conductor. The cable has an inner conductor of radius $a$ with surface charge density $\rho_{sa}$ and an outer conducting shell of radius $b$, held at a potential $V(b)=0$. The region $a<r<b$ is filled with a dielectric of permittivity $\varepsilon$ and volumetric charge density given by:
$$\rho(r)=\rho_0\frac{a}{r}$$

### 中文

右侧同轴电缆的电介质质量较差，导致一些电荷泄漏到内导体与外导体之间。电缆的内导体半径为 $a$，面电荷密度为 $\rho_{sa}$；外导体壳的半径为 $b$，保持电势 $V(b)=0$。区域 $a<r<b$ 填充了介电常数为 $\varepsilon$ 的电介质，其体电荷密度给定为：
$$\rho(r)=\rho_0\frac{a}{r}$$

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem combines cylindrical Gauss's law, potential integration and differential capacitance in the presence of volume charge. Do not directly apply the coaxial formulas for a gap without volume charge.

#### 中文

本题考查有体电荷时的柱对称高斯定律、电势积分和微分电容，不能直接套用无体电荷的同轴公式。

### Givens

#### English

The conductors have radii $a$ and $b$, inner surface-charge density $\rho_{sa}$ and a grounded outer conductor. The gap has permittivity $\varepsilon$ and volume-charge density $\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$.

#### 中文

内、外导体半径为 $a$、$b$，内表面电荷密度为 $\rho_{sa}$，外导体接地；间隙介电常数为 $\varepsilon$，体电荷密度为 $\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$。

### Goal

#### English

Find field, potential and conductor voltage, express $\rho_{sa}$ as a function of voltage, then obtain capacitance per unit length using $dQ/dV_0$.

#### 中文

求电场、电势和导体间电压，再将 $\rho_{sa}$ 写成电压的函数，最后用 $dQ/dV_0$ 求单位长度电容。

### Approach

#### English

A coaxial Gaussian surface encloses both inner-conductor charge and part of the volume charge. Account for both before integrating the field to potential; hold the given volume-charge distribution fixed when differentiating for capacitance.

#### 中文

同轴高斯面包围的电荷包括内导体表面电荷和一部分体电荷。先分别计入二者，再积分得到电势；求微分电容时保持给定体电荷分布不变。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Using Gauss’s law, find the electric field $\vec E(\vec r)$ in the region $a<r<b$. Include a diagram that shows the location of your Gaussian surface.

#### 中文

使用高斯定律，求区域 $a<r<b$ 内的电场 $\vec E(\vec r)$。附图说明所选高斯面的位置。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the original Gauss equation and substitute $\epsilon\vec E$ for displacement.

#### 中文

先写原始高斯定律，再将电位移换成 $\epsilon\vec E$。

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_S\vec{D}\cdot d\vec{S}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}},\qquad\vec{D}=\epsilon\vec{E}
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

Choose a coaxial closed cylinder of radius $R$ and length $\ell$, with $a<R<b$. End-cap normals are perpendicular to the radial field.

#### 中文

选半径 $R$、长度 $\ell$ 的同轴闭合圆柱，高斯半径满足 $a<R<b$；端面的法向与径向电场垂直。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}=E_r(R)\hat{r},\quad d\vec{S}_{\mathrm{side}}=\hat{r}R\,d\phi\,dz,\quad \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{S}_{\mathrm{caps}}=0
$$

#### References

- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 3

#### English

On the cylindrical side the radius is fixed at $R$, and the field is independent of angle and axial coordinate. Write the dot product before integrating over the angle.

#### 中文

柱侧面上半径固定为 $R$，场强不随方位角或轴向坐标变化。先写出点积，再对方位角积分。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Phi_D&=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}(\epsilon E_r(R)\hat r)\cdot(\hat r R\,d\phi\,dz)\\&=\epsilon E_r(R)R\int_0^\ell[\phi]_0^{2\pi}\,dz\\&=2\pi\epsilon E_r(R)R\int_0^\ell1\,dz\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Next evaluate the length antiderivative and endpoints. This gives the total closed-cylinder flux because both end-cap contributions are zero.

#### 中文

再算长度方向的原函数和端点，得到整个闭合柱面的通量；两个端面贡献已经为零。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Phi_D&=2\pi\epsilon E_r(R)R[z]_0^\ell\\&=2\pi\epsilon E_r(R)R(\ell-0)\\&=2\pi R\ell\epsilon E_r(R)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Split the enclosed charge into inner-conductor surface charge and dielectric volume charge. The surface-charge area element uses $a$, not the Gaussian radius $R$.

#### 中文

包围电荷分成内导体面电荷与介质体电荷。面电荷的面积元用 $a$，而不是高斯半径 $R$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Q_{\mathrm{surface}}&=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\rho_{sa}a\,d\phi\,dz\\&=\rho_{sa}a\int_0^\ell[\phi]_0^{2\pi}\,dz\\&=2\pi a\rho_{sa}[z]_0^\ell\\&=2\pi a\ell\rho_{sa}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

For volume charge use a varying radius $r'$. Substitute both $\rho_0a/r'$ and the cylindrical volume element $r'\,dr'\,d\phi\,dz$.

#### 中文

体电荷积分必须使用变半径 $r'$；将密度 $\rho_0a/r'$ 和柱坐标体积元 $r'\,dr'\,d\phi\,dz$ 同时代入。

#### Equation

$$
Q_{\mathrm{volume}}=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_a^R\frac{\rho_0a}{r^\prime}\,r^\prime\,dr^\prime d\phi dz
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Cancel the varying-radius factors inside the integrand first. The $1/r^\prime$ density factor cancels the $r^\prime$ volume-element factor, leaving the constant $\rho_0a$.

#### 中文

先在被积函数内部约去变半径。密度中的 $1/r^\prime$ 与体积元中的 $r^\prime$ 抵消，剩下的 $\rho_0a$ 是常数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\rho_0a}{r^\prime}r^\prime&=\rho_0a\\Q_{\mathrm{volume}}&=\rho_0a\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_a^R1\,dr^\prime\,d\phi\,dz\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Start with the innermost radial integral. Its antiderivative is $r^\prime$, so endpoint substitution gives outer radius minus inner radius, not an area or volume.

#### 中文

从最内层的径向积分开始。原函数是 $r^\prime$，代入后得到外半径减内半径，而不是面积或体积。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_a^R1\,dr^\prime&=[r^\prime]_a^R=R-a\\Q_{\mathrm{volume}}&=\rho_0a(R-a)\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}1\,d\phi\,dz\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Then do the angular and axial integrals, writing each endpoint difference before multiplying the three factors.

#### 中文

再做方位角积分和轴向积分，逐一写出端点差，最后才把三个因子相乘。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_0^{2\pi}1\,d\phi&=[\phi]_0^{2\pi}=2\pi\\\int_0^\ell1\,dz&=[z]_0^\ell=\ell\\Q_{\mathrm{volume}}&=\rho_0a(R-a)(2\pi)\ell\\&=2\pi\ell\rho_0a(R-a)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 10

#### English

Add both charge terms, substitute into Gauss’s law and cancel the common length and $2\pi$.

#### 中文

把两项电荷相加后代入高斯定律，最后约去共同长度和 $2\pi$。

#### Equation

$$
2\pi R\ell\epsilon E_r(R)=2\pi a\ell[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(R-a)]
$$

### Step 11

#### English

First divide both sides by the common nonzero factor $2\pi\ell$, then by $\epsilon R$. Relabel $R$ as observation radius $r$ only after completing the fixed-surface integrals.

#### 中文

两边先除以共同的非零因子 $2\pi\ell$，再除以 $\epsilon R$。只有在做完固定高斯面的积分后，才把 $R$ 改记为观察半径 $r$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\epsilon R E_r(R)&=a[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(R-a)]\\E_r(R)&=\frac{a[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(R-a)]}{\epsilon R}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 12

#### English

Divide by $\epsilon R$ and relabel the arbitrary Gaussian radius $R$ as observation radius $r$.

#### 中文

除以 $\epsilon R$，并把任意高斯半径 $R$ 改记为观察半径 $r$。

#### Equation

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{a}{\epsilon r}[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(r-a)]\hat{r}
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{a}{\epsilon r}[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(r-a)]\hat{r}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the potential $V(\vec r)$ in the region $a<r<b$.

#### 中文

求区域 $a<r<b$ 内的电势 $V(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the potential-difference integral, referenced to the grounded outer conductor.

#### 中文

先写电势差原式，参考点选题设接地的外导体。

#### Equation

$$
V(r)-V(b)=-\int_b^r\vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l},\qquad V(b)=0
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Use a radial path and integration variable $s$, distinct from endpoint $r$.

#### 中文

选径向路径，积分变量用 $s$，避免和终点 $r$ 混淆。

#### Equation

$$
d\vec{l}=\hat{r}\,ds,\qquad V(r)=-\int_b^r\frac{a}{\epsilon s}[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(s-a)]\,ds
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Expand the integrand and combine like terms first. This identifies the terms that produce a logarithm and those that produce a linear function.

#### 中文

先展开被积函数，再将同类项合并；这一步能看出哪些项产生对数、哪些项产生线性函数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(s-a)}s&=\frac{\rho_{sa}+\rho_0s-\rho_0a}s\\&=\frac{\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0}s+\rho_0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Reversing a definite integral changes its sign, cancelling the minus sign in the potential definition. Then split the integral of a sum into two integrals.

#### 中文

将积分上下限交换，定积分会多出一个负号，它正好抵消电势定义中的负号。再把和的积分拆成两个积分。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(r)&=\frac a\epsilon\int_r^b\left[\frac{\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0}s+\rho_0\right]ds\\&=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\int_r^b\frac{ds}s+\rho_0\int_r^b1\,ds\right]\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The radius in the first term is positive. Use a logarithm of a dimensionless ratio as the antiderivative; evaluating its endpoints gives $\ln(b/r)$ and avoids taking a logarithm of a dimensional length by itself.

#### 中文

第一项中半径为正。用无量纲比值的对数作原函数，代入上、下限后得到 $\ln(b/r)$；这样也避免对有单位的长度单独取对数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\int_r^b\frac{ds}s&=[\ln(s/a)]_r^b\\&=\ln(b/a)-\ln(r/a)\\&=\ln\!\left(\frac{b/a}{r/a}\right)=\ln(b/r)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The second term is a constant integral. Its antiderivative is the integration variable itself; upper minus lower gives $b-r$.

#### 中文

第二项是常数积分。原函数是积分变量本身，代入上限再减下限，得到 $b-r$。

#### Equation

$$
\int_r^b1\,ds=[s]_r^b=b-r
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Multiply each integral value by its own coefficient and add them. Substitution at the grounded conductor checks that both terms vanish.

#### 中文

把两个积分值分别乘回各自的系数后相加。代入接地点可以逐项核对电势为零。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(r)&=\frac a\epsilon[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(b/r)+\rho_0(b-r)]\\V(b)&=\frac a\epsilon[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln1+\rho_0(b-b)]=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Evaluate both endpoints and use $\ln b-\ln r=\ln(b/r)$. Both terms vanish at $r=b$.

#### 中文

代入两端，使用 $\ln b-\ln r=\ln(b/r)$。在 $r=b$ 时对数项和线性项都为零。

#### Equation

$$
V(r)=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln\frac br+\rho_0(b-r)\right]
$$

### Answer

$$
V(r)=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln\frac br+\rho_0(b-r)\right]
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the potential difference between the conductors, $V_0=V(a)-V(b)$. Then, express the surface charge density on the inner conductor, $\rho_{sa}$, as a function of $V_0$ and $\rho_0$.

#### 中文

求导体之间的电势差 $V_0=V(a)-V(b)$。然后将内导体的面电荷密度 $\rho_{sa}$ 表示为 $V_0$ 和 $\rho_0$ 的函数。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the conductor potential difference and evaluate the preceding potential at $a$ and $b$.

#### 中文

先按定义写两导体电势差，再把上一问电位函数取在 $a$ 和 $b$。

#### Equation

$$
V_0=V(a)-V(b),\qquad V(b)=0
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute $r=a$, retaining both logarithmic and linear contributions.

#### 中文

将 $r=a$ 代入，保留完整的对数项与线性项。

#### Equation

$$
V_0=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln\frac ba+\rho_0(b-a)\right]
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Multiply both sides by $\epsilon/a$ to remove the outer coefficient.

#### 中文

两边乘 $\epsilon/a$，先消去最外层的系数。

#### Equation

$$
\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a}=(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(b/a)+\rho_0(b-a)
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Move the linear term independent of the unknown surface density to the left by subtracting it from both sides.

#### 中文

先把不含未知面密度的线性项移到左侧：两边同时减去同一项，不能只改变它在右侧的符号。

#### Equation

$$
\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a}-\rho_0(b-a)=(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(b/a)
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Since $b>a>0$, the quantity $\ln(b/a)$ is positive and nonzero, so divide both sides by it.

#### 中文

由于 $b>a>0$，$\ln(b/a)$ 为正且不为零，因此两边都能除以它。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0=\frac{\epsilon V_0/a-\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Add $a\rho_0$ to both sides, then divide each numerator term by the common denominator.

#### 中文

两边加回 $a\rho_0$，再把分子的两项分别除以同一个分母。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}=a\rho_0+\frac{\epsilon V_0/a}{\ln(b/a)}-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Add back $a\rho_0$ and expand the numerator. Every term has dimension $\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{m}^{2}$.

#### 中文

最后加回 $a\rho_{0}$，并把分子展开；每一项的量纲都是 $\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{m}^{2}$。

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}+a\rho_0-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}+a\rho_0-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

When a volumetric charge density exists in the region between two conductors, the capacitance cannot be computed using the usual formulas. Instead, it must be defined by $C=\frac{dQ}{dV_0}$, where $Q$ is the total charge on one conductor and $V_0$ is the potential difference between the conductors. Using this definition and your results from earlier parts, determine the capacitance per unit length $C^{\prime}$ of the cable.

#### 中文

当两导体之间存在体电荷密度时，不能用通常的公式计算电容，而必须定义为 $C=\frac{dQ}{dV_0}$，其中 $Q$ 为其中一个导体的总电荷，$V_0$ 为导体间的电势差。使用这一定义和前几问的结果，求电缆的单位长度电容 $C^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the differential capacitance required by the question: hold the background space charge fixed while varying the conductor voltage.

#### 中文

本题明确要求微分电容：背景空间电荷保持不变，只改变两导体间电势差。

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac1\ell\left.\frac{\partial Q}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}=\left.\frac{\partial Q^\prime}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}
$$

#### References

- [capacitance](formula:capacitance)

### Step 2

#### English

First convert the inner-conductor surface density to charge per unit length.

#### 中文

先用内导体表面电荷密度求每单位长度电荷。

#### Equation

$$
Q=2\pi a\ell\rho_{sa},\qquad Q^\prime=\frac Q\ell=2\pi a\rho_{sa}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the full expression for $\rho_{sa}$ from part c).

#### 中文

把 c) 求出的 $\rho_{sa}$ 完整代入。

#### Equation

$$
Q^\prime=2\pi a\left[\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}+a\rho_0-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}\right]
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Separate the term linear in $V_{0}$ from the fixed-charge offset.

#### 中文

把含 $V_{0}$ 的线性项与固定电荷造成的偏移量分开。

#### Equation

$$
Q^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}V_0+2\pi a\rho_0\left[a-\frac{b-a}{\ln(b/a)}\right]
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Only $V_0$ varies during differentiation. Geometry, permittivity and the specified volume density are fixed, so the entire term independent of $V_0$ is a constant.

#### 中文

求导时只改变 $V_0$。几何尺寸、介电常数和题设体电荷密度都固定，因此不含 $V_0$ 的整项都按常数求导。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\left.\frac{\partial Q^\prime}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}&=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\frac{\partial V_0}{\partial V_0}\\&\quad+2\pi a\left[a-\frac{b-a}{\ln(b/a)}\right]\frac{\partial\rho_0}{\partial V_0}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The derivative of a variable with respect to itself is $1$, while the fixed background density has zero voltage derivative. Substitution leaves only the coefficient of the linear term.

#### 中文

变量对自身的导数为 $1$；固定的背景密度对电压的导数为 $0$。逐项代入后只留下线性项的系数。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial V_0}{\partial V_0}&=1,\qquad\left.\frac{\partial\rho_0}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}=0\\\frac{\partial Q^\prime}{\partial V_0}&=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}(1)\\&\quad+2\pi a\left[a-\frac{b-a}{\ln(b/a)}\right](0)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Space charge changes the $Q\text{–}V$ intercept but not the slope in this model.

#### 中文

所以空间电荷改变 $Q\text{–}V$ 曲线截距，却不改变这里的斜率。

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$

### Answer

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$
