## Question

### English

The coaxial cable to the right has a poor dielectric, which has allowed some charge to leak between the inner conductor and outer conductor. The cable has an inner conductor of radius $a$ with surface charge density $\rho_{sa}$ and an outer conducting shell of radius $b$, held at a potential $V(b)=0$. The region $a<r<b$ is filled with a dielectric of permittivity $\varepsilon$ and volumetric charge density given by:
$$\rho(r)=\rho_0\frac{a}{r}$$

### 中文

右侧同轴电缆的电介质质量较差，导致一些电荷泄漏到内导体与外导体之间。电缆的内导体半径为 $a$，面电荷密度为 $\rho_{sa}$；外导体壳的半径为 $b$，保持电势 $V(b)=0$。区域 $a<r<b$ 填充了介电常数为 $\varepsilon$ 的电介质，其体电荷密度给定为：
$$\rho(r)=\rho_0\frac{a}{r}$$

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

Cylindrical Gauss’s law, potential integration and differential capacitance.

#### 中文

柱对称高斯定律、电势积分与微分电容。

### Givens

#### English

$a<r<b$, $\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$, $\varepsilon$, $\rho_{sa}$ and $V(b)=0$.

#### 中文

$a<r<b$，$\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$，$\varepsilon$，$\rho_{sa}$，$V(b)=0$。

### Goal

#### English

Find $\vec E(r)$, $V(r)$, $\rho_{sa}(V_0)$ and $C^\prime$.

#### 中文

求 $\vec E(r)$、$V(r)$、$\rho_{sa}(V_0)$ 和 $C^\prime$。

### Approach

#### English

Include both surface and volume charge; hold $\rho_0$ fixed when finding capacitance.

#### 中文

先计入面电荷与体电荷；求电容时固定 $\rho_0$。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Using Gauss’s law, find the electric field $\vec E(\vec r)$ in the region $a<r<b$. Include a diagram that shows the location of your Gaussian surface.

#### 中文

使用高斯定律，求区域 $a<r<b$ 内的电场 $\vec E(\vec r)$。附图说明所选高斯面的位置。

### Step 1

#### English

Use Gauss’s law and the constitutive relation from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 中的高斯定律与本构关系：

#### Equation

$$
\oiint_S\vec D\cdot d\vec S=\iiint\rho\,d\mathcal V=Q_{\mathrm{enc}},\qquad\vec D=\varepsilon\vec E
$$

#### References

- [gauss](formula:gauss)
- [constitutive](formula:constitutive)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$, $\rho_{sa}$ and $\varepsilon$, find $\vec E(r)$. Choose a coaxial Gaussian cylinder of radius $a<R<b$ and length $\ell$:

#### 中文

已知 $\rho(r)=\rho_0a/r$、$\rho_{sa}$ 和 $\varepsilon$，求 $\vec E(r)$。取半径 $a<R<b$、长度 $\ell$ 的同轴高斯柱面：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\because\quad\vec E(R)&=E_r(R)\hat r,\quad d\vec S_{\mathrm{side}}=\hat r R\,d\phi\,dz,\\\vec E\cdot d\vec S_{\mathrm{caps}}&=0,\\\therefore\quad\Phi_D&=\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}(\varepsilon E_r(R)\hat r)\cdot(\hat r R\,d\phi\,dz)\\&=2\pi R\ell\varepsilon E_r(R)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the cylindrical volume element from the crib sheet and $\rho(r^\prime)=\rho_0a/r^\prime$ into the enclosed-charge integral, including the inner conductor’s surface charge:

#### 中文

把 crib sheet 的柱坐标体积元与 $\rho(r^\prime)=\rho_0a/r^\prime$ 代入包围电荷的积分，加上内导体的面电荷：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\because\quad d\mathcal V&=r^\prime\,dr^\prime\,d\phi\,dz,\quad\rho(r^\prime)=\frac{\rho_0a}{r^\prime},\\\therefore\quad Q_{\mathrm{enc}}&=2\pi a\ell\rho_{sa}+\int_0^\ell\!\int_0^{2\pi}\!\int_a^R\frac{\rho_0a}{r^\prime}\,r^\prime\,dr^\prime\,d\phi\,dz\\&=2\pi a\ell\rho_{sa}+2\pi\ell\rho_0a[r^\prime]_a^R\\&=2\pi a\ell[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(R-a)]\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [geometry](formula:geometry)

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute the flux and enclosed charge into Gauss’s law, cancel $2\pi\ell$ and write $R=r$. This gives

#### 中文

把通量和包围电荷代入高斯定律，约去 $2\pi\ell$，将 $R$ 写作 $r$，得

#### Equation

$$
2\pi R\ell\varepsilon E_r(R)=2\pi a\ell[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(R-a)]
$$

### Answer

$$
\vec{E}(r)=\frac{a}{\epsilon r}[\rho_{sa}+\rho_0(r-a)]\hat{r}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Find the potential $V(\vec r)$ in the region $a<r<b$.

#### 中文

求区域 $a<r<b$ 内的电势 $V(\vec r)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the potential-difference formula from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 中的电势差公式：

#### Equation

$$
V(b)-V(a)=-\int_a^b\vec E\cdot d\vec l
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $V(b)=0$, find $V(r)$. Substitute the field from (a), using the radial path $d\vec l=\hat r\,ds$:

#### 中文

已知 $V(b)=0$，求 $V(r)$。把 (a) 的电场代入上式，取径向路径 $d\vec l=\hat r\,ds$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V(r)&=-\int_b^r\vec E(s)\cdot(\hat r\,ds)=\int_r^b E_r(s)\,ds\\&=\frac a\varepsilon\int_r^b\left[\frac{\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0}{s}+\rho_0\right]ds\\&=\frac a\varepsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(s/a)+\rho_0s\right]_r^b\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Evaluate at the limits to obtain

#### 中文

代入积分上下限，得

#### Equation

$$
V(r)=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln\frac br+\rho_0(b-r)\right]
$$

### Answer

$$
V(r)=\frac a\epsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln\frac br+\rho_0(b-r)\right]
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Find the potential difference between the conductors, $V_0=V(a)-V(b)$. Then, express the surface charge density on the inner conductor, $\rho_{sa}$, as a function of $V_0$ and $\rho_0$.

#### 中文

求导体之间的电势差 $V_0=V(a)-V(b)$。然后将内导体的面电荷密度 $\rho_{sa}$ 表示为 $V_0$ 和 $\rho_0$ 的函数。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s potential-difference formula between the conductors:

#### 中文

将 crib sheet 的电势差公式用于两导体之间：

#### Equation

$$
V_0=V(a)-V(b)=-\int_b^a\vec E\cdot d\vec l
$$

#### References

- [potential](formula:potential)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $V(b)=0$, find $V_0$ and $\rho_{sa}(V_0,\rho_0)$. Substitute $r=a$ into (b) and collect the $\rho_{sa}$ term:

#### 中文

已知 $V(b)=0$，求 $V_0$ 及 $\rho_{sa}(V_0,\rho_0)$。把 $r=a$ 代入 (b)，整理 $\rho_{sa}$ 项：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V_0&=\frac a\varepsilon\left[(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(b/a)+\rho_0(b-a)\right],\\(\rho_{sa}-a\rho_0)\ln(b/a)&=\frac{\varepsilon V_0}{a}-\rho_0(b-a)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Solving for $\rho_{sa}$ gives

#### 中文

解出 $\rho_{sa}$，得

#### Equation

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}+a\rho_0-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

### Answer

$$
\rho_{sa}=\frac{\epsilon V_0}{a\ln(b/a)}+a\rho_0-\frac{\rho_0(b-a)}{\ln(b/a)}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

When a volumetric charge density exists in the region between two conductors, the capacitance cannot be computed using the usual formulas. Instead, it must be defined by $C=\frac{dQ}{dV_0}$, where $Q$ is the total charge on one conductor and $V_0$ is the potential difference between the conductors. Using this definition and your results from earlier parts, determine the capacitance per unit length $C^{\prime}$ of the cable.

#### 中文

当两导体之间存在体电荷密度时，不能用通常的公式计算电容，而必须定义为 $C=\frac{dQ}{dV_0}$，其中 $Q$ 为其中一个导体的总电荷，$V_0$ 为导体间的电势差。使用这一定义和前几问的结果，求电缆的单位长度电容 $C^{\prime}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the differential-capacitance definition supplied in the question:

#### 中文

使用题目给出的微分电容定义：

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac1\ell\left.\frac{\partial Q}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}=\left.\frac{\partial Q^\prime}{\partial V_0}\right|_{\rho_0}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

With $\rho_0$ held fixed, find $C^\prime$. Substitute $\rho_{sa}$ from (c) into the inner conductor’s charge per unit length, $Q^\prime=2\pi a\rho_{sa}$:

#### 中文

已知 $\rho_0$ 固定，求 $C^\prime$。把 (c) 的 $\rho_{sa}$ 代入内导体的单位长度电荷 $Q^\prime=2\pi a\rho_{sa}$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Q^\prime&=2\pi a\rho_{sa}\\&=\frac{2\pi\varepsilon}{\ln(b/a)}V_0+2\pi a\rho_0\left[a-\frac{b-a}{\ln(b/a)}\right]\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Differentiate with respect to $V_0$; the term containing $\rho_0$ is constant. This gives

#### 中文

对 $V_0$ 求导，含 $\rho_0$ 的常数项消失，得

#### Equation

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$

### Answer

$$
C^\prime=\frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}\quad[\mathrm{F/m}]
$$
