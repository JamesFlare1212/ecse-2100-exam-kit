## Question

### English

The transmission line in the circuit below has a characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$ and length $L=\frac{\lambda}{4}$. The amplitude of the sinusoidal source voltage is $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$, its wavelength is $\lambda=20\,\mathrm{m}$ and its frequency is $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$.

### 中文

下图电路中的传输线具有特性阻抗 $Z_0=50\Omega$，长度为 $L=\frac{\lambda}{4}$。正弦源电压的幅值为 $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$，其波长为 $\lambda=20\,\mathrm{m}$，频率为 $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem links quarter-wave impedance transformation, phasors, time-domain waves and power matching.

#### 中文

本题考查四分之一波长阻抗变换，以及相量、时域波形与功率匹配之间的联系。

### Givens

#### English

The original circuit has $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, $L=\lambda/4$, $R_g=50\,\Omega$ and $R_L=125\,\Omega$. The source amplitude is $10\,\mathrm V$, and wavelength and frequency are supplied.

#### 中文

原电路中 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$L=\lambda/4$、$R_g=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=125\,\Omega$；源幅值为 $10\,\mathrm V$，并给出了波长与频率。

### Goal

#### English

Find input impedance and total voltage in phasor and time-domain form, choose a matching line, and explain why local reflections can remain.

#### 中文

求输入阻抗、总电压的相量和时域表达式，选择匹配用电缆，并解释匹配时为何仍可存在局部反射。

### Approach

#### English

Use the quarter-wave property to identify the impedance seen by the source, then determine the waves from the source boundary condition. Treat port matching and reflection at the load as separate questions.

#### 中文

先用四分之一波长的特殊性质看清输入端负载，再由源端边界条件确定行波。将端口是否匹配与负载处是否反射分开讨论。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate $Z_{in}$ for the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算这根传输线的 $Z_{in}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the quarter-wave transformation from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的四分之一波长变换式：

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $Z_L=125\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$. Substitute:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$Z_L=125\,\Omega$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$。代入：

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(50\,\Omega)^2}{125\,\Omega}=\frac{2500}{125}\,\Omega
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=20\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=20\,\Omega
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Write down the phasor for the total voltage wave $\widetilde V(z)$ on the transmission line.

#### 中文

写出传输线上总电压波 $\widetilde V(z)$ 的相量。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the voltage phasor from the crib sheet, with the input at $z=0$, load at $z=L$ and zero source phase:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的总电压相量。取输入端 $z=0$、负载端 $z=L$，源相位为零：

#### Equation

$$
\widetilde V(z)=V^+e^{-j\beta z}+V^-e^{j\beta z}
$$

#### References

- [phasor](formula:phasor)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $\lambda=20\,\mathrm m$, $L=\lambda/4$, $Z_L=125\,\Omega$ and $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find $\widetilde V(z)$. Evaluate the wavenumber and reflection coefficient at the input plane:

#### 中文

已知 $\lambda=20\,\mathrm m$、$L=\lambda/4$、$Z_L=125\,\Omega$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求 $\widetilde V(z)$。把数值代入波数与反射系数，并移至输入参考面：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}=\frac{\pi}{10}\,\mathrm{rad/m},\quad L=\frac{20}{4}=5\,\mathrm m,\quad 2\beta L=\pi\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_L=\frac{125-50}{125+50}=\frac{75}{175}=\frac37\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{V^-}{V^+}=\Gamma_Le^{-2j\beta L}=\frac37e^{-j\pi}=-\frac37\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $R_g=Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $V_g=10\,\mathrm V$ into the source boundary to determine both wave amplitudes:

#### 中文

把 $R_g=Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$V_g=10\,\mathrm V$ 代入源端边界，求两个行波幅值：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}V_g&=\widetilde V(0)+R_g\widetilde I(0)\\&=(V^++V^-)+\frac{R_g}{Z_0}(V^+-V^-)\\&=2V^+\end{aligned}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V^+=\frac{10}{2}=5\,\mathrm V,\qquad V^-=-\frac37(5)=-\frac{15}{7}\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \widetilde V(0)=5-\frac{15}{7}=\frac{20}{7}\,\mathrm V=10\frac{20}{50+20}\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substituting both amplitudes and $\beta$ into the total phasor gives

#### 中文

把两个幅值和 $\beta$ 代回总相量，得

#### Equation

$$
\widetilde V(z)=5e^{-j\pi z/10}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j\pi z/10}\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
\widetilde V(z)=5e^{-j\pi z/10}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j\pi z/10}\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The official solution prints $5e^{-j\beta z}+2.145e^{j\beta z}$ without fixing its origin. For the input-origin, zero-source-phase convention here, the backward term must be negative to satisfy the source divider. With a load origin both coefficients also carry a common $-j$ phase.

#### 中文

官方解写成 $5e^{-j\beta z}+2.145e^{j\beta z}$，却未声明原点。对本页明确的“输入端 $z=0$、源相位 0”约定，反向项必须为负，否则不满足输入分压。若以负载为原点，两项还需统一的 $-j$ 相位。

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Convert your expression for $\widetilde V(z)$ to the time domain $v(z,t)$.

#### 中文

将 $\widetilde V(z)$ 的表达式转换为时域形式 $v(z,t)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the phasor-to-time definition with the crib sheet’s $e^{j\omega t}$ convention:

#### 中文

使用相量的时域定义，与 crib sheet 的 $e^{j\omega t}$ 约定一致：

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{\widetilde V(z)e^{j\omega t}\}
$$

#### References

- [phasor](formula:phasor)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $\widetilde V(z)$ from (b) and $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$, find $v(z,t)$. Substitute $\omega=2\pi f$ and both travelling waves:

#### 中文

已知 (b) 的 $\widetilde V(z)$ 和 $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$，求 $v(z,t)$。把 $\omega=2\pi f$ 和两个行波代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle f=100\times10^3\,\mathrm{Hz}=10^5\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad\omega=2\pi f=2\pi\times10^5\,\mathrm{rad/s}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle v(z,t)=\Re\left\{5e^{j(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)}\right\}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

Taking the real part with $\Re(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$ and retaining the reflected-wave sign gives

#### 中文

用 $\Re(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$ 取实部，保留反向波负号，得

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)-\frac{15}{7}\cos(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)-\frac{15}{7}\cos(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

In order to deliver maximum power to the load, you would need to find a transmission line with the same length $(L=\lambda/4)$, but with a different characteristic impedance. What would the characteristic impedance of this new transmission line need to be?

#### 中文

要向负载传输最大功率，需要找到一根长度相同 $(L=\lambda/4)$、但特性阻抗不同的传输线。新传输线的特性阻抗应是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the quarter-wave transformation from the crib sheet and match the input to the source resistance:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的四分之一波长变换式，并令输入与源电阻匹配：

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g,\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $R_g=50\,\Omega$ and $R_L=125\,\Omega$, find $Z_{0T}$. Substitute and take the positive square root:

#### 中文

已知 $R_g=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=125\,\Omega$，求新线阻抗 $Z_{0T}$。代入并开方：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle 50\,\Omega=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{125\,\Omega}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_{0T}^2=(50\,\Omega)(125\,\Omega)=6250\,\Omega^2,\qquad Z_{0T}=\sqrt{6250}\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T}=79.06\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{0T}=79.06\,\Omega
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Even though the transmission line you chose in part (d) delivers maximum power to the load in steady state, reflections will still occur on the transmission line at the load. How can there be maximum power transfer, but also reflections on the transmission line?

#### 中文

虽然第 (d) 问所选传输线在稳态下向负载传输最大功率，但传输线负载处仍会发生反射。为什么最大功率传输与传输线上的反射可以同时存在？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the local reflection and input-impedance formulas from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的局部反射系数与输入阻抗变换式：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{R_L-Z_{0T}}{R_L+Z_{0T}},\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_L}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)
- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Using $Z_{0T}=\sqrt{50\cdot125}\,\Omega$ from (d), determine whether maximum power transfer and load reflection can coexist. Evaluate the input match and load reflection separately:

#### 中文

已知 (d) 得 $Z_{0T}=\sqrt{50\cdot125}\,\Omega$，求最大功率与负载反射能否共存。分别代入输入端和负载端关系：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(\sqrt{50\cdot125})^2}{125}=50\,\Omega=R_g\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_L=\frac{125-\sqrt{50\cdot125}}{125+\sqrt{50\cdot125}}\approx0.2251\ne0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

These conditions refer to different planes. The crib sheet’s net-power relation shows that a reflected wave can coexist with positive delivered power:

#### 中文

两者参考面不同；用 crib sheet 的净功率式可见，反向波存在时仍向负载传输净功率：

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{|V^+|^2-|V^-|^2}{2Z_{0T}}>0
$$

#### References

- [power](formula:power)

### Step 4

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g\quad\text{with}\quad\Gamma_L\ne0
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g\quad\text{with}\quad\Gamma_L\ne0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Overall input matching can coexist with internal reflections.

#### 中文

输入端整体匹配与内部反射可以同时存在。
