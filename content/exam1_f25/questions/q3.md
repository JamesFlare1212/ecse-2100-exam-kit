## Question

### English

The transmission line in the circuit below has a characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$ and length $L=\frac{\lambda}{4}$. The amplitude of the sinusoidal source voltage is $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$, its wavelength is $\lambda=20\,\mathrm{m}$ and its frequency is $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$.

### 中文

下图电路中的传输线具有特性阻抗 $Z_0=50\Omega$，长度为 $L=\frac{\lambda}{4}$。正弦源电压的幅值为 $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$，其波长为 $\lambda=20\,\mathrm{m}$，频率为 $f=100\,\mathrm{kHz}$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem links quarter-wave impedance transformation, phasors, time-domain waves and power matching.

#### 中文

本题考查四分之一波长阻抗变换，以及相量、时域波形与功率匹配之间的联系。

### Givens

#### English

The original circuit has $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, $L=\lambda/4$, $R_g=50\,\Omega$ and $R_L=125\,\Omega$. The source amplitude is $10\,\mathrm V$, and wavelength and frequency are supplied.

#### 中文

原电路中 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$L=\lambda/4$、$R_g=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=125\,\Omega$；源幅值为 $10\,\mathrm V$，并给出了波长与频率。

### Goal

#### English

Find input impedance and total voltage in phasor and time-domain form, choose a matching line, and explain why local reflections can remain.

#### 中文

求输入阻抗、总电压的相量和时域表达式，选择匹配用电缆，并解释匹配时为何仍可存在局部反射。

### Approach

#### English

Use the quarter-wave property to identify the impedance seen by the source, then determine the waves from the source boundary condition. Treat port matching and reflection at the load as separate questions.

#### 中文

先用四分之一波长的特殊性质看清输入端负载，再由源端边界条件确定行波。将端口是否匹配与负载处是否反射分开讨论。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate $Z_{in}$ for the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算这根传输线的 $Z_{in}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib quarter-wave impedance transformation.

#### 中文

先写原表的四分之一波长阻抗变换性质。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute $Z_{0}=50\,\Omega$ and load $125\,\Omega$, retaining units in the squared impedance.

#### 中文

将本题 $Z_{0}=50\,\Omega$、负载 $125\,\Omega$ 代入，平方的阻抗单位要保留。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(50\,\Omega)^2}{125\,\Omega}=\frac{2500}{125}\,\Omega
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The quarter-wave property gives a real input, which does not match the $50\,\Omega$ source.

#### 中文

由 $\lambda/4$ 的变换性质，输入为实数；与 $50\,\Omega$ 源并不匹配。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=20\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=20\,\Omega
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Write down the phasor for the total voltage wave $\widetilde V(z)$ on the transmission line.

#### 中文

写出传输线上总电压波 $\widetilde V(z)$ 的相量。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the total traveling-wave phasor. Set $z=0$ at the input, $z=L$ at the load and zero source phase.

#### 中文

先写总行波相量原式。明确本页取输入端 $z=0$、负载端 $z=L$，源相位为零。

#### Equation

$$
\widetilde V(z)=V^+e^{-j\beta z}+V^-e^{j\beta z}
$$

#### References

- [phasor](formula:phasor)

### Step 2

#### English

Use the wavelength to compute $\beta$ and length, then the round-trip phase.

#### 中文

由波长求 $\beta$ 与线长，再求往返传播相位。

#### Equation

$$
\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}=\frac{\pi}{10}\,\mathrm{rad/m},\quad L=\frac{20}{4}=5\,\mathrm m,\quad 2\beta L=\pi
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

First calculate the reflection coefficient at the load reference plane.

#### 中文

先计算负载参考面的反射系数。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{125-50}{125+50}=\frac{75}{175}=\frac37
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 4

#### English

Moving to the input plane requires the round-trip phase, making the backward coefficient negative there.

#### 中文

移至输入参考面必须乘往返相位；因此输入面的反向系数是负的。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{V^-}{V^+}=\Gamma_Le^{-2j\beta L}=\frac37e^{-j\pi}=-\frac37
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Write the source voltage boundary. Since $R_g=Z_{0}$, the backward terms cancel, leaving $2V^{+}$ and fixing the forward amplitude.

#### 中文

写源端电压边界。$R_g=Z_{0}$ 时反向项抵消，只剩 $2V^{+}$，由此确定正向幅度。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}V_g&=\widetilde V(0)+R_g\widetilde I(0)\\&=(V^++V^-)+\frac{R_g}{Z_0}(V^+-V^-)\\&=2V^+\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Substitute $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$ and multiply by the input reflection coefficient for the backward amplitude.

#### 中文

代入 $V_g=10\,\mathrm{V}$，并乘输入反射系数求反向幅度。

#### Equation

$$
V^+=\frac{10}{2}=5\,\mathrm V,\qquad V^-=-\frac37(5)=-\frac{15}{7}\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Check input voltage division: the coefficient sum must equal $10\times20/(50+20)$, verifying the backward sign.

#### 中文

核对输入端分压：相量之和应为 $10\times20/(50+20)$。这也检验反向项的符号。

#### Equation

$$
\widetilde V(0)=5-\frac{15}{7}=\frac{20}{7}\,\mathrm V=10\frac{20}{50+20}\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Substitute $\beta$ and both amplitudes into the traveling-wave phasor.

#### 中文

将 $\beta$、正向与反向系数代回总行波相量。

#### Equation

$$
\widetilde V(z)=5e^{-j\pi z/10}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j\pi z/10}\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
\widetilde V(z)=5e^{-j\pi z/10}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j\pi z/10}\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The official solution prints $5e^{-j\beta z}+2.145e^{j\beta z}$ without fixing its origin. For the input-origin, zero-source-phase convention here, the backward term must be negative to satisfy the source divider. With a load origin both coefficients also carry a common $-j$ phase.

#### 中文

官方解写成 $5e^{-j\beta z}+2.145e^{j\beta z}$，却未声明原点。对本页明确的“输入端 $z=0$、源相位 0”约定，反向项必须为负，否则不满足输入分压。若以负载为原点，两项还需统一的 $-j$ 相位。

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Convert your expression for $\widetilde V(z)$ to the time domain $v(z,t)$.

#### 中文

将 $\widetilde V(z)$ 的表达式转换为时域形式 $v(z,t)$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the phasor-to-time definition using the same $e^{j\omega t}$ convention as above.

#### 中文

先写相量到瞬时值的定义，采用与前问相同的 $e^{j\omega t}$ 约定。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{\widetilde V(z)e^{j\omega t}\}
$$

#### References

- [phasor](formula:phasor)

### Step 2

#### English

Convert $100\,\mathrm{kHz}$ to $\mathrm{Hz}$ and calculate angular frequency.

#### 中文

将 $100\,\mathrm{kHz}$ 转为 $\mathrm{Hz}$，再计算角频率。

#### Equation

$$
f=100\times10^3\,\mathrm{Hz}=10^5\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad\omega=2\pi f=2\pi\times10^5\,\mathrm{rad/s}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute both preceding waves and distribute the time factor.

#### 中文

把上一问两个行波全部代入，并把时间因子分别乘进去。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\left\{5e^{j(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)}-\frac{15}{7}e^{j(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)}\right\}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Use Euler's formula when taking the real part: cosine is real, while the sine term multiplied by $j$ is imaginary. Apply the rule separately to each real amplitude, retaining its original sign.

#### 中文

相量取实部使用欧拉公式：余弦是实部，正弦前带 $j$，属于虚部。对每个实数幅度分别使用这条规则，幅度原有的正负号都保留。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}e^{j\theta}&=\cos\theta+j\sin\theta\\\operatorname{Re}\{A e^{j\theta}\}&=A\cos\theta\qquad(A\in\mathbb R)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Apply $\operatorname{Re}(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$ term by term, retaining the negative real amplitude of the second wave.

#### 中文

逐项使用 $\operatorname{Re}(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$；第二项的负实幅度必须保留。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)-\frac{15}{7}\cos(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(2\pi10^5t-\pi z/10)-\frac{15}{7}\cos(2\pi10^5t+\pi z/10)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

In order to deliver maximum power to the load, you would need to find a transmission line with the same length $(L=\lambda/4)$, but with a different characteristic impedance. What would the characteristic impedance of this new transmission line need to be?

#### 中文

要向负载传输最大功率，需要找到一根长度相同 $(L=\lambda/4)$、但特性阻抗不同的传输线。新传输线的特性阻抗应是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the resistive maximum-power matching condition and the crib quarter-wave relation.

#### 中文

先写最大功率传输的实阻抗匹配条件，以及原表四分之一波长变换式。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g,\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Keep the length $\lambda/4$ and solve for transformer characteristic impedance $Z_{0T}$. Substitute source and load values.

#### 中文

保持线长 $\lambda/4$，待选的是变换线的特性阻抗 $Z_{0T}$。把源和负载代入。

#### Equation

$$
50\,\Omega=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{125\,\Omega}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Multiply by $125\,\Omega$ and take the positive square root.

#### 中文

两边乘 $125\,\Omega$，再取正平方根。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T}^2=(50\,\Omega)(125\,\Omega)=6250\,\Omega^2,\qquad Z_{0T}=\sqrt{6250}\,\Omega
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The resulting characteristic impedance presents a real $50\,\Omega$ input to the source.

#### 中文

所得特性阻抗使源看到 $50\,\Omega$ 的实输入阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T}=79.06\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{0T}=79.06\,\Omega
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Even though the transmission line you chose in part (d) delivers maximum power to the load in steady state, reflections will still occur on the transmission line at the load. How can there be maximum power transfer, but also reflections on the transmission line?

#### 中文

虽然第 (d) 问所选传输线在稳态下向负载传输最大功率，但传输线负载处仍会发生反射。为什么最大功率传输与传输线上的反射可以同时存在？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the local load reflection coefficient and the input impedance of the whole network; they describe different reference planes.

#### 中文

先写负载局部反射系数，再写整个网络向源呈现的输入阻抗；两者的参考位置不同。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{R_L-Z_{0T}}{R_L+Z_{0T}},\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_L}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)
- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute the chosen $Z_{0T}$ and explicitly verify the source input match.

#### 中文

将上一问选出的 $Z_{0T}$ 代入输入阻抗，明确验证源端匹配。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(\sqrt{50\cdot125})^2}{125}=50\,\Omega=R_g
$$

### Step 3

#### English

At the load the transformer impedance still differs from $125\,\Omega$, so the local reflection coefficient remains nonzero.

#### 中文

同一个变换线在负载处仍与 $125\,\Omega$ 不相等，所以局部反射系数非零。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{125-\sqrt{50\cdot125}}{125+\sqrt{50\cdot125}}\approx0.2251\ne0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Forward and backward waves jointly produce the matched input $V/I$ ratio. Net accepted power is conserved along the lossless line despite the internal backward wave.

#### 中文

无损线上的正反波共同构成匹配的输入电压电流比。接受的净功率沿线守恒，内部反向波并不等于没有功率送入负载。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{|V^+|^2-|V^-|^2}{2Z_{0T}}>0
$$

#### References

- [power](formula:power)

### Step 5

#### English

Maximum power transfer can therefore coexist with load reflections.

#### 中文

因此最大功率传输与负载存在反射可以同时成立。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g\quad\text{with}\quad\Gamma_L\ne0
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g\quad\text{with}\quad\Gamma_L\ne0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Overall input matching can coexist with internal reflections.

#### 中文

输入端整体匹配与内部反射可以同时存在。
