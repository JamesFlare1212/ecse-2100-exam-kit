## Question

### English

You want to connect your television to a cable TV feed point located on the opposite side of your house. The only coaxial cable ($\mathrm{T1}$) you have available for this purpose has the following properties:
• Length: $30\,\mathrm{m}$
• Characteristic impedance: $75\,\Omega$
• Velocity factor: $0.85$

The cable TV feed point consists of a voltage source $V_g$ with an amplitude of $1\,\mathrm{V}$, source impedance $R_g$ of $75\,\Omega$ and frequency $f$ of $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$. The television’s load impedance $R_L$ is also $75\,\Omega$. A circuit diagram illustrating this setup is shown below.

### 中文

你想把电视连接到位于房屋另一侧的有线电视信号接入点。你手头唯一可用于此目的的同轴电缆（$\mathrm{T1}$）具有以下性质：
• 长度：$30\,\mathrm{m}$
• 特性阻抗：$75\,\Omega$
• 速度因子：$0.85$

有线电视接入点由电压源 $V_g$ 构成，其幅值为 $1\,\mathrm{V}$，源阻抗 $R_g$ 为 $75\,\Omega$，频率 $f$ 为 $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$。电视的负载阻抗 $R_L$ 也是 $75\,\Omega$。下图给出了这一连接的电路图。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects impedance matching, propagation speed and low-loss attenuation in a cable-TV link.

#### 中文

本题把传输线匹配、传播速度和低损耗衰减放在同一条有线电视连接中考查。

### Givens

#### English

The cable is $30\,\mathrm m$ long, with velocity factor $0.85$ and frequency $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$. Initially the source, line and load are all $75\,\Omega$; the attenuation part also supplies the resistance per unit length.

#### 中文

电缆长 $30\,\mathrm m$，速度因子为 $0.85$，频率为 $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$；初始情况下源、线和负载均为 $75\,\Omega$。衰减小问另给出单位长度电阻。

### Goal

#### English

Check reflection, find delay, wavelength and the incident voltage at the load, then find the shortest matching length for a different cable.

#### 中文

判断反射，求延迟、波长和到达负载的入射电压，再研究换用另一种电缆后的最短匹配长度。

### Approach

#### English

Start by comparing load and line impedances, then convert velocity factor to speed. Separate the launch voltage divider from propagation loss, and use the periodicity of input impedance for the final part.

#### 中文

先比较负载与特性阻抗，再把速度因子换成波速。求电压幅值时分别考虑源端分压和沿线衰减；最后利用输入阻抗随电长度重复的性质。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

If you use the provided cable to connect the cable TV feed point to your television, will there be a reflection at the load? Explain why or why not.

#### 中文

如果用所给电缆将有线电视接入点连接到电视，负载处是否会发生反射？解释原因。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib load reflection coefficient, comparing load impedance with line characteristic impedance.

#### 中文

先写原表的负载反射系数，比较的是负载阻抗和线的特性阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Both the television load and cable are $75\,\Omega$. Substitute them into numerator and denominator.

#### 中文

本题电视负载与电缆均为 $75\,\Omega$，逐项代入分子和分母。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{75\,\Omega-75\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega+75\,\Omega}=\frac0{150\,\Omega}=0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The reflected voltage amplitude is the incident amplitude times $\Gamma_L$, so the load produces no backward wave.

#### 中文

反射电压幅度等于入射幅度乘 $\Gamma_L$，因此不存在由负载产生的反向波。

#### Equation

$$
V_0^-=\Gamma_LV_0^+=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The load is matched with no reflection; the equal source impedance also avoids source mismatch.

#### 中文

结论是负载匹配、无反射；源阻抗相同也避免源端失配。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=0
$$

### Answer

$$
\Gamma_L=0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

There is no reflection.

#### 中文

无反射。

## Part b

### Question

#### English

What is the time delay of the coaxial cable?

#### 中文

这根同轴电缆的时间延迟是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the wave-speed and propagation-time relations; velocity factor is relative to vacuum light speed.

#### 中文

先写波速和传播时间的关系，速度因子是相对真空光速的比值。

#### Equation

$$
u=\mathrm{vf}\,u_c,\qquad T_d=\frac Lu
$$

#### References

- [velocity](formula:velocity)
- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute 0.85 and $3\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}$ to obtain the cable propagation speed first.

#### 中文

将 0.85 和 $3\times10^{8}\,\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}$ 代入，先得到电缆内的实际传播速度。

#### Equation

$$
u=0.85(3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})=2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Divide length by speed; meters cancel, leaving seconds.

#### 中文

长度除以波速，米约去后结果为秒。

#### Equation

$$
T_d=\frac{30\,\mathrm m}{2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}=1.17647\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm s
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Convert using $1\,\mathrm{ns}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s}$. This is one-way time, so do not multiply by two.

#### 中文

利用 $1\,\mathrm{ns}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s}$ 转换单位；这是单程时间，不再乘 2。

#### Equation

$$
T_d=117.65\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### Answer

$$
T_d=117.65\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

What is the wavelength of the signal on the coaxial cable

#### 中文

信号在这根同轴电缆上的波长是多少

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib wavelength relation, using the cable wave speed.

#### 中文

先写原表的波长关系，使用电缆内部波速。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac uf,\qquad u=\mathrm{vf}\,u_c
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [velocity](formula:velocity)

### Step 2

#### English

Convert $\mathrm{MHz}$ to $\mathrm{Hz}$ and state the previously determined speed.

#### 中文

把频率由 $\mathrm{MHz}$ 换成 $\mathrm{Hz}$，并写出上问得到的波速。

#### Equation

$$
f=250\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad u=2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute numerical values and units into the wavelength equation.

#### 中文

将数值及单位代入波长公式。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac{2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}{250\times10^6\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}}=\frac{2.55}{2.50}\,\mathrm m
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Simplify to the cable wavelength, rather than the vacuum wavelength of $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$.

#### 中文

化简得到电缆内波长，而非真空中的 $1.20\,\mathrm{m}$。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=1.02\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
\lambda=1.02\,\mathrm m
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Assuming that the cable can be considered low-loss and has a per-length resistance of $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm{m}$, calculate the amplitude of the incident voltage wave at the load.

#### 中文

假设可将电缆视为低损耗线，且单位长度电阻为 $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm{m}$，计算负载处入射电压波的幅值。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib low-loss attenuation and traveling-wave envelope, using the model with negligible shunt conductance.

#### 中文

先写原表的低损耗衰减式与行波包络；本题使用可忽略并联漏电导的模型。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=\frac r{2Z_0},\qquad |V^+(z)|=|V_0^+|e^{-\alpha z}
$$

#### References

- [attenuation](formula:attenuation)
- [lossy-wave](formula:lossy-wave)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute resistance per length and characteristic impedance to calculate $\alpha$.

#### 中文

代入每单位长度电阻和特性阻抗，求出 $\alpha$。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=\frac{1\,\Omega/\mathrm m}{2(75\,\Omega)}=\frac1{150}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=6.66667\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The launched amplitude is not the $1\,\mathrm{V}$ source voltage; apply the source divider first.

#### 中文

负载入射波的起始幅度不是源电压 $1\,\mathrm{V}$，必须先做源端分压。

#### Equation

$$
V_0^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0}=(1\,\mathrm V)\frac{75\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega+75\,\Omega}=0.500\,\mathrm V
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 4

#### English

At $30\,\mathrm{m}$, the attenuation exponent $\alpha L$ is dimensionless and equals 0.2.

#### 中文

传播到 $30\,\mathrm{m}$，衰减指数 $\alpha L$ 是无量纲的 0.2。

#### Equation

$$
\alpha L=\frac1{150\,\mathrm m}(30\,\mathrm m)=0.2,\qquad |V^+(L)|=(0.500\,\mathrm V)e^{-0.2}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Evaluate the exponential to obtain the incident load amplitude. Since $\Gamma_L=0$, there is no additional reflected term.

#### 中文

求指数值得到负载处的入射电压幅度；$\Gamma_L=0$，所以没有额外反射项。

#### Equation

$$
|V^+(30\,\mathrm m)|=0.5e^{-0.2}=0.4094\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
|V^+(30\,\mathrm m)|=0.5e^{-0.2}=0.4094\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

If the coaxial cable’s characteristic impedance were instead $50\Omega$, what is the minimum length of cable $(L>0)$ needed to be to ensure maximum power transfer from the source to the load?

#### 中文

如果同轴电缆的特性阻抗改为 $50\Omega$，为保证从源到负载的最大功率传输，所需电缆的最小长度 $(L>0)$ 是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Maximum power transfer requires the input seen by the source to match its $75\,\Omega$ resistance. Use the exam’s lossless matching approximation.

#### 中文

最大功率传输要求源看到的输入阻抗与 $75\,\Omega$ 源电阻匹配；按原题采用无损匹配近似。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g=75\,\Omega
$$

### Step 2

#### English

The crib half-wave property repeats the load at the input even with a $50\,\Omega$ cable in between.

#### 中文

原表的半波线性质会将负载原样重复到输入端，不论中间电缆是否为 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
L=n\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L=75\,\Omega
$$

#### References

- [half](formula:half)

### Step 3

#### English

To verify why a half-wave line repeats its load, start from the crib's general input impedance. Write $t=\tan(\beta L)$ to shorten the algebra without changing its meaning.

#### 中文

要验证半波线为何重复负载，先写出原表的一般输入阻抗。用 $t=\tan(\beta L)$ 缩短符号，但不改变它的物理意义。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0t}{Z_0+jZ_Lt},\qquad t=\tan(\beta L)
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 4

#### English

Set the input impedance equal to this problem's load impedance. Multiply both sides by the denominator and expand; the common term $Z_0Z_L$ then cancels.

#### 中文

令输入阻抗等于本题的负载阻抗。两边乘分母，再展开左右两侧；共同的 $Z_0Z_L$ 项随后相消。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_L(Z_0+jZ_Lt)&=Z_0(Z_L+jZ_0t)\\Z_0Z_L+jZ_L^2t&=Z_0Z_L+jZ_0^2t\\jZ_L^2t&=jZ_0^2t\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Check the shortest positive length: setting $Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L$ in the general equation with $Z_0\ne Z_L$ requires $\tan(\beta L)=0$.

#### 中文

核对最短正长度：代入通用输入阻抗并令 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L$，因 $Z_0\ne Z_L$，只能使 $\tan(\beta L)=0$。

#### Equation

$$
j(Z_0^2-Z_L^2)\tan(\beta L)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\beta L=n\pi
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 6

#### English

The unchanged velocity factor and frequency give $\lambda=1.02\,\mathrm{m}$. Choose the smallest positive integer $n=1$.

#### 中文

沿用同一速度因子与频率，波长仍为 $1.02\,\mathrm{m}$，$n$ 取最小正整数 1。

#### Equation

$$
L_{\min}=\frac{\lambda}{2}=\frac{1.02\,\mathrm m}{2}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

This is the half-wave impedance-repeat length, rather than a quarter wavelength that transforms $75\,\Omega$ into a different resistance.

#### 中文

这是重复阻抗的半波长度，不是把 $75\,\Omega$ 变换成另一电阻的四分之一波长。

#### Equation

$$
L_{\min}=0.510\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
L_{\min}=0.510\,\mathrm m
$$
