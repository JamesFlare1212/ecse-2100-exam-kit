## Question

### English

You want to connect your television to a cable TV feed point located on the opposite side of your house. The only coaxial cable ($\mathrm{T1}$) you have available for this purpose has the following properties:
• Length: $30\,\mathrm{m}$
• Characteristic impedance: $75\,\Omega$
• Velocity factor: $0.85$

The cable TV feed point consists of a voltage source $V_g$ with an amplitude of $1\,\mathrm{V}$, source impedance $R_g$ of $75\,\Omega$ and frequency $f$ of $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$. The television’s load impedance $R_L$ is also $75\,\Omega$. A circuit diagram illustrating this setup is shown below.

### 中文

你想把电视连接到位于房屋另一侧的有线电视信号接入点。你手头唯一可用于此目的的同轴电缆（$\mathrm{T1}$）具有以下性质：
• 长度：$30\,\mathrm{m}$
• 特性阻抗：$75\,\Omega$
• 速度因子：$0.85$

有线电视接入点由电压源 $V_g$ 构成，其幅值为 $1\,\mathrm{V}$，源阻抗 $R_g$ 为 $75\,\Omega$，频率 $f$ 为 $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$。电视的负载阻抗 $R_L$ 也是 $75\,\Omega$。下图给出了这一连接的电路图。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects impedance matching, propagation speed and low-loss attenuation in a cable-TV link.

#### 中文

本题把传输线匹配、传播速度和低损耗衰减放在同一条有线电视连接中考查。

### Givens

#### English

The cable is $30\,\mathrm m$ long, with velocity factor $0.85$ and frequency $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$. Initially the source, line and load are all $75\,\Omega$; the attenuation part also supplies the resistance per unit length.

#### 中文

电缆长 $30\,\mathrm m$，速度因子为 $0.85$，频率为 $250\,\mathrm{MHz}$；初始情况下源、线和负载均为 $75\,\Omega$。衰减小问另给出单位长度电阻。

### Goal

#### English

Check reflection, find delay, wavelength and the incident voltage at the load, then find the shortest matching length for a different cable.

#### 中文

判断反射，求延迟、波长和到达负载的入射电压，再研究换用另一种电缆后的最短匹配长度。

### Approach

#### English

Start by comparing load and line impedances, then convert velocity factor to speed. Separate the launch voltage divider from propagation loss, and use the periodicity of input impedance for the final part.

#### 中文

先比较负载与特性阻抗，再把速度因子换成波速。求电压幅值时分别考虑源端分压和沿线衰减；最后利用输入阻抗随电长度重复的性质。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

If you use the provided cable to connect the cable TV feed point to your television, will there be a reflection at the load? Explain why or why not.

#### 中文

如果用所给电缆将有线电视接入点连接到电视，负载处是否会发生反射？解释原因。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [load-reflection formula](formula:reflection) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[负载反射系数](formula:reflection)：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{Z_L-Z_0}{Z_L+Z_0}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_L=Z_0=75\,\Omega$, determine whether the load reflects. Substitute both impedances:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_L=Z_0=75\,\Omega$，求是否有负载反射。把两阻抗代入：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{75\,\Omega-75\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega+75\,\Omega}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=0
$$

### Answer

$$
\Gamma_L=0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

There is no reflection.

#### 中文

无反射。

## Part b

### Question

#### English

What is the time delay of the coaxial cable?

#### 中文

这根同轴电缆的时间延迟是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s [wave speed](formula:velocity) and Lecture 04’s [transit-time relation](formula:bounce):

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[波速公式](formula:velocity)与 Lecture 04 的[传播时间关系](formula:bounce)：

#### Equation

$$
u=\mathrm{vf}\,u_c,\qquad T_d=\frac Lu
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given a velocity factor of $0.85$ and $L=30\,\mathrm m$, find the one-way delay. Substitute:

#### 中文

已知速度因子为 $0.85$、$L=30\,\mathrm m$，求单程延迟。把数值代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle u=0.85(3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})=2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle T_d=\frac{30\,\mathrm m}{2.55\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}=1.17647\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm s\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Converting to nanoseconds gives

#### 中文

换算为纳秒，得

#### Equation

$$
T_d=117.65\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### Answer

$$
T_d=117.65\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Assuming that the cable can be considered low-loss and has a per-length resistance of $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm{m}$, calculate the amplitude of the incident voltage wave at the load.

#### 中文

假设可将电缆视为低损耗线，且单位长度电阻为 $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm{m}$，计算负载处入射电压波的幅值。

### Step 1

#### English

Neglecting shunt leakage, use the [low-loss attenuation formula](formula:attenuation) and [travelling-wave envelope](formula:lossy-wave) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

忽略并联漏电导，使用 crib sheet 的[低损耗衰减式](formula:attenuation)与[行波包络](formula:lossy-wave)：

#### Equation

$$
\alpha=\frac r{2Z_0},\qquad |V^+(z)|=|V_0^+|e^{-\alpha z}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm m$, $Z_0=R_g=75\,\Omega$ and $V_g=1\,\mathrm V$, find the incident amplitude at the load. Evaluate attenuation and the [launch voltage](formula:bounce) using Lecture 04:

#### 中文

已知 $r=1\,\Omega/\mathrm m$、$Z_0=R_g=75\,\Omega$、$V_g=1\,\mathrm V$，求负载入射幅值。先代入衰减式，再按 Lecture 04 [分压](formula:bounce)：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \alpha=\frac{1\,\Omega/\mathrm m}{2(75\,\Omega)}=\frac1{150}\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}=6.66667\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Np/m}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_0^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0}=(1\,\mathrm V)\frac{75\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega+75\,\Omega}=0.500\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

Substitute $L=30\,\mathrm m$ and the launch amplitude into the propagation envelope:

#### 中文

把 $L=30\,\mathrm m$ 和初始幅值代入传播包络：

#### Equation

$$
\alpha L=\frac1{150\,\mathrm m}(30\,\mathrm m)=0.2,\qquad |V^+(L)|=(0.500\,\mathrm V)e^{-0.2}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
|V^+(30\,\mathrm m)|=0.5e^{-0.2}=0.4094\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
|V^+(30\,\mathrm m)|=0.5e^{-0.2}=0.4094\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

If the coaxial cable’s characteristic impedance were instead $50\Omega$, what is the minimum length of cable $(L>0)$ needed to be to ensure maximum power transfer from the source to the load?

#### 中文

如果同轴电缆的特性阻抗改为 $50\Omega$，为保证从源到负载的最大功率传输，所需电缆的最小长度 $(L>0)$ 是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Using the lossless matching approximation for this part, apply the [input-impedance formula](formula:zin) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

按本问的无损匹配近似，使用 crib sheet 的[输入阻抗公式](formula:zin)：

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0t}{Z_0+jZ_Lt},\qquad t=\tan(\beta L)
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $Z_L=R_g=75\,\Omega$, find the shortest positive matching length. Set $Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L$ in the [input-impedance formula](formula:zin) and rearrange:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$Z_L=R_g=75\,\Omega$，求匹配的最短正长度。令 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L$，代入[输入阻抗式](formula:zin)并整理：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=R_g=75\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}Z_L(Z_0+jZ_Lt)&=Z_0(Z_L+jZ_0t)\\Z_0Z_L+jZ_L^2t&=Z_0Z_L+jZ_0^2t\\jZ_L^2t&=jZ_0^2t\end{aligned}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle j(Z_0^2-Z_L^2)\tan(\beta L)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\beta L=n\pi\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

Since $Z_0\ne Z_L$, $\beta L=n\pi$. Using [half-wave impedance repetition](formula:half), substitute $\lambda=1.02\,\mathrm m$ from (c) and take $n=1$:

#### 中文

因 $Z_0\ne Z_L$，故 $\beta L=n\pi$。按[半波阻抗重复性质](formula:half)，把 (c) 的 $\lambda=1.02\,\mathrm m$ 代入，取 $n=1$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle L=n\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L=75\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle L_{\min}=\frac{\lambda}{2}=\frac{1.02\,\mathrm m}{2}\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 4

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
L_{\min}=0.510\,\mathrm m
$$

### Answer

$$
L_{\min}=0.510\,\mathrm m
$$
