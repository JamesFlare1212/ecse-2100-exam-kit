## Question

### English

You have been asked to match the load impedance $Z_L=150+j75\,\Omega$ to a feedline with characteristic impedance $Z_0=75\,\Omega$ by using an open-circuited transmission line stub. A diagram of the single-stub matching circuit is shown below. A Smith chart is attached to the back of your exam for recording your answers. A ruler will also be provided.

### 中文

要求使用一段开路传输线支节，将负载阻抗 $Z_L=150+j75\,\Omega$ 匹配到特性阻抗为 $Z_0=75\,\Omega$ 的馈线。单支节匹配电路如下图所示。试卷末尾附有 Smith 图，供你记录答案，并将提供直尺。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem uses Smith-chart impedance/admittance conversion and a parallel open-circuit matching stub.

#### 中文

本题考查 Smith 图上的阻抗与导纳转换，以及并联开路支节匹配。

### Givens

#### English

The load is $Z_L=(150+j75)\,\Omega$, the lines have characteristic impedance $75\,\Omega$, and the stub is open-circuited.

#### 中文

负载为 $Z_L=(150+j75)\,\Omega$，馈线与支节的特性阻抗为 $75\,\Omega$，支节终端开路。

### Goal

#### English

Locate the normalized load impedance and admittance, then find the stub position and length with the construction shown on the chart.

#### 中文

依次找出归一化负载阻抗、导纳、支节接入距离和支节长度，并在图上说明过程。

### Approach

#### English

Admittances add directly in a parallel network. Convert the load to admittance, rotate along its constant-reflection circle until the real part matches, then cancel the remaining imaginary part with the open stub.

#### 中文

并联结构用导纳相加最直接。先把负载转为导纳，沿等反射系数圆转到实部匹配的位置，再让开路支节补偿剩余的虚部。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the normalized load impedance and plot it on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

计算归一化负载阻抗，并在 Smith 图上标出。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with normalization. The load impedance $z$ is dimensionless and referenced to the feedline $Z_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写归一化定义。这里 $z$ 为无量纲负载阻抗，参考阻抗取该题馈线的 $Z_{0}$。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Divide the real and imaginary parts by $Z_{0}$ separately, preserving the positive reactance.

#### 中文

把复负载的实部和虚部分别除以 $Z_{0}$，保留电抗的正号。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{(150+j75)\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega}=2+j
$$

### Step 3

#### English

On the impedance chart intersect the $r=\operatorname{Re}(z_L)$ circle with the positive-$x$ reactance arc; verify the plotted coordinate with the reflection coefficient.

#### 中文

在 Smith 阻抗图中找 $r=\operatorname{Re}(z_L)$ 圆与 $x=\operatorname{Im}(z_L)>0$ 弧的交点；也可用反射系数验证坐标。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_z=\frac{z_L-1}{z_L+1}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 4

#### English

Treat the complex denominator by multiplying numerator and denominator by $3-j$. Use $j^2=-1$; the cross terms cancel in the denominator.

#### 中文

复数分母不能直接按实数除法计算。分子和分母同乘 $3-j$，利用 $j^2=-1$，交叉虚部在分母中相消。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\frac{1+j}{3+j}&=\frac{(1+j)(3-j)}{(3+j)(3-j)}\\(1+j)(3-j)&=3-j+3j-j^2=4+2j\\(3+j)(3-j)&=9-3j+3j-j^2=10\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Substitute the normalized complex impedance to obtain its $\Gamma$-plane coordinate, in the upper half-plane.

#### 中文

将已归一化的复数代入，得到该点的 $\Gamma$ 平面坐标；它位于上半平面。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_z=\frac{(2+j)-1}{(2+j)+1}=\frac{1+j}{3+j}=0.4+j0.2
$$

### Answer

$$
z_L=2+j
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Plot the normalized load admittance on the Smith chart and show how you determined it from the plotted normalized load impedance. Write down the normalized load admittance you found from the Smith chart.

#### 中文

在 Smith 图上标出归一化负载导纳，并展示如何从已标出的归一化负载阻抗确定它。写出从 Smith 图上读到的归一化负载导纳。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with normalized admittance and substitute $Y=1/Z$ to show why it equals $1/z_L$.

#### 中文

先写归一化导纳定义，再代入 $Y=1/Z$，说明为什么结果是 $1/z_L$。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac{Y_L}{Y_0}=\frac{1/Z_L}{1/Z_0}=\frac{Z_0}{Z_L}=\frac1{z_L}
$$

### Step 2

#### English

The conjugate of $u+jv$ is $u-jv$. Their cross terms cancel, and the final term $-j^2v^2$ becomes positive $v^2$.

#### 中文

复数 $u+jv$ 的共轭数是 $u-jv$。两者相乘时，两个交叉项一正一负而相消，最后的 $-j^2v^2$ 变成正的 $v^2$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(u+jv)(u-jv)&=u^2-juv+juv-j^2v^2\\&=u^2+v^2\\\frac1{u+jv}&=\frac{u-jv}{u^2+v^2}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Take the complex reciprocal by multiplying by the conjugate; the denominator becomes the sum of squared components.

#### 中文

复数取倒数要乘共轭数；分母变成实部平方加虚部平方。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac1{2+j}=\frac{2-j}{(2+j)(2-j)}=\frac{2-j}{5}=0.4-j0.2
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Reading admittance on the same impedance grid requires reversing the displayed point through the center, a $180^\circ$ rotation; the actual load reflection does not change sign.

#### 中文

在同一阻抗网格上读取导纳时，表示坐标应关于图心反转，即旋转 $180^\circ$。这不是让实际负载的反射改变符号。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_y=\frac{y_L-1}{y_L+1}=\frac{1-z_L}{1+z_L}=-\Gamma_z
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 5

#### English

Move to the diametrically opposite point on the same magnitude circle and read conductance and susceptance; the algebraic reciprocal verifies the chart.

#### 中文

因此沿同一个等模圆转到对径点，再读出电导与电纳；代数倒数与图上结果一致。

#### Equation

$$
y_L=\frac1{2+j}=\frac{2-j}{(2+j)(2-j)}=\frac{2-j}{5}=0.4-j0.2
$$

### Answer

$$
y_L=0.4-j0.2
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the distance $d_1$ (in terms of wavelength) from the load where the open-circuited stub should be placed, and give the input admittance $y_d$ of the transmission line at that point. Show all necessary work on the Smith chart for how you determined this value.

#### 中文

计算开路支节应放置在距负载多远的位置 $d_1$（用波长表示），并给出该处传输线的输入导纳 $y_d$。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一数值所需的全部过程。

### Step 1

#### English

Start from the crib's lossless input impedance, then divide by the reference impedance. The line and load use the same reference impedance.

#### 中文

先写公式表中的无损线输入阻抗，再除以参考阻抗得到归一化输入阻抗。主线和负载使用同一个参考阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in},d}&=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta d)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta d)}\\z_d&=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in},d}}{Z_0}=\frac{z_L+jt}{1+jz_Lt}\\t&=\tan(\beta d),\qquad\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Admittance is the reciprocal of impedance. Divide numerator and denominator by $z_L$ to express the result using the previously calculated load admittance $y_L=1/z_L$.

#### 中文

导纳是阻抗的倒数。分子、分母同时除以 $z_L$，就能用已经求出的负载导纳 $y_L=1/z_L$ 表示。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_d&=\frac1{z_d}=\frac{1+jz_Lt}{z_L+jt}\\&=\frac{1/z_L+jt}{1+jt/z_L}\\&=\frac{y_L+jt}{1+jy_Lt}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Write load admittance as $g+jb$ and express the denominator with real part $1-bt$ and imaginary part $gt$.

#### 中文

把负载导纳分成 $g+jb$，将分母写成实部 $1-bt$ 与虚部 $gt$。

#### Equation

$$
g=0.4,\quad b=-0.2,\quad |y_L|^2=0.20
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Substitute $y_L=g+jb$ into numerator and denominator. Since $j\cdot j=-1$, the denominator has real part $1-bt$ and imaginary part $gt$.

#### 中文

把 $y_L=g+jb$ 代入分子和分母。分母里的 $j\cdot j=-1$，因此实部是 $1-bt$，虚部是 $gt$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_L+jt&=g+j(b+t)\\1+jy_Lt&=1+j(g+jb)t\\&=(1-bt)+jgt\\y_d&=\frac{g+j(b+t)}{(1-bt)+jgt}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's conjugate. The denominator becomes real, allowing the real and imaginary parts to be read separately.

#### 中文

分子和分母同乘分母的共轭数。这样分母变成实数，才可以分别读取实部和虚部。

#### Equation

$$
y_d=\frac{[g+j(b+t)][(1-bt)-jgt]}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Expand the numerator into real and imaginary terms. The real-part terms $-gbt$ and $gbt$ cancel, leaving $g(1+t^2)$.

#### 中文

展开分子的实部和虚部。实部中的两项 $-gbt$ 与 $gbt$ 恰好相消，留下 $g(1+t^2)$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}N_{\mathrm{real}}&=g(1-bt)+(b+t)gt\\&=g-gbt+gbt+gt^2\\&=g(1+t^2)\\N_{\mathrm{imag}}&=(b+t)(1-bt)-g^2t\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Multiply by the denominator conjugate and take the real part. Matching conductance to $Y_{0}$ requires normalized real part one.

#### 中文

乘分母共轭数，单独取实部。匹配实部要求实际输入电导等于 $Y_{0}$，即归一化实部等于 1。

#### Equation

$$
\Re(y_d)=\frac{g(1+t^2)}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}=1
$$

### Step 8

#### English

Expand the denominator and multiply the matching condition by it. Move $g+gt^2$ to the right and group by powers of $t$.

#### 中文

先展开分母，再将匹配条件两边同乘分母。最后把左边的 $g+gt^2$ 移到右边，并按 $t$ 的幂次整理。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2&=1-2bt+(b^2+g^2)t^2\\g+gt^2&=1-2bt+(b^2+g^2)t^2\\0&=(g^2+b^2-g)t^2-2bt+(1-g)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Evaluate the three quadratic coefficients, then divide both sides by negative $0.2$. Applying the same operation to both sides preserves the equality and changes all three coefficient signs.

#### 中文

逐项计算二次方程的三个系数，再同除以负的 $0.2$。整条等式两边都作相同运算，所以等式成立，三个系数的符号也都会改变。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}g^2+b^2-g&=0.4^2+(-0.2)^2-0.4=-0.2\\-2b&=0.4,\qquad1-g=0.6\\-0.2t^2+0.4t+0.6&=0\\t^2-2t-3&=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 10

#### English

Find two numbers with product $-3$ and sum $-2$: they are $-3$ and $1$. After factoring, the product is zero when either factor is zero.

#### 中文

找出乘积为 $-3$、和为 $-2$ 的两个数，即 $-3$ 与 $1$。分解后，只要一个因子为零，乘积就为零。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}t^2-2t-3&=(t-3)(t+1)=0\\t-3=0&\quad\text{or}\quad t+1=0\\t_1=3,&\qquad t_2=-1\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 11

#### English

List both $t$ values, then choose branches satisfying $0\le d<\lambda/2$ using $t=\tan(2\pi d/\lambda)$.

#### 中文

列出两个 $t$ 值，再由 $t=\tan(2\pi d/\lambda)$ 选 $0\le d<\lambda/2$ 的分支。

#### Equation

$$
t_1=3,\quad t_2=-1
$$

### Step 12

#### English

Write the tangent period before recovering length. The first root lies in the first quadrant. The second has a negative principal value, so add $\pi$ to place it in the nonnegative half-wavelength interval.

#### 中文

由正切反解长度前，先写清周期。第一根在第一象限；第二根的主值为负，要加 $\pi$ 才落在非负的半波长度区间。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}2\pi d_1/\lambda&=\arctan3\approx1.249046\\d_1/\lambda&=1.249046/(2\pi)\approx0.198792\\2\pi d_2/\lambda&=\arctan(-1)+\pi\\&=-\pi/4+\pi=3\pi/4\\d_2/\lambda&=(3\pi/4)/(2\pi)=0.375000\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 13

#### English

Insert $t_1=3$, combine numerator and denominator, then multiply by the conjugate. Divide the real and imaginary numerator terms by the real denominator to obtain the first admittance.

#### 中文

第一根 $t_1=3$ 代回后，先合并分子和分母，再同乘共轭数。实部和虚部分别除以实分母，得到第一个交点的导纳。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,1}&=\frac{0.4+j(-0.2+3)}{(1+0.2\cdot3)+j(0.4\cdot3)}\\&=\frac{0.4+j2.8}{1.6+j1.2}\\&=\frac{(0.4+j2.8)(1.6-j1.2)}{1.6^2+1.2^2}\\&=\frac{4+j4}{4}=1+j\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 14

#### English

Substitute $t_2=-1$ independently. Keep track of the two negative signs in the denominator's real part and the negative imaginary part.

#### 中文

把第二根 $t_2=-1$ 独立代回。特别注意分母实部中的两个负号，以及分母虚部的负号。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,2}&=\frac{0.4+j(-0.2-1)}{[1-(-0.2)(-1)]+j(0.4)(-1)}\\&=\frac{0.4-j1.2}{0.8-j0.4}\\&=\frac{(0.4-j1.2)(0.8+j0.4)}{0.8^2+0.4^2}\\&=\frac{0.8-j0.8}{0.8}=1-j\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 15

#### English

Choose the first nonnegative distance as the primary solution; the other branch is also valid.

#### 中文

选第一组非负距离作为本页主答案；另一分支仍是有效匹配方案。

#### Equation

$$
d=0.198792\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j1
$$

### Answer

$$
d=0.198792\lambda,\qquad y_d=1+j1
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The other branch is $d=0.375\lambda$ with $y_d=1-j$. Distances repeat modulo $\lambda/2$.

#### 中文

另一有效分支为 $d=0.375\lambda$、$y_d=1-j$。所有距离按 $\lambda/2$ 周期重复。

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the length $l_1$ (in terms of wavelength) of the stub needed to match the source, and give the input admittance $y_s$ of the open-circuited stub of that length. Show all necessary work on the Smith chart for how you determined this value.

#### 中文

计算匹配源所需的支节长度 $l_1$（用波长表示），并给出该长度开路支节的输入导纳 $y_s$。在 Smith 图上展示确定这一数值所需的全部过程。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib open-line impedance, then invert and normalize with $Y_{0}=1/Z_{0}$.

#### 中文

先写原表开路传输线阻抗，再取倒数并用 $Y_{0}=1/Z_{0}$ 归一化。

#### Equation

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell),\qquad y_s=\frac{1/Z_s}{1/Z_0}=j\tan(\beta\ell)
$$

#### References

- [open](formula:open)

### Step 2

#### English

When taking the open-stub impedance reciprocal, cancel the reference impedance first. Then use $1/(-j)=j$ and $1/\cot\theta=\tan\theta$ to obtain the admittance formula step by step.

#### 中文

把开路支节阻抗取倒数时，先约去参考阻抗。再利用 $1/(-j)=j$ 和 $1/\cot\theta=\tan\theta$，逐步得到所用的导纳式。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}y_s&=\frac{Z_0}{-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell)}\\&=\frac1{-j}\frac1{\cot(\beta\ell)}\\\frac1{-j}&=\frac j{(-j)j}=j\\y_s&=j\tan(\beta\ell)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Parallel admittances add and the target total is one. If the main line is $1+jB$, the stub must supply $-jB$.

#### 中文

支节并联时导纳相加，目标是总归一化导纳为 1。若主线为 $1+jB$，支节必须为 $-jB$。

#### Equation

$$
y_{\mathrm{in}}=y_d+y_s=1,\qquad y_s=1-(1+jB)=-jB
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Insert the susceptance of the selected branch from c) and write the tangent equation.

#### 中文

代入 c) 选中分支的电纳，列出需要满足的正切方程。

#### Equation

$$
y_s=-j,\qquad \tan(2\pi\ell/\lambda)=-1
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Choose the smallest nonnegative open-stub length; a negative tangent requires a second-quadrant electrical length.

#### 中文

选最小非负的开路支节长度；正切为负时使用第二象限的电长度。

#### Equation

$$
\beta\ell=-\pi/4+\pi=3\pi/4,\qquad \frac{\ell}{\lambda}=\frac{3\pi/4}{2\pi}=0.375
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Check exact susceptance cancellation. Choosing the other main-line intersection also requires its corresponding stub length.

#### 中文

核对虚部恰好抵消；若改用另一主线交点，必须同时改用配套的支节长度。

#### Equation

$$
d_2=0.375\lambda,\quad y_{d,2}=1-j,\quad y_{s,2}=+j,\quad\ell_2=0.125\lambda
$$

### Step 7

#### English

The open-stub parameters for the selected branch are:

#### 中文

本页所选分支的开路支节参数如下。

#### Equation

$$
\ell=0.375000\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j1
$$

### Answer

$$
\ell=0.375000\lambda,\qquad y_s=-j1
$$
