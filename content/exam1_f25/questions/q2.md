## Question

### English

In superconducting quantum computers, (microwave) voltage pulses are sent down transmission lines to perform operations on qubits. Consider one such transmission line with the following parameters:
• Per-length inductance: $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$
• Per-length capacitance: $c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$
• Transit time (delay): $T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$
At time $t=0$, the voltage source sends a single DC pulse with amplitude $3\,\mathrm{V}$ and duration $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ down the transmission line toward the load. Due to damage to the circuit, the values of the source impedance and load impedance have increased from their originally designed values to $R_g=100\,\Omega$ and $R_L=200\,\Omega$, as shown in the circuit schematic below.

### 中文

在超导量子计算机中，沿传输线发送（微波）电压脉冲来对量子比特进行操作。考虑一根具有以下参数的传输线：
• 单位长度电感：$l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$
• 单位长度电容：$c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$
• 传输时间（延迟）：$T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$
在 $t=0$ 时，电压源沿传输线向负载发送一个幅值为 $3\,\mathrm{V}$、持续时间为 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ 的单个直流脉冲。由于电路损坏，源阻抗和负载阻抗从原设计值增大为 $R_g=100\,\Omega$ 和 $R_L=200\,\Omega$，如下方电路图所示。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem follows the launch and repeated reflections of a finite-duration pulse on a mismatched line.

#### 中文

本题考查有限宽度脉冲在失配传输线上的发射、反射与反弹图。

### Givens

#### English

The line has $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$, $c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$ and $T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$. A $3\,\mathrm V$, $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ source pulse drives $R_g=100\,\Omega$ and $R_L=200\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

$l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$、$c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$、$T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$；源脉冲为 $3\,\mathrm V$、持续 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$，$R_g=100\,\Omega$、$R_L=200\,\Omega$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the characteristic impedance and initial pulse, label reflected waves and measured endpoint voltages, and determine the long-time behavior.

#### 中文

求特性阻抗和初始入射脉冲，标清各次反射与端点测得的电压，并判断长时间后的状态。

### Approach

#### English

Find line impedance from its distributed parameters, then the launch divider and both reflection coefficients. Distinguish traveling-wave amplitudes from total endpoint voltage, and remember that the source pulse ends.

#### 中文

先由分布参数求特性阻抗，再求源端分压与两端反射系数。反弹图中要区分行波幅值和端点总电压，并记住源脉冲会结束。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the characteristic impedance of the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算传输线的特性阻抗。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the lossless characteristic-impedance equation; $l$ and $c$ are both per-length parameters.

#### 中文

先写无损传输线特性阻抗原式。$l$、$c$ 都是每单位长度参数。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc}
$$

#### References

- [impedance](formula:impedance)

### Step 2

#### English

Convert $\mathrm{nH}$ and $\mathrm{pF}$ to $\mathrm H$ and $\mathrm F$ before forming their ratio.

#### 中文

先把 $\mathrm{nH}$ 与 $\mathrm{pF}$ 换成 $H$ 与 $\mathrm{F}$，两个前缀不能直接相消。

#### Equation

$$
l=400\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{H/m},\qquad c=160\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Evaluate the ratio under the square root, in $\Omega^{2}$.

#### 中文

计算根号内的比值，单位为 $\Omega^{2}$。

#### Equation

$$
\frac lc=\frac{400\times10^{-9}}{160\times10^{-12}}=2.5\times10^3=2500\,\Omega^2
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Take the positive square root for this positive real characteristic impedance.

#### 中文

取正平方根，因为这里特性阻抗为正实数。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=50\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0=50\,\Omega
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

What is the initial amplitude of the voltage pulse $V_1^+$ that makes it into the transmission line?

#### 中文

进入传输线的初始电压脉冲 $V_1^+$ 的幅值是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

The launch-divider equation gives the first traveling wave before any load reflection returns.

#### 中文

原始分压公式给出尚未收到负载反射时进入线路的第一行波。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 2

#### English

The source pulse is $3\,\mathrm{V}$, the damaged source resistance is $100\,\Omega$ and the line remains $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

本题源脉冲为 $3\,\mathrm{V}$，损坏后的源电阻为 $100\,\Omega$，线路仍为 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=(3\,\mathrm V)\frac{50\,\Omega}{100\,\Omega+50\,\Omega}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Evaluate $50/150=1/3$ and multiply by source amplitude; pulse width remains $1\,\mathrm{ns}$.

#### 中文

先求分压比 $50/150=1/3$，再乘源幅度；脉冲宽度仍是 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=(3\,\mathrm V)\frac13
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The initially launched voltage is therefore $1\,\mathrm{V}$, rather than the full $3\,\mathrm{V}$ source pulse.

#### 中文

因此最初入射到线路的电压是 $1\,\mathrm{V}$，而非整个源脉冲的 $3\,\mathrm{V}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Sketch a bounce diagram for the voltage amplitude of the pulse for time $0\le t<4T$. Be sure to label both traveling voltage amplitudes and the amplitudes that would be measured at the load and the source. You do not need to calculate the total voltage at the source for $t=4T_d$.

#### 中文

画出 $0\le t<4T$ 内脉冲电压幅值的反弹图。必须标注行波电压幅值，以及在负载和源端测得的幅值。不必计算 $t=4T_d$ 时源端的总电压。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with endpoint reflection and the relation between measured voltage, arriving wave and its immediate reflection.

#### 中文

先写端点反射公式以及到达波与反射波相加得到测量电压的关系。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{R-Z_0}{R+Z_0},\qquad V_{\mathrm{ref}}=\Gamma V_{\mathrm{arr}},\qquad V_{\mathrm{end}}=(1+\Gamma)V_{\mathrm{arr}}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute the load and source resistances separately to obtain both positive reflection coefficients.

#### 中文

分别把负载和源阻抗代入，得到两个正的反射系数。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{200-50}{200+50}=\frac35,\qquad \Gamma_g=\frac{100-50}{100+50}=\frac13
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The initial pulse leaves the source at zero and reaches the load at $T^{d}=10\,\mathrm{ns}$, producing the first backward wave.

#### 中文

初始脉冲在 0 时刻从源出发，于 $T^{d}=10\,\mathrm{ns}$ 到达负载；负载产生第一反向波。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_1^-=\Gamma_LV_1^+
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Calculate the first load reflection and actual load voltage. The reflection coefficient multiplies incident $V_{1}^{+}$.

#### 中文

逐项计算第一次负载反射及负载实际电压；此处反射系数乘的是入射的 $V_{1}^{+}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^-=\frac35(1)=0.60\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_L(T_d)=(1+0.6)(1)=1.60\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 5

#### English

At $20\,\mathrm{ns}$ the return reaches the source and generates $V_{2}^{+}$; measured source voltage is $V_{1}^{-}+V_{2}^{+}$.

#### 中文

$20\,\mathrm{ns}$ 时反向波回到源，源反射生成 $V_{2}^{+}$；源端测到的是 $V_{1}^{-}+V_{2}^{+}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_2^+=\Gamma_gV_1^-=\frac13(0.6)=0.20\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=0.6+0.2=0.80\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 6

#### English

At $30\,\mathrm{ns}$ the second forward pulse reaches the load. Multiply by $\Gamma_L$ again and add arrival plus reflection.

#### 中文

$30\,\mathrm{ns}$ 时第二次正向脉冲到达负载，继续乘 $\Gamma_L$，负载电压仍是到达波与反射波之和。

#### Equation

$$
V_2^-=\Gamma_LV_2^+=0.6(0.2)=0.12\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_L(3T_d)=0.2+0.12=0.32\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 7

#### English

Label the diagram at 0, 10, 20, 30 and $40\,\mathrm{ns}$. All pulses last $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ and do not overlap, so do not accumulate old pulses as for a permanent step.

#### 中文

在反弹图上按 0、10、20、30、$40\,\mathrm{ns}$ 标时刻。所有波宽均为 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$，彼此不重叠，不能像永久阶跃那样累加旧脉冲。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^-=0.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^+=0.20\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=0.12\,\mathrm V\\V_L(10\,\mathrm{ns})=1.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_{\mathrm{in}}(20\,\mathrm{ns})=0.80\,\mathrm V,\quad V_L(30\,\mathrm{ns})=0.32\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^-=0.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^+=0.20\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=0.12\,\mathrm V\\V_L(10\,\mathrm{ns})=1.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_{\mathrm{in}}(20\,\mathrm{ns})=0.80\,\mathrm V,\quad V_L(30\,\mathrm{ns})=0.32\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

What will be the voltage on the transmission line after an infinite number of bounces $(t\to\infty)$?

#### 中文

经过无限多次反弹后 $(t\to\infty)$，传输线上的电压是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the bounce recurrence: successive forward waves differ by one load and one source reflection.

#### 中文

先写每一轮反弹的递推式；相继正向波相差一整次负载反射和源反射。

#### Equation

$$
V_{n+1}^+=\Gamma_g\Gamma_LV_n^+
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)
- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 2

#### English

Multiply the preceding coefficients to obtain the round-trip factor, smaller than one.

#### 中文

将上一问两系数相乘，得到小于 1 的往返倍率。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_g\Gamma_L=\frac13(0.6)=0.2,\qquad V_n^+=(1\,\mathrm V)(0.2)^{n-1}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Only one $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ pulse is emitted, after which the source returns to zero; subsequent finite-width pulse amplitudes decay to zero.

#### 中文

题目只发出一个 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ 脉冲，源随后回到零；后续有限宽脉冲的幅度逐轮趋于零。

#### Equation

$$
V_g(t>1\,\mathrm{ns})=0,\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}(0.2)^{n-1}=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

There is no sustained excitation. The termination resistors absorb the pulse energy, so a permanent-DC divider value does not apply.

#### 中文

因此稳态没有持续激励。端点电阻吸收脉冲能量，不能套用持续 DC 源的分压值。

#### Equation

$$
\lim_{t\to\infty}V(z,t)=0\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
\lim_{t\to\infty}V(z,t)=0\,\mathrm V
$$
