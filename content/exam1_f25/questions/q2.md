## Question

### English

In superconducting quantum computers, (microwave) voltage pulses are sent down transmission lines to perform operations on qubits. Consider one such transmission line with the following parameters:
• Per-length inductance: $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$
• Per-length capacitance: $c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$
• Transit time (delay): $T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$
At time $t=0$, the voltage source sends a single DC pulse with amplitude $3\,\mathrm{V}$ and duration $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ down the transmission line toward the load. Due to damage to the circuit, the values of the source impedance and load impedance have increased from their originally designed values to $R_g=100\,\Omega$ and $R_L=200\,\Omega$, as shown in the circuit schematic below.

### 中文

在超导量子计算机中，沿传输线发送（微波）电压脉冲来对量子比特进行操作。考虑一根具有以下参数的传输线：
• 单位长度电感：$l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$
• 单位长度电容：$c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$
• 传输时间（延迟）：$T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$
在 $t=0$ 时，电压源沿传输线向负载发送一个幅值为 $3\,\mathrm{V}$、持续时间为 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ 的单个直流脉冲。由于电路损坏，源阻抗和负载阻抗从原设计值增大为 $R_g=100\,\Omega$ 和 $R_L=200\,\Omega$，如下方电路图所示。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem follows the launch and repeated reflections of a finite-duration pulse on a mismatched line.

#### 中文

本题考查有限宽度脉冲在失配传输线上的发射、反射与反弹图。

### Givens

#### English

The line has $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$, $c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$ and $T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$. A $3\,\mathrm V$, $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ source pulse drives $R_g=100\,\Omega$ and $R_L=200\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

$l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$、$c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$、$T_d=10\,\mathrm{ns}$；源脉冲为 $3\,\mathrm V$、持续 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$，$R_g=100\,\Omega$、$R_L=200\,\Omega$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the characteristic impedance and initial pulse, label reflected waves and measured endpoint voltages, and determine the long-time behavior.

#### 中文

求特性阻抗和初始入射脉冲，标清各次反射与端点测得的电压，并判断长时间后的状态。

### Approach

#### English

Find line impedance from its distributed parameters, then the launch divider and both reflection coefficients. Distinguish traveling-wave amplitudes from total endpoint voltage, and remember that the source pulse ends.

#### 中文

先由分布参数求特性阻抗，再求源端分压与两端反射系数。反弹图中要区分行波幅值和端点总电压，并记住源脉冲会结束。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the characteristic impedance of the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算传输线的特性阻抗。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the lossless [characteristic impedance](formula:impedance) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的无损线[特性阻抗公式](formula:impedance)：

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$ and $c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$, find $Z_0$. Convert to SI units and substitute:

#### 中文

已知 $l=400\,\mathrm{nH/m}$、$c=160\,\mathrm{pF/m}$，求 $Z_0$。换成 SI 单位后代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle l=400\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{H/m},\qquad c=160\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F/m}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \frac lc=\frac{400\times10^{-9}}{160\times10^{-12}}=2.5\times10^3=2500\,\Omega^2\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Taking the positive square root gives

#### 中文

取正平方根，得

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=50\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_0=50\,\Omega
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

What is the initial amplitude of the voltage pulse $V_1^+$ that makes it into the transmission line?

#### 中文

进入传输线的初始电压脉冲 $V_1^+$ 的幅值是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [initial-wave voltage divider](formula:bounce) from Lecture 04:

#### 中文

使用 Lecture 04 的[初始行波分压式](formula:bounce)：

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $V_g=3\,\mathrm V$, $R_g=100\,\Omega$ and $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ from (a), find $V_1^+$. Substitute:

#### 中文

已知 $V_g=3\,\mathrm V$、$R_g=100\,\Omega$，且 (a) 得 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求 $V_1^+$。把这些值代入：

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=(3\,\mathrm V)\frac{50\,\Omega}{100\,\Omega+50\,\Omega}=(3\,\mathrm V)\frac13
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Sketch a bounce diagram for the voltage amplitude of the pulse for time $0\le t<4T$. Be sure to label both traveling voltage amplitudes and the amplitudes that would be measured at the load and the source. You do not need to calculate the total voltage at the source for $t=4T_d$.

#### 中文

画出 $0\le t<4T$ 内脉冲电压幅值的反弹图。必须标注行波电压幅值，以及在负载和源端测得的幅值。不必计算 $t=4T_d$ 时源端的总电压。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection); the endpoint voltage is the arriving wave plus its reflection:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[反射系数](formula:reflection)；端点电压为到达波与反射波之和：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{R-Z_0}{R+Z_0},\qquad V_{\mathrm{ref}}=\Gamma V_{\mathrm{arr}},\qquad V_{\mathrm{end}}=(1+\Gamma)V_{\mathrm{arr}}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, $R_L=200\,\Omega$ and $R_g=100\,\Omega$, find the bounces and endpoint voltages. First evaluate both reflection coefficients:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=200\,\Omega$、$R_g=100\,\Omega$，求各次反弹与端点电压。先代入两端阻抗：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{200-50}{200+50}=\frac35,\qquad \Gamma_g=\frac{100-50}{100+50}=\frac13
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Starting with $V_1^+=1\,\mathrm V$, multiply successively by $\Gamma_L$, $\Gamma_g$ and $\Gamma_L$, adding arriving and reflected waves at each endpoint:

#### 中文

把 $V_1^+=1\,\mathrm V$ 依次乘 $\Gamma_L$、$\Gamma_g$、$\Gamma_L$，并在端点相加：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V_1^+=1.00\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_1^-=\Gamma_LV_1^+\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_1^-=\frac35(1)=0.60\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_L(T_d)=(1+0.6)(1)=1.60\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_2^+=\Gamma_gV_1^-=\frac13(0.6)=0.20\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=0.6+0.2=0.80\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_2^-=\Gamma_LV_2^+=0.6(0.2)=0.12\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_L(3T_d)=0.2+0.12=0.32\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

This gives the following amplitudes. Each pulse is $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ wide; arrivals at $10$, $20$ and $30\,\mathrm{ns}$ do not overlap.

#### 中文

得下列幅值。各脉冲宽 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$，在 $10$、$20$、$30\,\mathrm{ns}$ 到达，彼此不重叠。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^-=0.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^+=0.20\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=0.12\,\mathrm V\\V_L(10\,\mathrm{ns})=1.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_{\mathrm{in}}(20\,\mathrm{ns})=0.80\,\mathrm V,\quad V_L(30\,\mathrm{ns})=0.32\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}V_1^-=0.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^+=0.20\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^-=0.12\,\mathrm V\\V_L(10\,\mathrm{ns})=1.60\,\mathrm V,\quad V_{\mathrm{in}}(20\,\mathrm{ns})=0.80\,\mathrm V,\quad V_L(30\,\mathrm{ns})=0.32\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

What will be the voltage on the transmission line after an infinite number of bounces $(t\to\infty)$?

#### 中文

经过无限多次反弹后 $(t\to\infty)$，传输线上的电压是多少？

### Step 1

#### English

Starting with Lecture 04’s [initial wave](formula:bounce), applying the crib sheet’s [reflection relation](formula:reflection) at both ends gives:

#### 中文

从 Lecture 04 的[初始行波](formula:bounce)出发，按 crib sheet 的两端[反射关系](formula:reflection)，每次往返满足：

#### Equation

$$
V_{n+1}^+=\Gamma_g\Gamma_LV_n^+
$$


### Step 2

#### English

A single $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ pulse is launched. Find the voltage as $t\to\infty$ by substituting the reflection coefficients from (c):

#### 中文

已知仅发出一个 $1\,\mathrm{ns}$ 脉冲，求 $t\to\infty$ 的电压。把 (c) 的两反射系数代入递推式：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \Gamma_g\Gamma_L=\frac13(0.6)=0.2,\qquad V_n^+=(1\,\mathrm V)(0.2)^{n-1}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_g(t>1\,\mathrm{ns})=0,\qquad \lim_{n\to\infty}(0.2)^{n-1}=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The subsequent pulses decay and the source returns to zero, giving

#### 中文

因后续脉冲逐次衰减且源已归零，得

#### Equation

$$
\lim_{t\to\infty}V(z,t)=0\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
\lim_{t\to\infty}V(z,t)=0\,\mathrm V
$$
