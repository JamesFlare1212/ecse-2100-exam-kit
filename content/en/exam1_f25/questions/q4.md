## Question

You have been asked to match the load impedance $Z_L=150+j75\,\Omega$ to a feedline with characteristic impedance $Z_0=75\,\Omega$ by using an open-circuited transmission line stub. A diagram of the single-stub matching circuit is shown below. A Smith chart is attached to the back of your exam for recording your answers. A ruler will also be provided.

## Guide

This problem uses Smith-chart impedance/admittance conversion and a parallel open-circuit matching stub.

**Given：** The load is $Z_L=(150+j75)\,\Omega$, the lines have characteristic impedance $75\,\Omega$, and the stub is open-circuited.

**Find：** Locate the normalized load impedance and admittance, then find the stub position and length with the construction shown on the chart.

**Start here：** Admittances add directly in a parallel network. Convert the load to admittance, rotate along its constant-reflection circle until the real part matches, then cancel the remaining imaginary part with the open stub.

## Part a

Calculate the normalized load impedance and plot it on the Smith chart.

### Solution

Use the definition of normalized impedance:

$$
z_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_0}
$$

Given $Z_L=150+j75\,\Omega$ and $Z_0=75\,\Omega$, find $z_L$. Substitute and use the crib sheet’s [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection) to locate the Smith-chart point:

$$
\begin{aligned}
z_L&=\frac{(150+j75)\,\Omega}{75\,\Omega}=2+j,\\[4pt]
\Gamma_z&=\frac{z_L-1}{z_L+1}=\frac{(2+j)-1}{(2+j)+1}=\frac{1+j}{3+j}=0.4+j0.2
\end{aligned}
$$

Mark the intersection of the $r=2$ circle and $x=+1$ arc:

$$
z_L=\boxed{2+j}
$$

## Part b

Plot the normalized load admittance on the Smith chart and show how you determined it from the plotted normalized load impedance. Write down the normalized load admittance you found from the Smith chart.

### Solution

Use the relation between admittance and normalized impedance:

$$
y_L=\frac{Y_L}{Y_0}=\frac{1/Z_L}{1/Z_0}=\frac{Z_0}{Z_L}=\frac1{z_L}
$$

Given $z_L=2+j$ from (a), find $y_L$. Take the reciprocal and rotate the [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection)’s chart coordinate to the diametrically opposite point:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle y_L=\frac1{2+j}=\frac{2-j}{(2+j)(2-j)}=\frac{2-j}{5}=0.4-j0.2\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_y=\frac{y_L-1}{y_L+1}=\frac{1-z_L}{1+z_L}=-\Gamma_z\end{aligned}
$$

Reading the same impedance grid after a $180^\circ$ rotation gives

$$
y_L=\boxed{0.4-j0.2}
$$

## Part c

Calculate the distance $d_1$ (in terms of wavelength) from the load where the open-circuited stub should be placed, and give the input admittance $y_d$ of the transmission line at that point. Show all necessary work on the Smith chart for how you determined this value.

### Solution

Use the crib sheet’s [input-impedance formula](formula:zin) with $t=\tan(\beta d)$ and normalize:

$$
\begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in},d}&=Z_0\frac{Z_L+jZ_0\tan(\beta d)}{Z_0+jZ_L\tan(\beta d)}\\z_d&=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{in},d}}{Z_0}=\frac{z_L+jt}{1+jz_Lt}\\t&=\tan(\beta d),\qquad\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\end{aligned}
$$

Given $y_L=0.4-j0.2$, find the distances where $\Re(y_d)=1$. Take the reciprocal impedance:

$$
y_d=\frac1{z_d}=\frac{y_L+jt}{1+jy_Lt},\qquad y_L=g+jb
$$

Substitute $g+jb$, multiply by the denominator’s conjugate and set the real part to 1:

$$
\begin{aligned}y_d&=\frac{[g+j(b+t)][(1-bt)-jgt]}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2},\\\Re(y_d)&=\frac{g(1+t^2)}{(1-bt)^2+(gt)^2}=1,\\0&=(g^2+b^2-g)t^2-2bt+(1-g)\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute $g=0.4$ and $b=-0.2$, solve both roots and choose $0\le d<\lambda/2$:

$$
\begin{aligned}-0.2t^2+0.4t+0.6&=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(t-3)(t+1)=0,\\t_1&=3,\qquad t_2=-1,\\\frac{d_1}{\lambda}&=\frac{\arctan3}{2\pi}\approx0.198792,\\\frac{d_2}{\lambda}&=\frac{\arctan(-1)+\pi}{2\pi}=0.375\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute both roots into $y_d$ to obtain the intersections of the constant-magnitude circle and $g=1$ circle:

$$
\begin{aligned}y_{d,1}&=\frac{0.4+j2.8}{1.6+j1.2}=\frac{4+j4}{4}=1+j,\\y_{d,2}&=\frac{0.4-j1.2}{0.8-j0.4}=\frac{0.8-j0.8}{0.8}=1-j\end{aligned}
$$

Choosing the shorter distance gives

$$
d=\boxed{0.198792\lambda},\qquad y_d=\boxed{1+j1}
$$

### Source correction

The other branch is $d=0.375\lambda$ with $y_d=1-j$. Distances repeat modulo $\lambda/2$.

## Part d

Calculate the length $l_1$ (in terms of wavelength) of the stub needed to match the source, and give the input admittance $y_s$ of the open-circuited stub of that length. Show all necessary work on the Smith chart for how you determined this value.

### Solution

Use the [open-circuit stub impedance](formula:open) from the crib sheet, take its reciprocal and normalize:

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell),\qquad y_s=\frac{1/Z_s}{1/Z_0}=j\tan(\beta\ell)
$$

Given the selected branch $y_d=1+j$, find $y_s$ and $\ell$. Substitute into the shunt matching condition:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle y_{\mathrm{in}}=y_d+y_s=1,\qquad y_s=1-(1+jB)=-jB\\[4pt]&\displaystyle y_s=-j,\qquad \tan(2\pi\ell/\lambda)=-1\end{aligned}
$$

Substitute the required susceptance into $y_s=j\tan(2\pi\ell/\lambda)$ and choose the shortest nonnegative length. The other main-line branch requires its corresponding stub:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \beta\ell=-\pi/4+\pi=3\pi/4,\qquad \frac{\ell}{\lambda}=\frac{3\pi/4}{2\pi}=0.375\\[4pt]&\displaystyle d_2=0.375\lambda,\quad y_{d,2}=1-j,\quad y_{s,2}=+j,\quad\ell_2=0.125\lambda\end{aligned}
$$

This gives

$$
\ell=\boxed{0.375000\lambda},\qquad y_s=\boxed{-j1}
$$
