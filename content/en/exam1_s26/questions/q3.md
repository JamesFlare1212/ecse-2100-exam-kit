## Question

For all circuits in this problem, the frequency of the generator voltage is $f=200\mathrm{MHz}=200\times10^6\mathrm{Hz}$ and the velocity factor of all coaxial cables is $0.65$. In each part, provide a numerical answer with units.

## Guide

These circuits test impedance transformation, matching and the filtering action of an open-circuit stub.

**Given：** All circuits operate at $200\,\mathrm{MHz}$ with velocity factor $0.65$. Part a offers $25\,\Omega$, $50\,\Omega$ and $75\,\Omega$ cables; each diagram supplies its connections and terminations.

**Find：** Select cable type and physical length for matching, calculate the specified input impedance, and choose a stub length that produces total reflection.

**Start here：** Relate electrical length to physical length, then recognize half-wave and quarter-wave properties. For a parallel stub, use admittance and identify whether the junction effectively sees an open or a short.

## Part a(i)

For part a) only: you have access to spools of coaxial cable with characteristic impedances:
• Cable type #1: $Z_{01}=25\Omega$
• Cable type #2: $Z_{02}=50\Omega$
• Cable type #3: $Z_{03}=75\Omega$
In each of the circuits below, specify the type of cable (which characteristic impedance $Z_0$) and length $L$ that match the generator impedance to the load impedance.

Circuit #1

### Solution

Use the [half-wave impedance repetition](formula:half) from the crib sheet:

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L
$$

Given $R_g=R_L=100\,\Omega$, [velocity factor](formula:velocity) $0.65$ and $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$, find the cable type and length. Evaluate the [wavelength](formula:wave) and choose a half-wave line:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=100\,\Omega=R_g,\qquad Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{\mathrm{vf}\,u_c}{f}=\frac{0.65(3\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})}{200\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}}=0.975\,\mathrm m\\[4pt]&\displaystyle L=\frac{0.975\,\mathrm m}{2}=0.4875\,\mathrm m\end{aligned}
$$

All three cable types repeat the $100\,\Omega$ load, giving

$$
\boxed{Z_0\in\{25,50,75\}\,\Omega},\qquad L=\boxed{0.4875\,\mathrm m}
$$

## Part a(ii)

Circuit #2

### Solution

Use the [quarter-wave transformation](formula:quarter) from the crib sheet and match the input to the source:

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{R_L}=R_g
$$

Given $R_g=50\,\Omega$, $R_L=12.5\,\Omega$ and velocity factor $0.65$, find the cable type and length. Substitute into the impedance and [wavelength relation](formula:wave)s:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_0=\sqrt{R_gR_L}=\sqrt{(50\,\Omega)(12.5\,\Omega)}=\sqrt{625}\,\Omega=25\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{0.65(3\times10^8)}{200\times10^6}=0.975\,\mathrm m\\[4pt]&\displaystyle L=\frac{0.975}{4}=0.24375\,\mathrm m,\qquad \frac{25^2}{12.5}=50\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

Choose cable type #1:

$$
Z_0=\boxed{25\,\Omega},\qquad L=\boxed{0.24375\,\mathrm m}
$$

## Part b

Determine $Z_{in}$ for the circuit below.

### Solution

Use the capacitor impedance and the [quarter-wave transformation](formula:quarter) from the crib sheet:

$$
Z_C=\frac1{j\omega C},\qquad Z_L=R+Z_C,\qquad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

Given $f=200\,\mathrm{MHz}$, $C=1\,\mu\mathrm F$ and $R=Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$. Evaluate the [angular frequency](formula:wave) and series load and multiply by the denominator’s conjugate:

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \omega=2\pi(200\times10^6)\,\mathrm{rad/s},\qquad C=10^{-6}\,\mathrm F\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_C=-\frac j{2\pi(200\times10^6)(10^{-6})}=-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega,\qquad Z_L=50-j7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \begin{aligned}Z_{\mathrm{in}}&=\frac{2500}{50-jX_C}\\&=\frac{2500(50+jX_C)}{50^2+X_C^2},\quad X_C=7.95775\times10^{-4}\,\Omega\end{aligned}\end{aligned}
$$

This gives

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}\approx\boxed{(50+j7.95775\times10^{-4})\,\Omega}
$$

## Part c

In the circuit below, what length of open-circuited cable $\ell$ would lead to full reflection of the voltage wave at $z=A$?

### Solution

Use the [open-line input impedance](formula:open) from the crib sheet:

$$
Z_s=-jZ_0\cot(\beta\ell)
$$

An open stub is in parallel with a finite load. Find its shortest length for total reflection at A. Set $Z_s=0$ and substitute into the [wavenumber](formula:wave) relation, parallel impedance and [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection):

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \beta\ell=\frac\pi2,\qquad\ell=\frac{\pi/2}{2\pi/\lambda}=\frac\lambda4\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_A=\frac{Z_sZ_L}{Z_s+Z_L}=\frac{0\cdot Z_L}{0+Z_L}=0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_A=\frac{Z_A-Z_0}{Z_A+Z_0}=\frac{-Z_0}{Z_0}=-1\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting $\lambda=0.975\,\mathrm m$ gives

$$
\ell=\frac\lambda4=\boxed{0.24375\,\mathrm m},\qquad |\Gamma_A|=\boxed{1}
$$
