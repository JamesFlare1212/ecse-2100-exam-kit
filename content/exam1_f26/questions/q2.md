## Question

### English

The transmission line in Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ below has been damaged, resulting in an abrupt change in its characteristic impedance at a point somewhere along the transmission line.

We can model Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ above by replacing the transmission line between the point of the damage and the load by an effective input impedance $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$, as shown in Circuit $\mathrm{B}$ below.

Using a directional coupler, it has been determined that the steady-state forward-traveling voltage wave on the transmission line between its input and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in Circuit $\mathrm{B}$ is given by the phasor $\widetilde V^+(z)=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$, and the backward-traveling voltage wave is given by the phasor $\widetilde V^-(z)=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$.

### 中文

下方电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 中的传输线遭到损坏，导致其特性阻抗在沿线某处突变。

可将上方电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 中从损坏点到负载之间的传输线替换为一个有效输入阻抗 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$，得到下方电路 $\mathrm{B}$ 所示的模型。

利用定向耦合器，已确定电路 $\mathrm{B}$ 中输入端至 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间的稳态正向行波电压相量为 $\widetilde V^+(z)=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$，反向行波电压相量为 $\widetilde V^-(z)=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem uses measured forward and backward voltage phasors to analyze standing waves, power and an effective termination.

#### 中文

本题从已测得的正、反向电压相量分析受损线上的驻波、功率和等效终端。

### Givens

#### English

The waves are $\widetilde V^+=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$ and $\widetilde V^-=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$, with $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and the operating frequency supplied.

#### 中文

两行波为 $\widetilde V^+=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$ 与 $\widetilde V^-=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$，并给出 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ 和工作频率。

### Goal

#### English

Find time-domain voltage, reflection coefficient, standing-wave ratio, maximum voltage, net average power and effective impedance, then compare with the undamaged line.

#### 中文

求时域电压、反射系数、驻波比、最大电压、净平均功率与等效阻抗，并与未损坏时比较。

### Approach

#### English

Keep propagation direction and phase conventions explicit. Use the ratio and superposition of the waves, and subtract backward-wave power rather than treating total voltage magnitude as a resistor voltage.

#### 中文

先保留传播方向和相位约定，再从两行波的比值与叠加出发。功率要扣除反向波携带的功率，不能只把总电压幅值代入电阻功率式。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Write the expression for the total voltage wave on the transmission line between its input and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in the time domain.

#### 中文

写出输入端至 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间传输线上总电压波的时域表达式。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the real-part definition, adding forward and backward phasors first.

#### 中文

先写相量取实部的定义，将正反行波相量先相加。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{[\widetilde V^+(z)+\widetilde V^-(z)]e^{j\omega t}\}
$$

#### References

- [phasor](formula:phasor)

### Step 2

#### English

Use the circuit frequency of $150\,\mathrm{MHz}$ to calculate angular frequency; do not confuse it with spatial phase constant $2\pi$.

#### 中文

由原图的 $150\,\mathrm{MHz}$ 频率求角频率，不把相位常数 $2\pi$ 当成角频率。

#### Equation

$$
\omega=2\pi f=2\pi(150\times10^6)=3\pi\times10^8\,\mathrm{rad/s}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute both given terms $5e^{-j2\pi z}$ and $3e^{j2\pi z}$.

#### 中文

将给定的 $5e^{-j2\pi z}$ 与 $3e^{j2\pi z}$ 两项完整代入。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{[5e^{-j2\pi z}+3e^{j2\pi z}]e^{j3\pi10^8t}\}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Combine each spatial and temporal exponent, then use $\operatorname{Re}(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$.

#### 中文

分别合并空间与时间指数，再用 $\operatorname{Re}(e^{j\theta})=\cos\theta$。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{5e^{j(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)}+3e^{j(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)}\}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Use Euler's formula when taking the real part: cosine is real, while the sine term multiplied by $j$ is imaginary. Apply the rule separately to each real amplitude, retaining its original sign.

#### 中文

相量取实部使用欧拉公式：余弦是实部，正弦前带 $j$，属于虚部。对每个实数幅度分别使用这条规则，幅度原有的正负号都保留。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}e^{j\theta}&=\cos\theta+j\sin\theta\\\operatorname{Re}\{A e^{j\theta}\}&=A\cos\theta\qquad(A\in\mathbb R)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Obtain time-domain voltage as one forward and one backward cosine wave.

#### 中文

得到含一个正向、一个反向余弦行波的时域电压。

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)+3\cos(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)+3\cos(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Calculate the reflection coefficient between the transmission line and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

#### 中文

计算传输线与 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间的反射系数。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib voltage reflection coefficient, comparing backward and forward waves at the same reference plane.

#### 中文

先写原表的电压反射系数，必须比较同一参考面上的反向与正向波。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{V_0^-}{V_0^+}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

At the $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ reference plane used for this problem, the given wave coefficients are positive real $3\,\mathrm{V}$ and $5\,\mathrm{V}$.

#### 中文

按本题采用的 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 参考面，两个给定行波系数分别为正实数 $3\,\mathrm{V}$ 与 $5\,\mathrm{V}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_0^-=3\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_0^+=5\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Form the ratio; voltage units cancel and the phase difference is zero.

#### 中文

代入比值，电压单位约去，相位差为零。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{3\,\mathrm V}{5\,\mathrm V}=0.6e^{j0}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The reflection coefficient is therefore positive real, rather than merely having a known magnitude.

#### 中文

因此反射系数为正实数，而非只知道它的模。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=+0.60
$$

### Answer

$$
\Gamma=+0.60
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Calculate the standing wave ratio.

#### 中文

计算驻波比。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib standing-wave-ratio formula.

#### 中文

先写原表的驻波比公式。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{SWR}=\frac{1+|\Gamma|}{1-|\Gamma|}
$$

#### References

- [swr](formula:swr)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute $|\Gamma|=0.6$ and evaluate numerator and denominator separately.

#### 中文

代入 $|\Gamma|=0.6$，分别计算分子、分母。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{SWR}=\frac{1+0.6}{1-0.6}=\frac{1.6}{0.4}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Cross-check with envelope extrema: the two amplitudes sum to 8 and differ by 2.

#### 中文

也可直接用最大最小包络交叉核对：两幅度相加为 8，相减为 2。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\max}=5+3=8\,\mathrm V,\quad V_{\min}=5-3=2\,\mathrm V,\quad\frac{V_{\max}}{V_{\min}}=\frac82
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The standing-wave ratio is dimensionless.

#### 中文

驻波比是无量纲量。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{SWR}=4.00
$$

### Answer

$$
\mathrm{SWR}=4.00
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the maximum voltage on the transmission line. How does this voltage compare with the maximum voltage on the transmission line when Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ is undamaged? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

计算传输线上的最大电压。该电压与电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 未损坏时传输线上的最大电压相比如何？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib maximum voltage envelope: the traveling waves add at constructive-interference positions.

#### 中文

先写原表中的电压包络最大值：正反行波在同相位置相加。

#### Equation

$$
|V|_{\max}=|V_0^+|+|V_0^-|
$$

#### References

- [swr](formula:swr)

### Step 2

#### English

Insert the two measured peak amplitudes, rather than arbitrarily adding instantaneous cosine values.

#### 中文

将测得的两个峰值幅度代入，而不是把瞬时余弦值任意相加。

#### Equation

$$
|V|_{\max}=|5|+|3|=8\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 3

#### English

A complex magnitude squared equals the value times its conjugate. Conjugation changes the sign of $j$ in each exponent; expansion produces two conjugate cross terms.

#### 中文

复数模的平方等于它与共轭数的乘积。共轭会把指数中的 $j$ 变号，逐项相乘后出现两个互为共轭的交叉项。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}|\widetilde V(z)|^2&=(5e^{-j\beta z}+3e^{j\beta z})(5e^{j\beta z}+3e^{-j\beta z})\\&=25+15e^{-2j\beta z}+15e^{2j\beta z}+9\\&=34+15(e^{2j\beta z}+e^{-2j\beta z})\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Combine the cross terms with Euler's identity and use the maximum cosine value $1$. Here $\beta=2\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$, so the cross-term phase is $4\pi z$.

#### 中文

用欧拉公式合并两个交叉项，再利用余弦的最大值为 $1$。这里 $\beta=2\pi\,\mathrm{rad/m}$，所以交叉项的相位是 $4\pi z$。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}e^{j\theta}+e^{-j\theta}&=2\cos\theta\\|\widetilde V(z)|^2&=34+30\cos(4\pi z)\\&\le34+30=64\,\mathrm{V^2}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Before damage the load was matched, with $\Gamma=0$ and zero backward amplitude, giving only a $5\,\mathrm{V}$ maximum envelope.

#### 中文

未受损时负载匹配，$\Gamma=0$、反向幅度为零，最大包络仅为 $5\,\mathrm{V}$。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\max,\mathrm{undamaged}}=5+0=5\,\mathrm V,\qquad \frac{8}{5}=1.6
$$

### Step 6

#### English

After damage the maximum voltage is 1.6 times the undamaged value.

#### 中文

损坏后最大电压增至原来的 1.6 倍。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\max}=8.00\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_{\max}=8.00\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Calculate the time-average power delivered to $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$. How does this delivered power compare with the power delivered to the load when Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ is undamaged? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

计算传递给 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的时间平均功率。该功率与电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 未损坏时传递给负载的功率相比如何？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib net average-power equation. The voltage phasors are peak amplitudes, so the denominator includes two.

#### 中文

先写原表的净平均功率式。题目电压相量是峰值幅度，所以分母含 2。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{|V_0^+|^2}{2Z_0}(1-|\Gamma|^2)
$$

#### References

- [power](formula:power)

### Step 2

#### English

Calculate forward power first and reflected-power magnitude separately.

#### 中文

先算正向波功率，再算反射功率的大小。

#### Equation

$$
P^+=\frac{5^2}{2(50)}=0.25\,\mathrm W,\qquad |P^-|=\frac{3^2}{2(50)}=0.09\,\mathrm W
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Reflected power travels toward the source, so net power delivered to $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is the difference.

#### 中文

反向功率朝源传播，因此送给 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的净功率是两者之差。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=P^+-|P^-|=0.25-0.09=0.16\,\mathrm W
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Verify through $\Gamma$: $1-0.6^{2}=0.64$, so $64\%$ of forward power is accepted as net power.

#### 中文

同样代入 $\Gamma$ 的公式可核对：$1-0.6^{2}=0.64$，表示正向功率的 $64\%$ 被净接收。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{25}{100}(1-0.36)=0.25(0.64)=0.16\,\mathrm W
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Before damage there was no reflection and delivered power was $0.25\,\mathrm{W}$, so damage reduces it by $36\%$.

#### 中文

未受损时无反射，送达功率为 $0.25\,\mathrm{W}$，因此损坏后功率下降 $36\%$。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=0.160\,\mathrm W
$$

### Answer

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=0.160\,\mathrm W
$$

## Part f

### Question

#### English

Calculate the value of the impedance $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$. Did the damage to the transmission line increase or decrease its characteristic impedance at that location?

#### 中文

计算阻抗 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的值。传输线的损坏使该位置的特性阻抗增大还是减小？

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the original relation between reflection coefficient and effective impedance.

#### 中文

先写反射系数与等效阻抗的原始关系。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{eff}}-Z_0}{Z_{\mathrm{eff}}+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Clear the denominator and collect terms containing $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

#### 中文

先消分母，再把含 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的项移到同一侧。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma Z_{\mathrm{eff}}+\Gamma Z_0=Z_{\mathrm{eff}}-Z_0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Factor out $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and solve, showing explicitly how reflection gives impedance.

#### 中文

提取 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 并解出它，显示反射系数如何变成阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
(1-\Gamma)Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=(1+\Gamma)Z_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=Z_0\frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Insert $+0.6$ and $50\,\Omega$, first evaluating the dimensionless multiplier $1.6/0.4=4$.

#### 中文

代入 $+0.6$ 与 $50\,\Omega$，先求无量纲倍率 $1.6/0.4=4$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=(50\,\Omega)\frac{1+0.6}{1-0.6}=(50\,\Omega)(4)
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The effective input increases from $50\,\Omega$ to $200\,\Omega$, which is the increase inferred in the official simplified model.

#### 中文

等效输入阻抗由 $50\,\Omega$ 增至 $200\,\Omega$；这就是官方简化模型中“损坏使阻抗增大”的含义。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=200\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=200\,\Omega
$$

### Answer text

#### English

The effective input impedance increases. $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ alone does not uniquely determine the damaged section’s local characteristic impedance; the official simplified model calls it an increase.

#### 中文

有效输入阻抗增大。仅由 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 不能唯一还原损坏段本身的局部特性阻抗；原解在其简化模型下称为增大。
