## Question

### English

The transmission line in Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ below has been damaged, resulting in an abrupt change in its characteristic impedance at a point somewhere along the transmission line.

We can model Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ above by replacing the transmission line between the point of the damage and the load by an effective input impedance $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$, as shown in Circuit $\mathrm{B}$ below.

Using a directional coupler, it has been determined that the steady-state forward-traveling voltage wave on the transmission line between its input and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in Circuit $\mathrm{B}$ is given by the phasor $\widetilde V^+(z)=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$, and the backward-traveling voltage wave is given by the phasor $\widetilde V^-(z)=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$.

### 中文

下方电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 中的传输线遭到损坏，导致其特性阻抗在沿线某处突变。

可将上方电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 中从损坏点到负载之间的传输线替换为一个有效输入阻抗 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$，得到下方电路 $\mathrm{B}$ 所示的模型。

利用定向耦合器，已确定电路 $\mathrm{B}$ 中输入端至 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间的稳态正向行波电压相量为 $\widetilde V^+(z)=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$，反向行波电压相量为 $\widetilde V^-(z)=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm{V}$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem uses measured forward and backward voltage phasors to analyze standing waves, power and an effective termination.

#### 中文

本题从已测得的正、反向电压相量分析受损线上的驻波、功率和等效终端。

### Givens

#### English

The waves are $\widetilde V^+=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$ and $\widetilde V^-=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$, with $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and the operating frequency supplied.

#### 中文

两行波为 $\widetilde V^+=5e^{-j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$ 与 $\widetilde V^-=3e^{j2\pi z}\,\mathrm V$，并给出 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$ 和工作频率。

### Goal

#### English

Find time-domain voltage, reflection coefficient, standing-wave ratio, maximum voltage, net average power and effective impedance, then compare with the undamaged line.

#### 中文

求时域电压、反射系数、驻波比、最大电压、净平均功率与等效阻抗，并与未损坏时比较。

### Approach

#### English

Keep propagation direction and phase conventions explicit. Use the ratio and superposition of the waves, and subtract backward-wave power rather than treating total voltage magnitude as a resistor voltage.

#### 中文

先保留传播方向和相位约定，再从两行波的比值与叠加出发。功率要扣除反向波携带的功率，不能只把总电压幅值代入电阻功率式。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Write the expression for the total voltage wave on the transmission line between its input and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in the time domain.

#### 中文

写出输入端至 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间传输线上总电压波的时域表达式。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the time-domain definition of a phasor, with the [travelling-wave convention](formula:phasor) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用相量的时域定义，与 crib sheet 的[行波相量](formula:phasor)约定一致：

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=\Re\{[\widetilde V^+(z)+\widetilde V^-(z)]e^{j\omega t}\}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given waves $5e^{-j2\pi z}$ and $3e^{j2\pi z}$ at $f=150\,\mathrm{MHz}$, find $v(z,t)$. Substitute both phasors and the [angular frequency](formula:wave) $\omega=2\pi f$:

#### 中文

已知两行波为 $5e^{-j2\pi z}$、$3e^{j2\pi z}$，且 $f=150\,\mathrm{MHz}$，求 $v(z,t)$。把两相量和[角频率](formula:wave) $\omega=2\pi f$ 代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \omega=2\pi f=2\pi(150\times10^6)=3\pi\times10^8\,\mathrm{rad/s}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle v(z,t)=\Re\{[5e^{-j2\pi z}+3e^{j2\pi z}]e^{j3\pi10^8t}\}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle v(z,t)=\Re\{5e^{j(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)}+3e^{j(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)}\}\end{aligned}
$$


### Step 3

#### English

Taking the real part term by term gives

#### 中文

逐项取实部，得

#### Equation

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)+3\cos(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

### Answer

$$
v(z,t)=5\cos(3\pi10^8t-2\pi z)+3\cos(3\pi10^8t+2\pi z)\quad[\mathrm V]
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Calculate the reflection coefficient between the transmission line and $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

#### 中文

计算传输线与 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 之间的反射系数。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [voltage reflection coefficient](formula:reflection) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[电压反射系数](formula:reflection)：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{V_0^-}{V_0^+}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Using the official solution’s effective-terminal reference plane, the forward and backward coefficients are $5\,\mathrm V$ and $3\,\mathrm V$. Find $\Gamma$ by substituting their amplitudes and phases:

#### 中文

沿用官方解的等效终端参考面，正、反向系数为 $5\,\mathrm V$、$3\,\mathrm V$，求 $\Gamma$。代入幅值和相位：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V_0^-=3\,\mathrm V,\qquad V_0^+=5\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma=\frac{3\,\mathrm V}{5\,\mathrm V}=0.6e^{j0}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=+0.60
$$

### Answer

$$
\Gamma=+0.60
$$

### Step 3

This gives

\mathrm{SWR}=4.00

## Part d

### Question

#### English

Calculate the maximum voltage on the transmission line. How does this voltage compare with the maximum voltage on the transmission line when Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ is undamaged? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

计算传输线上的最大电压。该电压与电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 未损坏时传输线上的最大电压相比如何？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [maximum standing-wave envelope relation](formula:swr) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet [驻波包络的最大值关系](formula:swr)：

#### Equation

$$
|V|_{\max}=|V_0^+|+|V_0^-|
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given peak forward and backward amplitudes $5\,\mathrm V$ and $3\,\mathrm V$, find the maximum voltage and compare with the undamaged line. Substitute; the undamaged line has no reflected wave:

#### 中文

已知正、反向峰值幅度为 $5\,\mathrm V$、$3\,\mathrm V$，求最大电压并与未受损时比较。代入；未受损时反向波为零：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle |V|_{\max}=|5|+|3|=8\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_{\max,\mathrm{undamaged}}=5+0=5\,\mathrm V,\qquad \frac{8}{5}=1.6\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives the maximum voltage, $1.6$ times the undamaged value.

#### 中文

得最大电压，为未受损时的 $1.6$ 倍。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\max}=8.00\,\mathrm V
$$

### Answer

$$
V_{\max}=8.00\,\mathrm V
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Calculate the time-average power delivered to $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$. How does this delivered power compare with the power delivered to the load when Circuit $\mathrm{A}$ is undamaged? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

计算传递给 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的时间平均功率。该功率与电路 $\mathrm{A}$ 未损坏时传递给负载的功率相比如何？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [net average-power formula](formula:power) from the crib sheet; the phasors are peak amplitudes:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[净平均功率公式](formula:power)；相量为峰值幅度：

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{|V_0^+|^2}{2Z_0}(1-|\Gamma|^2)
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $V_0^+=5\,\mathrm V$, $V_0^-=3\,\mathrm V$ and $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find the delivered power. Substitute and subtract reflected power from incident power:

#### 中文

已知 $V_0^+=5\,\mathrm V$、$V_0^-=3\,\mathrm V$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求送达功率。把两幅值代入，正向功率减去反向功率：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle P^+=\frac{5^2}{2(50)}=0.25\,\mathrm W,\qquad |P^-|=\frac{3^2}{2(50)}=0.09\,\mathrm W\\[4pt]&\displaystyle P_{\mathrm{av}}=P^+-|P^-|=0.25-0.09=0.16\,\mathrm W\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives a $36\%$ reduction from the undamaged value of $0.25\,\mathrm W$:

#### 中文

得；未受损时为 $0.25\,\mathrm W$，因此下降 $36\%$。

#### Equation

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=0.160\,\mathrm W
$$

### Answer

$$
P_{\mathrm{av}}=0.160\,\mathrm W
$$

## Part f

### Question

#### English

Calculate the value of the impedance $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$. Did the damage to the transmission line increase or decrease its characteristic impedance at that location?

#### 中文

计算阻抗 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 的值。传输线的损坏使该位置的特性阻抗增大还是减小？

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [reflection–impedance relation](formula:reflection) from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[反射系数](formula:reflection)与阻抗关系：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{eff}}-Z_0}{Z_{\mathrm{eff}}+Z_0}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $\Gamma=0.6$ and $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$. Solve for impedance and substitute:

#### 中文

已知 $\Gamma=0.6$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$。解出阻抗后代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \Gamma Z_{\mathrm{eff}}+\Gamma Z_0=Z_{\mathrm{eff}}-Z_0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle (1-\Gamma)Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=(1+\Gamma)Z_0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=Z_0\frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=(50\,\Omega)\frac{1+0.6}{1-0.6}=(50\,\Omega)(4)\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=200\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{eff}}=200\,\Omega
$$

### Answer text

#### English

The effective input impedance increases. $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ alone does not uniquely determine the damaged section’s local characteristic impedance; the official simplified model calls it an increase.

#### 中文

有效输入阻抗增大。仅由 $Z_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 不能唯一还原损坏段本身的局部特性阻抗；原解在其简化模型下称为增大。
