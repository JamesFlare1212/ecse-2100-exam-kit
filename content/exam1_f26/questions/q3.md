## Question

### English

In each of the cases below:
1. Calculate $Z_{in}$.
2. State whether or not the generator circuit sees a matched load.
Briefly justify your answer using a transmission line property or impedance matching method. Answers relying solely on the general mathematical equation for $Z_{in}$ will receive reduced credit.

### 中文

对下列每一种情况：
1. 计算 $Z_{in}$。
2. 判断发生器电路所见的负载是否匹配。
使用传输线性质或阻抗匹配方法简要说明答案。仅依赖 $Z_{in}$ 通用数学公式的答案会被扣分。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem tests recognition of line properties rather than substitution into the general input-impedance formula.

#### 中文

本题考查能否识别传输线的特殊性质，而不是直接套用通用输入阻抗公式。

### Givens

#### English

The diagrams supply terminations, source resistances and lengths including $\lambda/6$, $\lambda/2$, $\lambda/4$ and a parallel open stub. The characteristic impedance is $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

各电路给出终端、发生器电阻与线长，包括 $\lambda/6$、$\lambda/2$、$\lambda/4$ 及并联开路支节；线的特性阻抗为 $50\,\Omega$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the impedance seen by the generator in each circuit and decide whether the source is matched.

#### 中文

分别求发生器看到的输入阻抗，并据此判断源端是否匹配。

### Approach

#### English

Check for a matched termination, then recognize half-wave repetition and quarter-wave transformation. Combine a parallel stub in admittance before comparing the resulting input impedance with the source resistance.

#### 中文

先检查终端是否已匹配，再识别半波重复与四分之一波变换。并联支节应先合成导纳，最后才将总输入阻抗与发生器电阻比较。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/6$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/6$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the matched-load property rather than only the general impedance formula. First check load reflection.

#### 中文

优先使用匹配负载的性质，而不是只代通用输入阻抗式。先检验负载反射系数。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{R_L-Z_0}{R_L+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

The original circuit has a $50\,\Omega$ load and $50\,\Omega$ line, so $\Gamma_L=0$.

#### 中文

原图负载和线阻抗均为 $50\,\Omega$，因此 $\Gamma_L=0$。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{50-50}{50+50}=0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

With only a forward wave, $V/I$ is $Z_{0}$ everywhere; a $\lambda/6$ length therefore leaves the input unchanged.

#### 中文

只有正向行波时，任意位置 $V/I$ 均为 $Z_{0}$；所以 $\lambda/6$ 长度也不改变输入阻抗。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{V^+e^{-j\beta z}}{(V^+/Z_0)e^{-j\beta z}}=Z_0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The $50\,\Omega$ input equals source resistance and is matched.

#### 中文

输入 $50\,\Omega$ 等于源电阻，因此是匹配的。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/2$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/2$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib half-wave impedance-repeat property.

#### 中文

先写原表的半波阻抗重复性质。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L
$$

#### References

- [half](formula:half)

### Step 2

#### English

A half wavelength gives $\beta L=\pi$ and round-trip phase $2\pi$, repeating the load voltage/current ratio at the input.

#### 中文

半波长度对应 $\beta L=\pi$，一次往返相位为 $2\pi$，输入端与负载的电压电流比重复。

#### Equation

$$
2\beta L=2\pi,\qquad \Gamma_{\mathrm{in}}=\Gamma_Le^{-j2\pi}=\Gamma_L
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Substitute the original $25\,\Omega$ load to obtain a $25\,\Omega$ input, even though line $Z_{0}$ is $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

把原图 $25\,\Omega$ 负载代入，得到输入也是 $25\,\Omega$，虽然线本身的 $Z_{0}$ 为 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega=R_g
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Compare the whole input seen by the source with source resistance; this circuit is matched.

#### 中文

比较的是源看到的整体输入与源电阻，故本电路匹配。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/4$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/4$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib quarter-wave impedance inversion.

#### 中文

先写原表的四分之一波长阻抗反演性质。

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute $Z_{0}=50\,\Omega$ and load $100\,\Omega$, retaining units in the square.

#### 中文

代入 $Z_{0}=50\,\Omega$、负载 $100\,\Omega$，保留平方的单位。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(50\,\Omega)^2}{100\,\Omega}=\frac{2500}{100}\,\Omega=25\,\Omega
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The circuit source is $25\,\Omega$, so the line is exactly a quarter-wave transformer between $25\,\Omega$ and $100\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

原图源电阻是 $25\,\Omega$，因此这段线正是匹配 $25\,\Omega$ 与 $100\,\Omega$ 的四分之一波变换线。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{R_gR_L}=\sqrt{25\cdot100}=50\,\Omega
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The input equals source resistance and is matched.

#### 中文

输入阻抗等于源电阻，判断为匹配。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to a feedline of characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$. The feedline is connected to the parallel combination of two other transmission lines:
• a line of length $d$, input admittance $Y_d$, and terminating impedance $Z_L$
• a line of length $l$, input admittance $Y_s$, and an open-circuit termination

#### 中文

发生器电路连接到特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的馈线。馈线与另外两根传输线的并联组合相连：
• 长度为 $d$、输入导纳为 $Y_d$、终端阻抗为 $Z_L$ 的传输线
• 长度为 $l$、输入导纳为 $Y_s$、终端开路的传输线

### Step 1

#### English

The parallel main line and stub share a voltage. Begin by adding branch currents to obtain admittance addition.

#### 中文

并联主线与支节共享电压，先由两支路电流相加推导导纳相加。

#### Equation

$$
I_{\mathrm{in}}=Y_dV+Y_sV=(Y_d+Y_s)V\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Y_{\mathrm{in}}=Y_d+Y_s
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute both given admittances with units and add real and imaginary components separately.

#### 中文

代入题给的两个有单位导纳，按实部和虚部分别相加。

#### Equation

$$
Y_{\mathrm{in}}=(0.02+j0.04)\,\mathrm S+(-j0.04)\,\mathrm S
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The main-line and open-stub susceptances cancel, leaving a purely real admittance.

#### 中文

主线感性电纳与开路支节电纳抵消，总导纳成为纯实数。

#### Equation

$$
Y_{\mathrm{in}}=0.02+j(0.04-0.04)=0.02\,\mathrm S
$$

#### References

- [open](formula:open)

### Step 4

#### English

Invert admittance to obtain node impedance; reciprocal siemens are ohms.

#### 中文

导纳取倒数得到节点阻抗；西门子取倒数的单位为欧姆。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac1{0.02\,\mathrm S}=50\,\Omega
$$

### Step 5

#### English

The node matches the $50\,\Omega$ feedline and source; the matched feedline repeats that impedance to the source.

#### 中文

节点与 $50\,\Omega$ 馈线和源匹配；匹配馈线将这个阻抗重复到源。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$
