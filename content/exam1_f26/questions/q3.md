## Question

### English

In each of the cases below:
1. Calculate $Z_{in}$.
2. State whether or not the generator circuit sees a matched load.
Briefly justify your answer using a transmission line property or impedance matching method. Answers relying solely on the general mathematical equation for $Z_{in}$ will receive reduced credit.

### 中文

对下列每一种情况：
1. 计算 $Z_{in}$。
2. 判断发生器电路所见的负载是否匹配。
使用传输线性质或阻抗匹配方法简要说明答案。仅依赖 $Z_{in}$ 通用数学公式的答案会被扣分。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem tests recognition of line properties rather than substitution into the general input-impedance formula.

#### 中文

本题考查能否识别传输线的特殊性质，而不是直接套用通用输入阻抗公式。

### Givens

#### English

The diagrams supply terminations, source resistances and lengths including $\lambda/6$, $\lambda/2$, $\lambda/4$ and a parallel open stub. The characteristic impedance is $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

各电路给出终端、发生器电阻与线长，包括 $\lambda/6$、$\lambda/2$、$\lambda/4$ 及并联开路支节；线的特性阻抗为 $50\,\Omega$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the impedance seen by the generator in each circuit and decide whether the source is matched.

#### 中文

分别求发生器看到的输入阻抗，并据此判断源端是否匹配。

### Approach

#### English

Check for a matched termination, then recognize half-wave repetition and quarter-wave transformation. Combine a parallel stub in admittance before comparing the resulting input impedance with the source resistance.

#### 中文

先检查终端是否已匹配，再识别半波重复与四分之一波变换。并联支节应先合成导纳，最后才将总输入阻抗与发生器电阻比较。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/6$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/6$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the reflection coefficient and matched-load property from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的反射系数和匹配负载性质：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{R_L-Z_0}{R_L+Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $R_L=Z_0=50\,\Omega$ and $L=\lambda/6$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ and assess matching. The reflection vanishes, so the voltage-to-current ratio equals $Z_0$ at any length:

#### 中文

已知 $R_L=Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$L=\lambda/6$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ 并判断匹配。代入得无反向波，因此任意长度的电压电流比均为 $Z_0$：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \Gamma_L=\frac{50-50}{50+50}=0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{V^+e^{-j\beta z}}{(V^+/Z_0)e^{-j\beta z}}=Z_0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Comparing with $R_g=50\,\Omega$ gives

#### 中文

与 $R_g=50\,\Omega$ 比较，得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/2$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/2$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the half-wave impedance repetition from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的半波阻抗重复性质：

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=Z_L
$$

#### References

- [half](formula:half)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $L=\lambda/2$ and $R_L=R_g=25\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ and assess matching. The round-trip phase is $2\pi$, so substitute the repeated load:

#### 中文

已知 $L=\lambda/2$、$R_L=R_g=25\,\Omega$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ 并判断匹配。半波往返相位为 $2\pi$，代入负载：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle 2\beta L=2\pi,\qquad \Gamma_{\mathrm{in}}=\Gamma_Le^{-j2\pi}=\Gamma_L\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega=R_g\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part c

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to the load via a transmission line of length $L=\lambda/4$ and characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$.

#### 中文

发生器电路通过长度为 $L=\lambda/4$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的传输线连接到负载。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the quarter-wave impedance inversion from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的四分之一波长阻抗反演：

#### Equation

$$
L=\frac{\lambda}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{Z_0^2}{Z_L}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_0=50\,\Omega$, $R_L=100\,\Omega$ and $R_g=25\,\Omega$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ and assess matching. Substitute into the inversion:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_0=50\,\Omega$、$R_L=100\,\Omega$、$R_g=25\,\Omega$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ 并判断匹配。代入反演式：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac{(50\,\Omega)^2}{100\,\Omega}=\frac{2500}{100}\,\Omega=25\,\Omega\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_0=\sqrt{R_gR_L}=\sqrt{25\cdot100}=50\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=25\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

The generator circuit is connected to a feedline of characteristic impedance $Z_0=50\Omega$. The feedline is connected to the parallel combination of two other transmission lines:
• a line of length $d$, input admittance $Y_d$, and terminating impedance $Z_L$
• a line of length $l$, input admittance $Y_s$, and an open-circuit termination

#### 中文

发生器电路连接到特性阻抗为 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的馈线。馈线与另外两根传输线的并联组合相连：
• 长度为 $d$、输入导纳为 $Y_d$、终端阻抗为 $Z_L$ 的传输线
• 长度为 $l$、输入导纳为 $Y_s$、终端开路的传输线

### Step 1

#### English

Use admittance addition, obtained by summing the parallel branch currents:

#### 中文

使用并联支路电流相加得到的导纳关系：

#### Equation

$$
I_{\mathrm{in}}=Y_dV+Y_sV=(Y_d+Y_s)V\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Y_{\mathrm{in}}=Y_d+Y_s
$$

### Step 2

#### English

Given $Y_d=0.02+j0.04\,\mathrm S$ and $Y_s=-j0.04\,\mathrm S$, find $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ and assess matching. Add the admittances and take the reciprocal:

#### 中文

已知 $Y_d=0.02+j0.04\,\mathrm S$、$Y_s=-j0.04\,\mathrm S$，求 $Z_{\mathrm{in}}$ 并判断匹配。代入相加，再取倒数：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle Y_{\mathrm{in}}=(0.02+j0.04)\,\mathrm S+(-j0.04)\,\mathrm S\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Y_{\mathrm{in}}=0.02+j(0.04-0.04)=0.02\,\mathrm S\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac1{0.02\,\mathrm S}=50\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The susceptances cancel, yielding the $50\,\Omega$ feedline and source impedance:

#### 中文

虚部抵消，所得阻抗等于馈线与源的 $50\,\Omega$，得

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in}}=50\,\Omega\quad\text{matched}
$$
