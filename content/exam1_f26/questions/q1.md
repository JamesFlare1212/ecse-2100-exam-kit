## Question

### English

The transmission line model below consists of segments of length $\Delta z\ll\lambda$ and the following circuit element parameters:
• $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$
• $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$

### 中文

下方传输线模型由长度为 $\Delta z\ll\lambda$ 的小段组成，其电路元件参数如下：
• $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$
• $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects a distributed line model to lossless propagation and a DC bounce diagram.

#### 中文

本题从分布参数模型出发，把无损线的传播性质与直流反弹图连接起来。

### Givens

#### English

The model supplies $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}$ and $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}$ for a $60\,\mathrm m$ cable. The later circuit supplies its source and load parameters.

#### 中文

模型给出 $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}$、$c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}$，电缆长度为 $60\,\mathrm m$；后半题的源与负载参数见电路图。

### Goal

#### English

Identify loss, find velocity factor, characteristic impedance and delay, then calculate reflections and the requested endpoint voltages after switching.

#### 中文

判断线是否有损，求速度因子、特性阻抗与延迟，再计算开关闭合后的反射和指定端点电压。

### Approach

#### English

First identify the storage and loss elements in the model, then calculate the line parameters. Use them in the bounce diagram, distinguishing individual wave amplitudes from summed endpoint voltages.

#### 中文

先确认模型包含哪些储能或耗能元件，再求线参数。反弹分析使用这些参数作为输入，并把单个行波幅值与端点叠加电压区分开。

## Part a(i)

### Question

#### English

Is the transmission line that is represented by the model above a lossy transmission line or a lossless transmission line? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

上方模型表示的传输线是有损线还是无损线？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with propagation constant in terms of distributed parameters; its real part $\alpha$ is attenuation.

#### 中文

先写传播常数的分布参数表达式，实部 $\alpha$ 表示衰减。

#### Equation

$$
\gamma=\alpha+j\beta=\sqrt{(r+j\omega l)(g+j\omega c)}
$$

#### References

- [lossy-wave](formula:lossy-wave)

### Step 2

#### English

Inspect the original cell: it contains series $l\Delta z$ and shunt $c\Delta z$, with no dissipative $r\Delta z$ or $g\Delta z$ elements.

#### 中文

逐项检查原图：只有串联电感 $l\Delta z$ 和并联电容 $c\Delta z$，没有耗散元件 $r\Delta z$ 或 $g\Delta z$。

#### Equation

$$
r=0,\qquad g=0,\qquad l>0,\quad c>0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Insert zero resistance and conductance to obtain a purely imaginary propagation constant.

#### 中文

把零电阻、零漏电导代入传播常数，得到纯虚数。

#### Equation

$$
\gamma=\sqrt{(j\omega l)(j\omega c)}=j\omega\sqrt{lc}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\alpha=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The model is lossless: inductors and capacitors exchange stored energy with no resistive average power dissipation.

#### 中文

因此该模型是无损线；电感和电容交换储能，没有电阻性平均功率损耗。

#### Equation

$$
\text{Lossless: }r=g=0
$$

### Answer

$$
\text{Lossless: }r=g=0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

A lossless line.

#### 中文

无损线。

## Part a(ii)

### Question

#### English

Calculate the velocity factor and characteristic impedance of the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算传输线的速度因子和特性阻抗。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib lossless speed, characteristic impedance and velocity-factor relations.

#### 中文

先写原表的无损线波速、特性阻抗和速度因子定义。

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac1{\sqrt{lc}},\qquad Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc},\qquad \mathrm{vf}=\frac u{u_c}
$$

#### References

- [velocity](formula:velocity)
- [impedance](formula:impedance)

### Step 2

#### English

Convert the per-length parameters to SI units, then form their product.

#### 中文

先将每单位长度参数转为 SI 单位，再代入乘积。

#### Equation

$$
l=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m},\quad c=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m},\quad lc=1.7375\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^2}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Take the square root of the product and invert to obtain speed.

#### 中文

对乘积开方后取倒数，求得波速。

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac1{\sqrt{1.7375\times10^{-17}}}=2.3990406\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Divide by vacuum light speed for the dimensionless velocity factor.

#### 中文

再除以真空光速，计算无量纲速度因子。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{vf}=\frac{2.3990406\times10^8}{3.00\times10^8}=0.799680\approx0.80
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Independently evaluate the square root of $l/c$; the square root of $\mathrm{H}/\mathrm{F}$ has units $\Omega$.

#### 中文

独立计算 $l/c$ 的平方根，$\mathrm{H}/\mathrm{F}$ 的平方根单位是 $\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_0=\sqrt{\frac{3.125\times10^{-7}}{5.56\times10^{-11}}}=\sqrt{5620.5036}\,\Omega\approx74.97\,\Omega
$$

### Step 6

#### English

State both results. The later bounce diagram uses rounded $Z_{0}\approx75\,\Omega$, as in the official solution.

#### 中文

列出两个结果。后面的反弹图按标准答案使用舍入值 $Z_{0}\approx75\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{vf}\approx0.80,\qquad Z_0\approx74.97\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
\mathrm{vf}\approx0.80,\qquad Z_0\approx74.97\,\Omega
$$

## Part a(iii)

### Question

#### English

If the transmission line length is $L=60\mathrm{m}$, calculate the time delay $T_d$ of the transmission line.

#### 中文

若传输线长度为 $L=60\mathrm{m}$，计算其时间延迟 $T_d$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with propagation distance versus one-way time and solve for $T_d$.

#### 中文

先写传播距离与单程时间的关系，并解出 $T_d$。

#### Equation

$$
L=uT_d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T_d=\frac Lu
$$

#### References

- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute $60\,\mathrm{m}$ and the unrounded speed from the preceding part.

#### 中文

将 $60\,\mathrm{m}$ 与上问未舍入的波速代入。

#### Equation

$$
T_d=\frac{60\,\mathrm m}{2.3990406\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}=2.5009998\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm s
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Convert seconds to nanoseconds using $1\,\mathrm{s}=10^{9}\,\mathrm{ns}$.

#### 中文

秒转换为纳秒，$1\,\mathrm{s}=10^{9}\,\mathrm{ns}$。

#### Equation

$$
T_d=(2.5009998\times10^{-7})(10^9)\,\mathrm{ns}=250.100\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

This agrees to the required precision with the official $250\,\mathrm{ns}$ using $\mathrm{vf}=0.8$.

#### 中文

与原解采用 $\mathrm{vf}=0.8$ 的 $250\,\mathrm{ns}$ 一致到所需精度。

#### Equation

$$
T_d\approx250.1\,\mathrm{ns}\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### Answer

$$
T_d\approx250.1\,\mathrm{ns}\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Now consider the DC circuit below, which contains a switch that closes at $t=0$, and a transmission line with the same properties as specified by the model in part a).

For the bounce diagram below, calculate the following parameters:
• All reflection coefficients
• All voltage amplitudes
• The voltage at the load $V_L$ at $t=T_d$
• The voltage at the input to the transmission line $V_{in}$ at $t=2T_d$

#### 中文

现在考虑下方直流电路，其中有一个在 $t=0$ 闭合的开关，以及一根性质与第 a) 问模型规定相同的传输线。

对下方反弹图，计算下列参数：
• 所有反射系数
• 所有电压幅值
• $t=T_d$ 时的负载电压 $V_L$
• $t=2T_d$ 时的传输线输入电压 $V_{in}$

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with endpoint reflection, the launch divider and the traveling-wave recurrence.

#### 中文

先写端点反射系数、初始分压和行波反射递推式。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{R-Z_0}{R+Z_0},\quad V_1^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0},\quad V_n^-=\Gamma_LV_n^+,\quad V_{n+1}^+=\Gamma_gV_n^-
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)
- [bounce](formula:bounce)

### Step 2

#### English

Use the official rounded line impedance of $75\,\Omega$ and the $25\,\Omega$ load.

#### 中文

沿用标准答案舍入的 $75\,\Omega$ 线路阻抗；负载为 $25\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{25-75}{25+75}=\frac{-50}{100}=-\frac12
$$

### Step 3

#### English

The source resistance is also $75\,\Omega$, so returning waves are absorbed without another forward wave.

#### 中文

源电阻同样为 $75\,\Omega$，所以返回源端的波被吸收，不再产生新正向波。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_g=\frac{75-75}{75+75}=0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Find the first forward voltage at $t=0$ by dividing the $12\,\mathrm{V}$ source.

#### 中文

$t=0$ 时发出的第一正向电压由 $12\,\mathrm{V}$ 分压求得。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^+=(12\,\mathrm V)\frac{75}{75+75}=6\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 5

#### English

On reaching the load at $T_d\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}$, the reflected voltage changes sign because $\Gamma_L<0$.

#### 中文

在 $T_d\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}$ 到达负载，反射电压因 $\Gamma_L<0$ 而反相。

#### Equation

$$
V_1^-=\Gamma_LV_1^+=-\frac12(6)=-3\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 6

#### English

At $2T_d\approx500\,\mathrm{ns}$ the return reaches the source. Multiplication by $\Gamma_g=0$ makes subsequent traveling waves zero.

#### 中文

在 $2T_d\approx500\,\mathrm{ns}$ 返回源后，再乘 $\Gamma_g=0$，后续正反向行波都为零。

#### Equation

$$
V_2^+=\Gamma_gV_1^-=0(-3)=0,\qquad V_2^-=\Gamma_LV_2^+=0
$$

### Step 7

#### English

At the first load arrival the measured voltage is the sum of incident and immediate reflected waves.

#### 中文

负载第一次到达时测到入射和即时反射的代数和。

#### Equation

$$
V_L(T_d)=V_1^++V_1^-=6+(-3)=3\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 8

#### English

The source is sustained DC. At $500\,\mathrm{ns}$ include the existing $6\,\mathrm{V}$ input plus the return and its new reflection.

#### 中文

源是持续 DC，$500\,\mathrm{ns}$ 后的输入电压需计入此前已有的 $6\,\mathrm{V}$，再加返回波和新反射波。

#### Equation

$$
V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=V_1^++V_1^-+V_2^+=6-3+0=3\,\mathrm V
$$

### Step 9

#### English

Label all nonzero traveling amplitudes and measured endpoint voltages; after $\Gamma_g=0$ there is no further nonzero traveling trajectory.

#### 中文

所有非零幅度与端点电压都应标在反弹图上；$\Gamma_g=0$ 后没有继续传播的非零轨迹。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}\Gamma_L=-0.5,\quad\Gamma_g=0\\V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V,\quad V_1^-=-3\,\mathrm V,\quad V_2^+=V_2^-=0\\V_L(T_d)=V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=3\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}\Gamma_L=-0.5,\quad\Gamma_g=0\\V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V,\;V_1^-=-3\,\mathrm V,\;V_2^+=V_2^-=0\\V_L(T_d)=V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=3\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$
