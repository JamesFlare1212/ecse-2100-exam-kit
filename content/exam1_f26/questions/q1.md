## Question

### English

The transmission line model below consists of segments of length $\Delta z\ll\lambda$ and the following circuit element parameters:
• $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$
• $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$

### 中文

下方传输线模型由长度为 $\Delta z\ll\lambda$ 的小段组成，其电路元件参数如下：
• $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$
• $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem connects a distributed line model to lossless propagation and a DC bounce diagram.

#### 中文

本题从分布参数模型出发，把无损线的传播性质与直流反弹图连接起来。

### Givens

#### English

The model supplies $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}$ and $c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}$ for a $60\,\mathrm m$ cable. The later circuit supplies its source and load parameters.

#### 中文

模型给出 $l=0.3125\,\mathrm{\mu H/m}$、$c=55.6\,\mathrm{pF/m}$，电缆长度为 $60\,\mathrm m$；后半题的源与负载参数见电路图。

### Goal

#### English

Identify loss, find velocity factor, characteristic impedance and delay, then calculate reflections and the requested endpoint voltages after switching.

#### 中文

判断线是否有损，求速度因子、特性阻抗与延迟，再计算开关闭合后的反射和指定端点电压。

### Approach

#### English

First identify the storage and loss elements in the model, then calculate the line parameters. Use them in the bounce diagram, distinguishing individual wave amplitudes from summed endpoint voltages.

#### 中文

先确认模型包含哪些储能或耗能元件，再求线参数。反弹分析使用这些参数作为输入，并把单个行波幅值与端点叠加电压区分开。

## Part a(i)

### Question

#### English

Is the transmission line that is represented by the model above a lossy transmission line or a lossless transmission line? Briefly explain.

#### 中文

上方模型表示的传输线是有损线还是无损线？简要解释。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s [propagation-constant notation](formula:lossy-wave), whose real part $\alpha$ is attenuation:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[传播常数表示](formula:lossy-wave)，实部 $\alpha$ 表示衰减：

#### Equation

$$
\gamma=\alpha+j\beta
$$


### Step 2

#### English

The diagram contains only inductors and capacitors. Determine whether the line is lossy by substituting $r=g=0$ into its distributed-parameter propagation constant:

#### 中文

已知图中只有电感、电容，求线路是否有损。把模型的 $r=g=0$ 代入分布参数传播常数：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle r=0,\qquad g=0,\qquad l>0,\quad c>0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \gamma=\sqrt{(j\omega l)(j\omega c)}=j\omega\sqrt{lc}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\alpha=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\text{Lossless: }r=g=0
$$

### Answer

$$
\text{Lossless: }r=g=0
$$

### Answer text

#### English

A lossless line.

#### 中文

无损线。

## Part a(ii)

### Question

#### English

Calculate the velocity factor and characteristic impedance of the transmission line.

#### 中文

计算传输线的速度因子和特性阻抗。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [wave speed](formula:velocity), [characteristic impedance](formula:impedance) and velocity factor from the crib sheet:

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[波速](formula:velocity)、[特性阻抗](formula:impedance)与速度因子公式：

#### Equation

$$
u=\frac1{\sqrt{lc}},\qquad Z_0=\sqrt{\frac lc},\qquad \mathrm{vf}=\frac u{u_c}
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $l=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$ and $c=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$, find the velocity factor and $Z_0$. Substitute:

#### 中文

已知 $l=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m}$、$c=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m}$，求速度因子和 $Z_0$。把参数代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle l=3.125\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H/m},\quad c=5.56\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F/m},\quad lc=1.7375\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm{s^2/m^2}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle u=\frac1{\sqrt{1.7375\times10^{-17}}}=2.3990406\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \mathrm{vf}=\frac{2.3990406\times10^8}{3.00\times10^8}=0.799680\approx0.80\\[4pt]&\displaystyle Z_0=\sqrt{\frac{3.125\times10^{-7}}{5.56\times10^{-11}}}=\sqrt{5620.5036}\,\Omega\approx74.97\,\Omega\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\mathrm{vf}\approx0.80,\qquad Z_0\approx74.97\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
\mathrm{vf}\approx0.80,\qquad Z_0\approx74.97\,\Omega
$$

## Part a(iii)

### Question

#### English

If the transmission line length is $L=60\mathrm{m}$, calculate the time delay $T_d$ of the transmission line.

#### 中文

若传输线长度为 $L=60\mathrm{m}$，计算其时间延迟 $T_d$。

### Step 1

#### English

Use the [one-way transit-time relation](formula:bounce) from Lecture 04:

#### 中文

使用 Lecture 04 的[单程传播时间关系](formula:bounce)：

#### Equation

$$
L=uT_d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T_d=\frac Lu
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $L=60\,\mathrm m$, find $T_d$. Substitute the unrounded speed from (a)(ii):

#### 中文

已知 $L=60\,\mathrm m$，求 $T_d$。把 (a)(ii) 的未舍入波速代入：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle T_d=\frac{60\,\mathrm m}{2.3990406\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}}=2.5009998\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm s\\[4pt]&\displaystyle T_d=(2.5009998\times10^{-7})(10^9)\,\mathrm{ns}=250.100\,\mathrm{ns}\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
T_d\approx250.1\,\mathrm{ns}\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### Answer

$$
T_d\approx250.1\,\mathrm{ns}\approx250\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

Now consider the DC circuit below, which contains a switch that closes at $t=0$, and a transmission line with the same properties as specified by the model in part a).

For the bounce diagram below, calculate the following parameters:
• All reflection coefficients
• All voltage amplitudes
• The voltage at the load $V_L$ at $t=T_d$
• The voltage at the input to the transmission line $V_{in}$ at $t=2T_d$

#### 中文

现在考虑下方直流电路，其中有一个在 $t=0$ 闭合的开关，以及一根性质与第 a) 问模型规定相同的传输线。

对下方反弹图，计算下列参数：
• 所有反射系数
• 所有电压幅值
• $t=T_d$ 时的负载电压 $V_L$
• $t=2T_d$ 时的传输线输入电压 $V_{in}$

### Step 1

#### English

Use the crib sheet’s [reflection coefficient](formula:reflection) and Lecture 04’s [initial voltage divider](formula:bounce):

#### 中文

使用 crib sheet 的[反射系数](formula:reflection)和 Lecture 04 的[初始分压式](formula:bounce)：

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma=\frac{R-Z_0}{R+Z_0},\quad V_1^+=V_g\frac{Z_0}{R_g+Z_0},\quad V_n^-=\Gamma_LV_n^+,\quad V_{n+1}^+=\Gamma_gV_n^-
$$


### Step 2

#### English

Given $Z_0\approx75\,\Omega$, $R_L=25\,\Omega$, $R_g=75\,\Omega$ and $V_g=12\,\mathrm V$, find the bounces and endpoint voltages. First evaluate both reflections and the launch voltage:

#### 中文

已知 $Z_0\approx75\,\Omega$、$R_L=25\,\Omega$、$R_g=75\,\Omega$、$V_g=12\,\mathrm V$，求各反弹幅值与端点电压。先代入两端反射和初始分压：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle \Gamma_L=\frac{25-75}{25+75}=\frac{-50}{100}=-\frac12\\[4pt]&\displaystyle \Gamma_g=\frac{75-75}{75+75}=0\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_1^+=(12\,\mathrm V)\frac{75}{75+75}=6\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Apply the reflection relations to $V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V$. The matched source terminates subsequent bounces:

#### 中文

把 $V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V$ 依次代入反射关系；源端匹配使后续行波归零：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V_1^-=\Gamma_LV_1^+=-\frac12(6)=-3\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_2^+=\Gamma_gV_1^-=0(-3)=0,\qquad V_2^-=\Gamma_LV_2^+=0\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Add each arriving and reflected wave to the existing voltage to find the load at $T_d$ and input at $2T_d$:

#### 中文

把到达波与反射波加到已有电压上，分别求 $T_d$ 的负载电压和 $2T_d$ 的输入电压：

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}&\displaystyle V_L(T_d)=V_1^++V_1^-=6+(-3)=3\,\mathrm V\\[4pt]&\displaystyle V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=V_1^++V_1^-+V_2^+=6-3+0=3\,\mathrm V\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

This gives

#### 中文

得

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}\Gamma_L=-0.5,\quad\Gamma_g=0\\V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V,\;V_1^-=-3\,\mathrm V,\;V_2^+=V_2^-=0\\V_L(T_d)=V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=3\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}\Gamma_L=-0.5,\quad\Gamma_g=0\\V_1^+=6\,\mathrm V,\;V_1^-=-3\,\mathrm V,\;V_2^+=V_2^-=0\\V_L(T_d)=V_{\mathrm{in}}(2T_d)=3\,\mathrm V\end{gathered}
$$
