## Question

### English

The load in the circuit below is to be matched to a $Z_0=50\Omega$ feedline using a quarter-wave transformer of characteristic impedance $Z_{0T}$ and a length of transmission line $d$ of characteristic impedance $Z_0$. The velocity factor of the transmission line of length $d$ is $0.85$ and the frequency of the signal on the line is $f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

### 中文

下方电路的负载需要通过特性阻抗为 $Z_{0T}$ 的四分之一波长变换器，以及一段长度为 $d$、特性阻抗为 $Z_0$ 的传输线，匹配到 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 的馈线。长度为 $d$ 的传输线速度因子为 $0.85$，线上信号的频率为 $f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}=100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}$。

## Guide

### Concepts

#### English

This problem first transforms a complex load to a real impedance, then completes the match with a quarter-wave transformer.

#### 中文

本题考查先用主线消去复负载的虚部，再用四分之一波长变换器完成匹配。

### Givens

#### English

The load is $Z_L=(150-j50)\,\Omega$ and the feedline is $50\,\Omega$. The section of length $d$ has velocity factor $0.85$ at $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$.

#### 中文

$Z_L=(150-j50)\,\Omega$、$Z_0=50\,\Omega$，长度为 $d$ 的线速度因子为 $0.85$，工作频率为 $100\,\mathrm{MHz}$。

### Goal

#### English

Find the two real input impedances on the Smith chart, choose a branch for physical length and transformer impedance, and identify interfaces where reflections remain.

#### 中文

在 Smith 图上找出两种纯实输入阻抗，选一支求实际线长与变换器阻抗，并判断系统内部哪些界面仍有反射。

### Approach

#### English

Find the real-axis crossings on the load's constant-reflection circle and convert wavelength readings to meters. The quarter-wave section performs the second transformation; a matched feedline does not imply that every internal interface is matched.

#### 中文

先沿负载的等反射系数圆找到实轴交点，再把图上的波长读数换成米。四分之一波变换器负责第二次阻抗变换；馈线匹配并不等于内部每个界面都匹配。

## Part a

### Question

#### English

Calculate the normalized load impedance. Plot it on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

计算归一化负载阻抗，并在 Smith 图上标出。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with normalized load impedance referenced to the specified $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

先写归一化负载阻抗定义，参考阻抗为题设 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_0}
$$

#### References

- [zin](formula:zin)

### Step 2

#### English

Divide the real $150\,\Omega$ and imaginary $-j50\,\Omega$ parts separately by $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

把实部 $150\,\Omega$ 与虚部 $-j50\,\Omega$ 分别除以 $50\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=\frac{(150-j50)\,\Omega}{50\,\Omega}=\frac{150}{50}-j\frac{50}{50}=3-j
$$

### Step 3

#### English

On the Smith chart intersect $r=3$ with $x=-1$; negative reactance lies in the lower half.

#### 中文

在 Smith 图上找 $r=3$ 圆与 $x=-1$ 弧的交点；负电抗位于下半图。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{z_L-1}{z_L+1}=\frac{2-j}{4-j}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 4

#### English

The denominator cross terms cancel, while the numerator cross terms must be combined. Substitute $j^2=-1$ explicitly to show the positive $1$ in the numerator.

#### 中文

分母的交叉项相消，而分子的交叉项需要相加。把 $j^2=-1$ 单独代入，说明分子中的正 $1$ 从哪里来。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}(2-j)(4+j)&=8+2j-4j-j^2\\&=9-2j\\(4-j)(4+j)&=16-j^2=17\end{aligned}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Use the conjugate to obtain the reflection-plane coordinate; subsequent real-axis intersections and rotations follow its magnitude circle.

#### 中文

用共轭数求反射系数坐标，后续实轴交点和旋转都沿同一个等模圆。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_L=\frac{(2-j)(4+j)}{(4-j)(4+j)}=\frac{9-2j}{17}
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The plotted normalized load impedance is:

#### 中文

标出的归一化负载阻抗如下。

#### Equation

$$
z_L=3-j
$$

### Answer

$$
z_L=3-j
$$

## Part b

### Question

#### English

What are the two values of purely real input impedance $Z_{in,d}$ that the transmission line of length $d$ could present? Show your work on the Smith chart. Give your impedances in unnormalized format.

#### 中文

长度为 $d$ 的传输线能够呈现的两种纯实数输入阻抗 $Z_{in,d}$ 是多少？在 Smith 图上展示过程。给出未归一化的阻抗。

### Step 1

#### English

Invert the reflection equation for normalized impedance. Purely real input impedance corresponds to real-axis $\Gamma$ on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

先由反射系数反解归一化阻抗。纯实输入阻抗对应 Smith 图实轴上的 $\Gamma$。

#### Equation

$$
z=\frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Derive the inverse impedance relation by cross-multiplying the reflection definition, moving the terms containing $z$ to one side and factoring them.

#### 中文

为说明阻抗反解式的来源，从反射系数的定义交叉相乘，再把含 $z$ 的项移到同一边并提取公因子。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{aligned}\Gamma&=\frac{z-1}{z+1}\\\Gamma z+\Gamma&=z-1\\z-\Gamma z&=1+\Gamma\\(1-\Gamma)z&=1+\Gamma\\z&=\frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}\end{aligned}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 3

#### English

Moving toward the source on a lossless line changes phase but not magnitude. Calculate the circle radius from the load.

#### 中文

无损线向源移动只改变相位，不改变 $\Gamma$ 的模；先由负载值求该圆的半径。

#### Equation

$$
\rho=|\Gamma_L|=\left|\frac{9-2j}{17}\right|=\frac{\sqrt{9^2+2^2}}{17}=\frac{\sqrt{85}}{17}\approx0.542326
$$

### Step 4

#### English

The two real-axis intersections are $+\rho$ and $-\rho$. Substitute both into the impedance relation.

#### 中文

实轴上的两个交点为 $+\rho$ 与 $-\rho$；分别代入阻抗反解式。

#### Equation

$$
r_{\min}=\frac{1-\rho}{1+\rho},\qquad r_{\max}=\frac{1+\rho}{1-\rho}
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Insert the radius to obtain reciprocal dimensionless resistances.

#### 中文

代入半径，得到两个无量纲电阻值，互为倒数。

#### Equation

$$
r_{\min}\approx0.2967425905,\qquad r_{\max}\approx3.3699240762
$$

### Step 6

#### English

The question asks for unnormalized values, so multiply each chart reading by $50\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

题目要求去归一化，分别乘回 $50\,\Omega$，而不是直接把图上刻度当欧姆。

#### Equation

$$
R_{\min}=50(0.2967425905)\,\Omega,\qquad R_{\max}=50(3.3699240762)\,\Omega
$$

### Step 7

#### English

These values are computed from the exact stated load; the official chart estimates remain documented in the source note.

#### 中文

结果由给定复负载精确计算；官方图上的近似读数保留在核对说明中。

#### Equation

$$
R_{\min}=14.8371\,\Omega,\qquad R_{\max}=168.4962\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
R_{\min}=14.8371\,\Omega,\qquad R_{\max}=168.4962\,\Omega
$$

### Source correction

#### English

The official chart reads about $14.5\,\Omega$ and $170\,\Omega$ and writes “$0.26\times50=14.5$”, an inconsistent multiplication. Here the values are computed exactly from the given load.

#### 中文

官方图读数约 $14.5\,\Omega$ 与 $170\,\Omega$，且把“$0.26\times50$”写成 14.5；该乘法本身不一致。本页由原负载精确计算，图上近似读数作为参考。

## Part c

### Question

#### English

Choose one of the two values of $Z_{in,d}$ from part b). How long $d$ (in meters) must the transmission line be to present your chosen input impedance $Z_{in,d}$? Show your work on the Smith chart.

#### 中文

从第 b) 问的两个 $Z_{in,d}$ 中任选一个。传输线长度 $d$ 应为多少米，才能呈现所选的输入阻抗 $Z_{in,d}$？在 Smith 图上展示过程。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with wavelength and reflection propagation. Moving $d$ toward the source rotates $\Gamma$ clockwise by $4\pi d/\lambda$.

#### 中文

先写波长与反射系数沿线传播关系；向源移动 $d$ 使 $\Gamma$ 顺时针旋转 $4\pi d/\lambda$。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac uf,\qquad \Gamma_{\mathrm{in},d}=\Gamma_Le^{-2j\beta d},\quad\beta=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}
$$

#### References

- [wave](formula:wave)
- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Substitute velocity factor and frequency for the wavelength needed to convert electrical length to meters.

#### 中文

代入速度因子和频率，求出将电长度换成米所需的波长。

#### Equation

$$
\lambda=\frac{0.85(3\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s})}{100\times10^6\,\mathrm{Hz}}=2.55\,\mathrm m
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Find the initial phase from $\Gamma_L=(9-2j)/17$, in radians.

#### 中文

由 $\Gamma_L=(9-2j)/17$ 求初始相位，使用弧度制。

#### Equation

$$
\theta_L=\arg(9-2j)=-\arctan(2/9)\approx-0.218669\,\mathrm{rad}
$$

### Step 4

#### English

For the smaller real resistance target $\Gamma=-\rho$, with phase $-\pi$. Write the rotation equation and solve for $d$.

#### 中文

若选较小实电阻，目标是 $\Gamma=-\rho$，即相位 $-\pi$。列出旋转角方程并解 $d$。

#### Equation

$$
\theta_L-\frac{4\pi d_1}{\lambda}=-\pi\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\frac{d_1}{\lambda}=\frac{\pi+\theta_L}{4\pi}=0.232598878
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Multiply the first electrical length by $\lambda$. Reaching the other real-axis intersection requires another half-turn, adding $\lambda/4$.

#### 中文

将第一分支电长度乘波长；另一实轴交点比它多转半圈，对应再加 $\lambda/4$。

#### Equation

$$
d_1=(0.232598878)(2.55)=0.593127\,\mathrm m,\qquad \frac{d_2}{\lambda}=0.232598878+\frac14=0.482598878
$$

### Step 6

#### English

Convert the second branch to meters as well. Either works, but the subsequent transformer impedance must use the matching branch.

#### 中文

第二分支也换成米。两个方案均可，后续变换线阻抗必须与所选分支配套。

#### Equation

$$
d_2=(0.482598878)(2.55)=1.230627\,\mathrm m
$$

### Step 7

#### English

State the resistance–length pairing for both branches.

#### 中文

列出两组电阻与长度的对应关系。

#### Equation

$$
\begin{gathered}R_{\min}:\ d=0.593127\,\mathrm m\\R_{\max}:\ d=1.230627\,\mathrm m\end{gathered}
$$

### Answer

$$
\begin{gathered}R_{\min}:\ d=0.593127\,\mathrm m\\R_{\max}:\ d=1.230627\,\mathrm m\end{gathered}
$$

## Part d

### Question

#### English

For the case you chose in part c), find the characteristic impedance $Z_{0T}$ of the quarter-wave transformer that matches the load network to the $Z_0=50\Omega$ feedline.

#### 中文

对第 c) 问选取的情况，求将负载网络匹配到 $Z_0=50\Omega$ 馈线所需的四分之一波长变换器特性阻抗 $Z_{0T}$。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with the crib quarter-wave equation; the transformer load is the real input resistance presented by the preceding line.

#### 中文

先写原表四分之一波长变换公式；变换线的负载是前段线路呈现的纯电阻。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in},T}=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_{\mathrm{in},d}}
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 2

#### English

Require a $50\,\Omega$ input to the feedline and rearrange to obtain the square-root formula.

#### 中文

要求变换线向馈线呈现 $50\,\Omega$，先移项得到平方根公式。

#### Equation

$$
50\,\Omega=\frac{Z_{0T}^2}{R_{\mathrm{in},d}}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Z_{0T}=\sqrt{(50\,\Omega)R_{\mathrm{in},d}}
$$

### Step 3

#### English

For the smaller-resistance branch in c), insert $14.8371295\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

若 c) 选择较小电阻分支，代入 $14.8371295\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T,1}=\sqrt{50(14.8371295)}\,\Omega=\sqrt{741.856476}\,\Omega\approx27.2370\,\Omega
$$

### Step 4

#### English

For the larger-resistance branch, use its corresponding $168.4962038\,\Omega$.

#### 中文

若选择较大电阻分支，必须相应改用 $168.4962038\,\Omega$。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T,2}=\sqrt{50(168.4962038)}\,\Omega=\sqrt{8424.81019}\,\Omega\approx91.7868\,\Omega
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Either branch works. Dividing $Z_{0T}^2$ by its corresponding resistance returns $50\,\Omega$ and verifies the feedline match.

#### 中文

任选一组即可；将 $Z_{0T}^2$ 除以对应电阻会恢复 $50\,\Omega$，验证馈线匹配。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{0T}=27.2370\,\Omega\ \text{or}\ 91.7868\,\Omega
$$

### Answer

$$
Z_{0T}=27.2370\,\Omega\ \text{or}\ 91.7868\,\Omega
$$

## Part e

### Question

#### English

Once the transmission line and load are matched to the feedline, maximum power will be transferred from the generator to the load in steady state. Where do reflections still occur in the system?
Circle all that apply.

a. At the interface between the feedline and the input to the $L=\lambda/4$ line.
b. At the interface between the $L=\lambda/4$ line and the input to the line of length $d$.
c. At the interface between the line of length $d$ and the load $Z_L$.
d. No reflections occur anywhere in the system since the feedline is matched to the load.

#### 中文

传输线和负载与馈线匹配后，发生器会在稳态下向负载传输最大功率。系统中哪些位置仍会发生反射？圈出所有适用项。

a. 馈线与 $L=\lambda/4$ 传输线输入端的界面。
b. $L=\lambda/4$ 传输线与长度为 $d$ 的传输线输入端的界面。
c. 长度为 $d$ 的传输线与负载 $Z_L$ 的界面。
d. 由于馈线已与负载匹配，系统任何位置都不会发生反射。

### Step 1

#### English

Begin with local interface reflection. Compare traveling-wave characteristic impedances on the two sides; this differs from the net input reflection of the whole network.

#### 中文

先写局部界面反射公式。这里比较界面两侧的行波特性阻抗；它与整个网络的净输入反射不是同一量。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_{\mathrm{local}}=\frac{Z_{\mathrm{right}}-Z_{\mathrm{left}}}{Z_{\mathrm{right}}+Z_{\mathrm{left}}}
$$

#### References

- [reflection](formula:reflection)

### Step 2

#### English

Option a: the $50\,\Omega$ feedline meets the $Z_{0T}$ transformer. The different characteristic impedances give a local reflection.

#### 中文

选项 a：$50\,\Omega$ 馈线接到 $Z_{0T}$ 的变换线，两侧特性阻抗不同，因此存在局部反射。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_a^{\mathrm{local}}=\frac{Z_{0T}-50}{Z_{0T}+50}\ne0
$$

### Step 3

#### English

Option b: the transformer meets the $50\,\Omega$ main line, again with a nonzero impedance step.

#### 中文

选项 b：变换线接回 $50\,\Omega$ 主线，阻抗差也非零。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_b^{\mathrm{local}}=\frac{50-Z_{0T}}{50+Z_{0T}}\ne0
$$

### Step 4

#### English

Option c: the main line meets the complex load $150-j50\,\Omega$ and still has load reflection.

#### 中文

选项 c：主线接到 $150-j50\,\Omega$ 的复负载，也仍然存在负载反射。

#### Equation

$$
\Gamma_c=\frac{(150-j50)-50}{(150-j50)+50}=\frac{9-2j}{17}\ne0
$$

### Step 5

#### English

Overall matching means the multiple reflected phasors returning into the feedline cancel, giving zero net input reflection; it does not remove the local reflection at $a$.

#### 中文

整体匹配表示多次反射返回馈线的相量恰好抵消，使净输入反射为零；不能据此删掉 a 的局部反射。

#### Equation

$$
Z_{\mathrm{in,network}}=50\,\Omega,\qquad\Gamma_{\mathrm{feed,net}}=\frac{50-50}{50+50}=0
$$

#### References

- [quarter](formula:quarter)

### Step 6

#### English

Thus, for the official question about interfaces with reflections, select a, b and c; option d is false.

#### 中文

所以按标准答案的“哪些界面有反射”问法，选 a、b、c，选项 d 不成立。

#### Equation

$$
\text{Interfaces (a), (b) and (c)}
$$

### Answer

$$
\text{Interfaces (a), (b) and (c)}
$$

### Answer text

#### English

Select a, b and c. Each impedance discontinuity has a local reflection; overall matching cancels the returning reflected phasors at the feedline, giving zero net input reflection.

#### 中文

选 a、b、c。三个阻抗突变处都有局部反射；整体匹配使这些返回馈线的反射相量抵消，净输入反射为零。
