# Study workspace

This page applies the generated Swiss Modernism direction to an engineering study tool.

- Product name: Fieldnotes / 场与波习题室. An academic reading workspace with three columns, not the generator's portfolio page pattern.
- Warm paper background, white surfaces, ink navy text, restrained burnt orange primary action, teal formula references. Define all colors as semantic tokens in the existing global CSS.
- Latin headings: Georgia or a locally available serif. Interface and bilingual body: self-hosted Inter plus system Chinese sans. KaTeX owns mathematical typography. No external font requests.
- Desktop: 250 px navigation, readable central document, 300 px source rail. Below 1180 px move the rail into a Sheet; below 800 px use a navigation Sheet. Keep a full document flow and visible focus.
- Both desktop sidebars can collapse independently from persistent top-bar panel buttons, following the shadcn block trigger pattern. Keep the reader centered at a comfortable maximum width, preserve panel content state and save preferences locally. Collapsed panels are inert; on compact screens these controls open the existing Sheets. Ctrl/Cmd+B toggles exam navigation.
- Native shadcn-svelte primitives: tabs, toggle groups, accordion, tooltip, dialog, sheet, popover, field, progress, buttons, inputs. Preserve their native semantics, keyboard behavior, tokens and variants.
- Illustrations: reproducible Matplotlib SVG with SVG text preserved, plus an original figure switch. Technical labels use standard symbols in both languages; omit boilerplate recreation captions. Reserve a lower-left area for the source-exam action in both views, and preserve drawing height when switching between them.
- Before derivations, introduce the concepts, actual givens, goals and starting approach in the selected language, with links to the relevant lecture PDF pages. Keep the introduction separate from the original exam wording.
- Formulas: comfortable display spacing, horizontal scrolling confined to a formula, units upright. Keep the terms of one sum or difference together when they fit at normal reading size; reserve aligned continuation rows for meaningful calculation stages and simplification. Place KaTeX boxed results inside the final derivation step, without an answer card or extra border. Avoid shrinking entire pages to fit mobile.
- Keep annotations brief while showing evaluated integrals one variable at a time: actual field/density and differential element, dot product, constant extraction, antiderivative, upper-minus-lower substitution (including negative lower limits) and simplification. Explain unfamiliar area/volume elements briefly at their first use. Split long derivations at meaningful stages; a beginner must not have to supply skipped integral evaluations. Reuse an earlier evaluated result with its origin identified, and respect drawing, setup-only and conceptual tasks.
- Solutions read like worked exam examples: crib sheet formulas → givens and goal → actual substitution → result. Place short bilingual lead-ins before their equations: “使用 … 公式”“已知 …，求 …”“把 … 代入 …”“得”. Identify question-supplied definitions and earlier derived results accurately. Use `\because` and `\therefore` where helpful. Group routine algebra in aligned equations; omit numbered-step rails and repetitive narration. Retain governing equations, substitutions, meaningful integration bounds, boundary conditions, units, directions and source corrections. Make the formula names in the lead-in prose inline source links, inheriting the paragraph’s font and line height with a subtle underline. Use the native Hover Card for preview and preserve PDF navigation. Do not repeat icon labels beside equations.
- Use one solution view without study/practice modes. Each part opens its full solution at once, including all calculations, the final answer, solution figure and source note. All parts start collapsed when opening a problem. The global toggle collapses all if any part is open, otherwise reveals all. Preserve retired mode preferences and legacy saved step counts without applying them to the UI. Completion, bookmarks, locale and theme persist locally; source PDFs stay local. Practice is done on paper; omit note-taking and per-part text entry.
- Titles and question wording follow the global locale. Chinese is a faithful translation of the original exam; English retains the original wording. Use a single question block with no separate translation switch or supplemental panel.
- Author papers separately in `content/en/` and `content/zh-cn/`, with single-language Markdown and complete independent paper assets in each. Global language switching selects that language's SVGs, original crops, PDF pages and downloads while retaining the same problem URL, theme and progress.
- Guides and solutions are natural Markdown. Pair translated questions and parts by stable ID; allow each language its own paragraphs and formula groups. Render the authored flow directly, with one final boxed calculation and no numbered step wrappers or duplicate answer model.
- Source links show a cropped original excerpt on hover/focus and open a page viewer with a percentage-based bounding rectangle. Page numbers always mean 1-based PDF pages.
- All drawings that are schematic or use illustrative parameter values must say so in the caption. Recreated source geometries preserve the source's symbolic dimensions.
- Source viewer: use native 36 px pagination buttons, page input and zoom buttons, with matching outline variants. At sufficient width and height, show a vertical, independently scrolling list of all page thumbnails. Highlight and reveal the selected page when navigation changes it; clicks change pages, while the current thumbnail also supports pointer/keyboard positioning within the main page. Keep the original citation and current viewport markers. Hide this overview on compact screens.
- Motion is subtle and respects reduced motion; no visual reliance on color alone.

- Keep question and solution diagrams as static SVG. Construct perspective solids in 3D and project their faces, dimensions and coordinate axes through one orthographic camera. Preserve the original figure switch and Gaussian-surface solution drawings; omit interactive 3D viewers and their controls.
- Scientific diagram labels are large and bold, including math symbols; position them clear of arrows. Navigate questions through the sidebar only; omit a bottom previous/next pager.
